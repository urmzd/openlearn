# S-M10b q18

The KL-regularized objective $\max_\pi \mathbb{E}_{y \sim \pi}[r(x, y)] - \beta\,\mathrm{KL}(\pi \Vert \pi_{\mathrm{ref}})$ has the optimum $\pi^*(y \mid x) = \pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y \mid x)\exp(r(x, y)/\beta)/Z(x)$, so $\pi^*(y \mid x)$ is proportional to $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y \mid x)\exp(r(x, y)/\beta)$ and $\log(\pi^*/\pi_{\mathrm{ref}})(y \mid x) = r(x, y)/\beta - \log Z(x)$. Taking the log odds between the chosen completion $y_w$ and the rejected one $y_l$, the normalizer $\log Z(x)$ is shared by both (it depends on $x$ only) and cancels, leaving $\log\frac{\pi^*(y_w \mid x)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y_w \mid x)} - \log\frac{\pi^*(y_l \mid x)}{\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}(y_l \mid x)} = \bigl(r(x, y_w) - r(x, y_l)\bigr)/\beta$, the reward difference divided by $\beta$.

# Tajik and Persian

The Tajik and Persian guide, script first. Tajik and Persian are one language in two scripts: Cyrillic in Tajikistan, the Perso-Arabic script in Iran and Afghanistan. This path teaches both.

**For**: A heritage speaker who speaks Tajik and wants to read and write it, in Cyrillic first and then in Persian script.

A learner who starts from zero follows the chapters in number order instead, and returns to this path once the core phrases are familiar.

The stages, their chapters, and what "done" means for each are in [`path.tsv`](path.tsv). Stages 1, 3 and 4 share the writing chapter and take the most sessions. From stage 7 on, the scenario chapters are reading and writing practice: you already know what the phrases mean, so read them from the script and cover the transliteration.

## A 30-minute session

One stage takes several sessions. Run each session the same way:

| Minutes | Do |
|---------|----|
| 5 | Review. Cover the target column of yesterday's tables and say each phrase from the English. |
| 15 | New material. Read one section aloud, then build three new sentences from its pattern templates. |
| 10 | Produce. Do the chapter's Practice table in writing, from memory, then check it. |

Move to the next stage when you meet its "done when" line without looking.

# Japanese

The Japanese guide in reading order, from the phrases that carry most conversations to the scenarios you will actually be in.

**For**: A traveler who wants to hold short, real exchanges in Japanese.

The stages, their chapters, and what "done" means for each are in [`path.tsv`](path.tsv). Each stage is finished when you can produce the language from memory, which a written session graded against the chapter will tell you.

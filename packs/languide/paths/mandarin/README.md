# Mandarin

The Mandarin guide in reading order: phrases and pinyin first, then the tones, then the characters you will meet most.

**For**: A learner who starts from zero and wants casual conversation and enough Mandarin for work.

Pronunciation comes before most phrases matter in Mandarin. If the tones are new to you, do stage 3 straight after stage 1.

The stages, their chapters, and what "done" means for each are in [`path.tsv`](path.tsv). Each stage is finished when you can produce the language from memory, which a written session graded against the chapter will tell you.

## A 30-minute session

One stage takes several sessions. Run each session the same way:

| Minutes | Do |
|---------|----|
| 5 | Review. Cover the target column of yesterday's tables and say each phrase from the English. |
| 15 | New material. Read one section aloud, then build three new sentences from its pattern templates. |
| 10 | Produce. Do the chapter's Practice table in writing, from memory, then check it. |

Move to the next stage when you meet its "done when" line without looking.

# Transportation

This chapter covers tickets, trains, the metro, buses, taxis, airports and driving. It also covers telling the time and the days of the week, because every transport conversation needs them.

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

| Pattern | French | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|--------|---------------|---------|
| A ticket to [place], please | Un billet pour [lieu], s'il vous plaît | ehn bee-yeh poor [place] seel voo pleh | Un billet pour Lyon, s'il vous plaît = A ticket to Lyon, please |
| What time does the [transport] to [place] leave? | À quelle heure part le [transport] pour [lieu]? | ah kel uhr par luh [transport] poor [place] | À quelle heure part le train pour Québec? = What time does the train to Quebec City leave? |
| Does this [transport] go to [place]? | Est-ce que ce [transport] va à [lieu]? | ess kuh suh [transport] vah ah [place] | Est-ce que ce bus va à l'aéroport? = Does this bus go to the airport? |
| Which [line / bus] goes to [place]? | Quelle ligne va à [lieu]? | kel leeny vah ah [place] | Quelle ligne va à la gare? = Which line goes to the station? |
| To [place], please | À [lieu], s'il vous plaît | ah [place] seel voo pleh | À l'aéroport, s'il vous plaît = To the airport, please |
| How long does it take to get to [place]? | Ça prend combien de temps pour aller à [lieu]? | sah prahn kohn-byehn duh tahn poor ah-lay ah [place] | Ça prend combien de temps pour aller au centre? = How long does it take to get to the centre? |
| I would like to [action] my ticket | Je voudrais [infinitif] mon billet | zhuh voo-dreh [action] mohn bee-yeh | Je voudrais changer mon billet = I would like to change my ticket |
| I come to work by [transport] | Je viens au travail en [transport] | zhuh vyehn oh trah-vye ahn [transport] | Je viens au travail en métro = I come to work by metro |
| The [transport] is [state] | Le [transport] est [état] | luh [transport] eh [state] | Le train est en retard = The train is late |
| At [time] | À [heure] | ah [time] | À huit heures et demie = At half past eight |

## Telling the Time

Timetables in France and Quebec use the 24-hour clock. In conversation, France uses both systems. Quebec mostly uses the 12-hour clock.

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| What time is it? | Quelle heure est-il? | kel uhr eh-teel | Polite |
| It is one o'clock | Il est une heure | eel eh ewn uhr | "une", feminine |
| It is eight o'clock | Il est huit heures | eel eh wee-tuhr | Liaison |
| It is half past eight | Il est huit heures et demie | eel eh wee-tuhr ay duh-mee | 8:30 |
| It is quarter past eight | Il est huit heures et quart | eel eh wee-tuhr ay kar | 8:15 |
| It is quarter to nine | Il est neuf heures moins le quart | eel eh nuh-vuhr mwehn luh kar | 8:45 |
| It is ten past three | Il est trois heures dix | eel eh trwah-zuhr deess | 3:10 |

| 2:30 PM | Quatorze heures trente | kah-tor-zuhr trahnt | 24-hour clock: no "et demie" |
| In the morning | Du matin | dew mah-tehn | huit heures du matin |

### Days of the Week

Days take no capital letter. "Lundi" means "on Monday". "Le lundi" means "every Monday".

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Monday | lundi | lehn-dee | The week starts on Monday |
| Tuesday | mardi | mar-dee | Quebec: mar-dzee |
| Wednesday | mercredi | mehr-kruh-dee | Weekday |
| Thursday | jeudi | zhuh-dee | Weekday |
| Friday | vendredi | vahn-druh-dee | Weekday |
| Saturday | samedi | sam-dee | The middle "e" is silent |
| Sunday | dimanche | dee-mahnsh | France: many shops are closed |
| The weekend | le week-end | luh wee-kend | France. Quebec: "la fin de semaine" |

## France and Quebec: Transport Vocabulary

| English | France | Quebec | Notes |
|---------|--------|--------|-------|
| Car | la voiture | l'auto, le char | "Char" is casual |
| Bus | le bus | l'autobus | "Le bus" is also understood in Quebec |
| To park | se garer | stationner, se stationner | Both regions understand both |
| Car park | le parking | le stationnement | Signs in Quebec say "P" or "Stationnement" |
| Petrol | l'essence | l'essence, le gaz | "Gaz" is casual |
| Ferry | le ferry | le traversier | Quebec avoids the English loanword |
| Weekend | le week-end | la fin de semaine | Quebec: "week-end" is rare |
| Metro or bus ticket | le ticket | le billet, le titre de transport | France: "billet" is for trains and planes |
| Traffic | la circulation | la circulation, le trafic | Both regions understand both |
| Mobile phone | le portable | le cellulaire | For tickets on your phone |
| Now | maintenant | maintenant, présentement | Announcements in Quebec use "présentement" |

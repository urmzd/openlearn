# Work and Business

This chapter gives you the French you need at work: introducing yourself and your job, taking part in meetings, writing email and chat messages, handling calls, and using numbers, dates and money. It closes with the register rules that separate office French from casual French.

Workplace French differs between France and Quebec more than everyday French does. Quebec uses French terms where France often uses English ones. The Notes column marks each case.

---

## The Formal Register at Work

Follow these rules in writing, with clients, and with anyone senior you do not know well.

| Rule | What to do | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| Use vous | Say "vous" to clients, suppliers, senior people and anyone new. In Quebec, colleagues move to "tu" at once. | Pourriez-vous m'envoyer le document? |
| Use the conditional for requests | Replace "je veux" and "pouvez-vous" with "je voudrais", "j'aimerais" and "pourriez-vous". | Je voudrais fixer une réunion. |
| Keep "ne" | Write and say the full negative. | Je ne suis pas disponible. Not: Je suis pas disponible. |
| Ask full questions | Use "est-ce que" or inversion, not intonation alone. | Avez-vous reçu ma facture? |
| Use "nous" in writing | Say "on" in speech and write "nous" in email and reports. | Nous vous remercions de votre confiance. |
| Use titles correctly | Write "Madame" or "Monsieur" alone, or with the family name. Never with the first name alone. Never "Mademoiselle". | Bonjour Madame Roy, |
| Open and close | Start with "Bonjour" and end with a closing formula, even in a two-line email. | Bonjour, ... Cordialement, |
| Avoid slang and short forms | No "ouais", "truc", "svp" or "cdlt" in external messages. | Write: s'il vous plaît, Cordialement |
| Soften disagreement | Put a softener before your objection. | Je ne suis pas tout à fait d'accord. |
| Use feminine job titles | Give a woman her feminine title. Quebec does this in every case. France now does it in most cases. | la directrice, la développeuse, l'ingénieure |

## Pattern Phrase Templates

| Pattern | French | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|--------|---------------|---------|
| I work as [job] at [company] | Je travaille comme [poste] chez [entreprise] | zhuh trah-vye kom [job] shay [company] | Je travaille comme analyste chez Hydro-Québec = I work as an analyst at Hydro-Québec |
| I am in charge of [thing] | Je suis responsable de [chose] | zhuh swee res-pohn-sahbl duh [thing] | Je suis responsable du budget = I am in charge of the budget |
| I am working on [thing] | Je travaille sur [chose] | zhuh trah-vye sewr [thing] | Je travaille sur un nouveau projet = I am working on a new project |
| Are you available [time]? | Êtes-vous disponible [moment]? | et voo dees-poh-neebl [time] | Êtes-vous disponible jeudi matin? = Are you available on Thursday morning? |
| Could you send me [thing]? | Pourriez-vous m'envoyer [chose]? | poo-ryay voo mahn-vwah-yay [thing] | Pourriez-vous m'envoyer le contrat? = Could you send me the contract? |
| I am writing to you about [topic] | Je vous écris au sujet de [sujet] | zhuh voo-zay-kree oh sew-zheh duh [topic] | Je vous écris au sujet de la facture = I am writing to you about the invoice |
| Please find attached [thing] | Veuillez trouver ci-joint [chose] | vuh-yay troo-vay see-zhwehn [thing] | Veuillez trouver ci-joint le rapport = Please find the report attached |
| I am going to [action] by [deadline] | Je vais [infinitif] d'ici [échéance] | zhuh veh [action] dee-see [deadline] | Je vais finir le rapport d'ici vendredi = I am going to finish the report by Friday |
| We have to [action] | Nous devons [infinitif] | noo duh-vohn [action] | Nous devons respecter le budget = We have to keep to the budget |
| What do you think of [thing]? | Que pensez-vous de [chose]? | kuh pahn-say voo duh [thing] | Que pensez-vous de cette proposition? = What do you think of this proposal? |
| Can we postpone [thing] to [time]? | Peut-on reporter [chose] à [moment]? | puh-tohn ruh-por-tay [thing] ah [time] | Peut-on reporter la réunion à lundi? = Can we postpone the meeting to Monday? |
| Thank you for [thing] | Merci pour [chose] | mehr-see poor [thing] | Merci pour votre réponse = Thank you for your reply |
| I'll get back to you about [topic] | Je vous reviens au sujet de [sujet] | zhuh voo ruh-vyehn oh sew-zheh duh [topic] | Je vous reviens au sujet du budget = I'll get back to you about the budget. Quebec form. France: Je reviens vers vous |

### A Model Email

> **Objet :** Réunion de suivi du projet, jeudi 3 décembre
>
> Bonjour Madame Roy,
>
> Je vous écris pour confirmer notre réunion du jeudi 3 décembre à 14 h. Vous trouverez l'ordre du jour en pièce jointe.
>
> Pourriez-vous me dire si cet horaire vous convient toujours?
>
> Je reste à votre disposition pour toute question.
>
> Cordialement,
>
> Alex Morgan, chef de projet

| Part | French | English | Notes |
|------|--------|---------|-------|
| Subject line | Objet : Réunion de suivi du projet, jeudi 3 décembre | Subject: Project follow-up meeting, Thursday 3 December | A noun phrase. A space before the colon |
| Opener | Bonjour Madame Roy, | Hello Ms Roy, | Title and family name |
| Purpose | Je vous écris pour confirmer notre réunion du jeudi 3 décembre à 14 h. | I am writing to confirm our meeting on Thursday 3 December at 2 PM. | 24-hour clock in writing |
| Attachment | Vous trouverez l'ordre du jour en pièce jointe. | You will find the agenda attached. | Future tense of "trouver" |
| Request | Pourriez-vous me dire si cet horaire vous convient toujours? | Could you tell me if this time still suits you? | Conditional for a polite request |
| Offer | Je reste à votre disposition pour toute question. | I remain at your disposal for any question. | Standard line before the closing |
| Closer | Cordialement, | Regards, | Comma, then your name on the next line |

# Restaurants and Dining

This chapter follows a meal from the door to the bill, then covers dietary needs, cafés, bakeries and meals with colleagues. Meal names differ between France and Quebec, so read the meal names table before you accept an invitation.

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

| Pattern | French | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|--------|---------------|---------|
| A table for [number], please | Une table pour [nombre], s'il vous plaît | ewn tahbl poor [number] seel voo pleh | Une table pour deux, s'il vous plaît = A table for two, please |
| I'll have [dish] | Je vais prendre [plat] | zhuh veh prahndr [dish] | Je vais prendre le poisson = I'll have the fish |
| For me, [dish] | Pour moi, [plat] | poor mwah [dish] | Pour moi, la soupe = For me, the soup |
| As a [course], [dish] | Comme [service], [plat] | kom [course] [dish] | Comme dessert, la tarte = For dessert, the tart |
| What is [dish]? | Qu'est-ce que c'est, [plat]? | kess kuh seh [dish] | Qu'est-ce que c'est, la poutine? = What is poutine? |
| Does it contain [ingredient]? | Est-ce que ça contient [ingrédient]? | ess kuh sah kohn-tyehn [ingredient] | Est-ce que ça contient des noix? = Does it contain nuts? |
| I don't eat [food] | Je ne mange pas de [aliment] | zhuh nuh mahnzh pah duh [food] | Je ne mange pas de porc = I don't eat pork |
| I am allergic to [food] | Je suis allergique à [aliment] | zhuh swee ah-lehr-zheek ah [food] | Je suis allergique au lait = I am allergic to milk |
| Without [ingredient], please | Sans [ingrédient], s'il vous plaît | sahn [ingredient] seel voo pleh | Sans fromage, s'il vous plaît = Without cheese, please |
| Could I have [thing]? | Est-ce que je pourrais avoir [chose]? | ess kuh zhuh poo-reh ah-vwahr [thing] | Est-ce que je pourrais avoir du pain? = Could I have some bread? |
| More [thing], please | Encore [chose], s'il vous plaît | ahn-kor [thing] seel voo pleh | Encore de l'eau, s'il vous plaît = More water, please |

## Meal Names

The same words mean different meals in France and Quebec. Belgium and Switzerland follow the Quebec pattern.

| Meal | France | Quebec | Notes |
|------|--------|--------|-------|
| Breakfast | le petit déjeuner | le déjeuner | puh-tee day-zhuh-nay / day-zhuh-nay |
| Lunch | le déjeuner | le dîner | day-zhuh-nay / dee-nay |
| Dinner | le dîner | le souper | dee-nay / soo-pay |
| Afternoon snack | le goûter | la collation | goo-tay / koh-lah-syohn |
| Brunch | le brunch | le brunch | bruhnsh |
| Drinks before dinner | l'apéritif, l'apéro | l'apéro, le 5 à 7 | The "5 à 7" is after-work drinks in Quebec |

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Shall we have lunch together? | On déjeune ensemble? | ohn day-zhuhn ahn-sahnbl | France |
| Shall we have lunch together? | On dîne ensemble? | ohn deen ahn-sahnbl | Quebec |
| Are you free for lunch on Thursday? | Vous êtes libre pour déjeuner jeudi? | voo-zet leebr poor day-zhuh-nay zhuh-dee | France. Quebec: "pour dîner" |
| What time do you have dinner? | Vous dînez à quelle heure? | voo dee-nay ah kel uhr | France. Quebec: "Vous soupez à quelle heure?" |
| Have you eaten? | Vous avez mangé? | voo-zah-vay mahn-zhay | Works in both regions |
| Cheers! | Santé! | sahn-tay | France and Quebec |
| To your health | À la vôtre | ah lah vohtr | Polite. Casual: "À la tienne" |

When someone from Quebec invites you to "dîner", it is at noon. When someone from France does, it is in the evening. If you are not sure, ask "À quelle heure?".

## Tipping and the Bill

| Region | What is included | What to do |
|--------|------------------|------------|
| France | Service is included in the menu price by law. Taxes are included. | Pay the total. Leave small change or one or two euros for good service. |
| Quebec | Service is not included. Taxes are added to the listed price. | Add a tip of 15 to 20 percent of the amount before taxes. The card machine asks you to choose. |

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Is the tip included? | Le pourboire est inclus? | luh poor-bwahr eh ehn-klew | Quebec: for large groups it sometimes is |
| Are taxes included? | Les taxes sont incluses? | lay tax sohn ehn-klewz | Quebec |
| Fifteen percent | Quinze pour cent | kehnz poor sahn | On the card machine |
| One bill or two? | Une facture ou deux? | ewn fak-tewr oo duh | Quebec: staff ask this |

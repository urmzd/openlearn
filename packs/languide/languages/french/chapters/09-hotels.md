# Hotels and Accommodation

This chapter covers booking, check-in, requests, problems and check-out. The last section covers longer stays and apartment rentals, for learners who move to a French-speaking city for work or study.

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

| Pattern | French | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|--------|---------------|---------|
| I would like to book [room type] | Je voudrais réserver [type de chambre] | zhuh voo-dreh ray-zehr-vay [room type] | Je voudrais réserver une chambre double = I would like to book a double room |
| For [number] nights | Pour [nombre] nuits | poor [number] nwee | Pour trois nuits = For three nights |
| From [date] to [date] | Du [date] au [date] | dew [date] oh [date] | Du 3 au 6 décembre = From 3 to 6 December |
| I have a booking in the name of [name] | J'ai une réservation au nom de [nom] | zhay ewn ray-zehr-vah-syohn oh nohn duh [name] | J'ai une réservation au nom de Morgan = I have a booking in the name of Morgan |
| Is there [facility]? | Est-ce qu'il y a [service]? | ess keel yah [facility] | Est-ce qu'il y a une piscine? = Is there a pool? |
| Could you bring me [thing]? | Pourriez-vous m'apporter [chose]? | poo-ryay voo mah-por-tay [thing] | Pourriez-vous m'apporter des serviettes? = Could you bring me some towels? |
| The [thing] doesn't work | [Chose] ne fonctionne pas | [thing] nuh fohnk-syon pah | Le chauffage ne fonctionne pas = The heating doesn't work |
| There is no [thing] | Il n'y a pas de [chose] | eel nyah pah duh [thing] | Il n'y a pas d'eau chaude = There is no hot water |
| What time is [service]? | À quelle heure est [service]? | ah kel uhr eh [service] | À quelle heure est le petit déjeuner? = What time is breakfast? |
| Is [thing] included? | Est-ce que [chose] est inclus? | ess kuh [thing] eh ehn-klew | Est-ce que le wifi est inclus? = Is wifi included? |

# Core Communication Phrases

This chapter gives the essential phrases for everyday interactions in French, organized by communication scenario. Each section includes **pattern phrases** (templates with slots you fill in) and **fixed phrases** (ready-to-use expressions).

Two rules apply to everything in this chapter:

- **Say "Bonjour" first.** In France and in Quebec, a request with no greeting sounds rude. Open every interaction with "Bonjour" (or "Bonsoir" in the evening).
- **Use "vous" by default.** Use "tu" with friends, family, children and people who use "tu" with you. Chapter 10 explains the differences between France and Quebec.

## Pattern Phrase Templates

These reusable patterns let you express many ideas. Swap the words in the slots marked with `[brackets]`.

### Requesting Something

| Pattern | French | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|--------|---------------|---------|
| [thing], please | [chose], s'il vous plaît | [thing] seel voo pleh | Un café, s'il vous plaît = A coffee, please |
| I would like [thing] | Je voudrais [chose] | zhuh voo-dreh [thing] | Je voudrais un billet = I would like a ticket |
| I would like to [action] | J'aimerais [infinitif] | zhem-reh [action] | J'aimerais réserver une table = I would like to book a table |
| Could you [action]? | Pourriez-vous [infinitif]? | poo-ryay voo [action] | Pourriez-vous répéter? = Could you repeat? |
| Can you [action]? | Est-ce que vous pouvez [infinitif]? | ess kuh voo poo-vay [action] | Est-ce que vous pouvez m'aider? = Can you help me? |
| I need [thing] | J'ai besoin de [chose] | zhay buh-zwehn duh [thing] | J'ai besoin d'un médecin = I need a doctor |
| I am looking for [thing] | Je cherche [chose] | zhuh shehrsh [thing] | Je cherche la gare = I am looking for the station |

### Politeness Tiers for One Request

## Practice

Cover the Answer column. Say each answer aloud, then check.

| Prompt | Answer |
|--------|--------|
| Greet a shop assistant in the morning and ask if they can help you. | Bonjour. Pouvez-vous m'aider? |
| Say "I would like a coffee, please." | Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît. |
| Ask politely where the toilets are. | Excusez-moi, où sont les toilettes? |
| Say that you did not understand and ask the person to repeat. | Je n'ai pas compris. Pouvez-vous répéter, s'il vous plaît? |
| Someone thanks you. Answer in the France way and in the Quebec way. | De rien. / Bienvenue. |
| Introduce yourself and say you are learning French. | Je m'appelle [prénom]. J'apprends le français. |
| Ask a friend casually how it is going. | Salut, ça va? |
| Tell someone you have lost your wallet. | J'ai perdu mon portefeuille. |
| Ask "Can I pay by card?" | Est-ce que je peux payer par carte? |
| Wish a colleague a good weekend in France and in Quebec. | Bon week-end! / Bonne fin de semaine! |

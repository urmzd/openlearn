# Shopping

This chapter covers shops, clothes, prices, payment, returns, groceries, the pharmacy and online orders. In small shops in France, say "Bonjour" when you walk in and "Au revoir" when you leave, even if you buy nothing.

## Practice

Cover the Answer column. Say each answer aloud, then check.

| Prompt | Answer |
|--------|--------|
| A shop assistant asks "Je peux vous aider?". Say you are just looking. | Je regarde seulement, merci. |
| Ask if they have this in black. | Avez-vous ça en noir? |
| Ask to try something on and ask where the fitting rooms are. | Je peux essayer? Où sont les cabines d'essayage? |
| Say "It is too small. Do you have the next size up?" | C'est trop petit. Avez-vous la taille au-dessus? |
| Ask how much it costs and say it is too expensive. | C'est combien? C'est trop cher. |

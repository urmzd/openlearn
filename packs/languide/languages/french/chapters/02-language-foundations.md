# Language Foundations: Quick Reference

This chapter gives the grammar you need to build your own sentences. Each section is short. Read the rule, say the examples aloud, then move on. Returning learners can go straight to the Practice table at the end to find the gaps.

---

## Sentence Structure

French follows **Subject-Verb-Object** order, like English. Unlike Spanish or Italian, French always needs a subject pronoun.

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| I speak French | Je parle français | zhuh parl frahn-seh | Subject pronoun is required |
| She works here | Elle travaille ici | el trah-vye ee-see | Subject, verb, place |
| We have a meeting | Nous avons une réunion | noo-zah-vohn ewn ray-ew-nyohn | Liaison between "nous" and "avons" |
| The shop closes at six | Le magasin ferme à six heures | luh mah-gah-zehn fehrm ah see-zuhr | Noun subject |
| I see him | Je le vois | zhuh luh vwah | Object pronouns go before the verb |

### Subject Pronouns

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| I | je | zhuh | Becomes "j'" before a vowel: j'ai |
| you (casual, one person) | tu | tew | Friends, family, children |
| he / it | il | eel | Masculine nouns |
| she / it | elle | el | Feminine nouns |
| we / one / people | on | ohn | The everyday word for "we". Takes the il / elle verb form |
| we | nous | noo | Formal speech and writing |
| you (polite, or several people) | vous | voo | One person politely, or any group |
| they (masculine or mixed) | ils | eel | Sounds the same as "il" |
| they (feminine) | elles | el | Sounds the same as "elle" |

**On versus nous:** In conversation, French speakers say "on" far more often than "nous". "On va au bureau" means "We are going to the office". Use "nous" in formal email and reports.

## Tu Versus Vous

French has two words for "you". The choice shows the relationship between the speakers.

| Situation | France | Quebec |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| A stranger in the street | vous | vous, but tu is common between young people |
| Shop, restaurant or hotel staff | vous | vous. Staff often use tu with customers in casual places |
| A child or a teenager | tu | tu |
| Friends and family | tu | tu |
| Colleagues of the same level | tu in most workplaces, after a short time | tu, usually from the first day |
| Your manager | vous until invited to use tu | tu in most workplaces |
| A client or a senior official | vous | vous |
| Several people, any relationship | vous | vous |

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Can we use "tu"? | On peut se tutoyer? | ohn puh suh tew-twah-yay | Polite way to propose the change |
| You can call me "tu" | Vous pouvez me tutoyer | voo poo-vay muh tew-twah-yay | Offering the casual form |
| Do you speak English? | Tu parles anglais? | tew parl ahn-gleh | Casual |
| Do you speak English? | Vous parlez anglais? | voo par-lay ahn-gleh | Polite |

When in doubt, use "vous". No one is offended by too much politeness. Chapter 10 gives more detail.

## Gender and Articles

Every French noun is **masculine** or **feminine**. The gender changes the article and the adjective. Learn each noun with its article.

### Guessing the Gender from the Ending

| Ending | Usual gender | Example | Common exceptions |
|--------|--------------|---------|-------------------|
| -age | masculine | le voyage (trip), le fromage (cheese) | la page, la plage, l'image (feminine) |
| -ment | masculine | le document, l'appartement | la jument (mare) |
| -eau | masculine | le bureau (office), le bateau (boat) | l'eau, la peau (feminine) |
| -isme | masculine | le tourisme | none in common use |
| A consonant | often masculine | le train, le sac, le contrat | la main, la nuit, la fleur |
| -tion, -sion | feminine | la réunion, la station, la décision | none in common use |
| -té | feminine | la société (company), la qualité | le côté, l'été (masculine) |
| -ette, -elle | feminine | la fourchette (fork), la poubelle (bin) | le squelette |
| -ure | feminine | la voiture (car), la facture (invoice) | le murmure |
| -ie | feminine | la pharmacie, la boulangerie | l'incendie, le génie (masculine) |
| -e (other) | often feminine | la table, la porte, la carte | le livre, le verre, le problème |

### The Articles

| Type | Masculine | Feminine | Before a vowel | Plural |
|------|-----------|----------|----------------|--------|
| The (definite) | le | la | l' | les |
| A, an, some (indefinite) | un | une | un / une | des |
| Some (partitive, uncountable) | du | de la | de l' | des |
| To the (à + article) | au | à la | à l' | aux |
| Of the, from the (de + article) | du | de la | de l' | des |

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| the office | le bureau | luh bew-roh | Masculine |
| the meeting | la réunion | lah ray-ew-nyohn | Feminine |
| the school | l'école | lay-kol | "la" becomes "l'" before a vowel |
| the friends | les amis | lay-zah-mee | Liaison: "s" sounds like "z" |
| a friend | un ami | ehn-nah-mee | Liaison: the "n" is pronounced |
| some bread | du pain | dew pehn | Partitive, masculine |
| some water | de l'eau | duh loh | Partitive before a vowel |
| I am going to the restaurant | Je vais au restaurant | zhuh veh oh res-toh-rahn | à + le = au |
| I am going to the United States | Je vais aux États-Unis | zhuh veh oh-zay-tah-zew-nee | à + les = aux |
| the manager's office | le bureau du directeur | luh bew-roh dew dee-rek-tuhr | de + le = du |

### Adjectives Agree

Adjectives match the noun in gender and number. Most add **-e** for the feminine and **-s** for the plural. Most adjectives go after the noun.

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| a small office | un petit bureau | ehn puh-tee bew-roh | "petit" goes before the noun |
| a small table | une petite table | ewn puh-teet tahbl | The feminine -e makes the "t" sound |
| an important document | un document important | ehn doh-kew-mahn ehn-por-tahn | After the noun |
| an important meeting | une réunion importante | ewn ray-ew-nyohn ehn-por-tahnt | Feminine form |
| good ideas | de bonnes idées | duh bon-zee-day | Plural. "des" becomes "de" before an adjective |
| She is happy | Elle est contente | el eh kohn-tahnt | Masculine: content (kohn-tahn) |

Adjectives that go **before** the noun include: petit, grand, bon, mauvais, jeune, vieux, nouveau, beau, joli, gros, premier, dernier, autre.

## Four Key Verbs: Être, Avoir, Aller, Faire

These four verbs are irregular. They appear in almost every conversation. Learn them by heart.

### Être (to be) and Avoir (to have)

| Subject | Être | Pronunciation | Avoir | Pronunciation |
|---------|------|---------------|-------|---------------|
| je / j' | suis | zhuh swee | ai | zhay |
| tu | es | tew eh | as | tew ah |
| il / elle / on | est | eel eh | a | eel ah |
| nous | sommes | noo som | avons | noo-zah-vohn |
| vous | êtes | voo-zet | avez | voo-zah-vay |
| ils / elles | sont | eel sohn | ont | eel-zohn |

Listen for the difference between "ils sont" (eel sohn, they are) and "ils ont" (eel-zohn, they have). Only the liaison tells them apart.

### Aller (to go) and Faire (to do, to make)

| Subject | Aller | Pronunciation | Faire | Pronunciation |
|---------|-------|---------------|-------|---------------|
| je | vais | zhuh veh | fais | zhuh feh |
| tu | vas | tew vah | fais | tew feh |
| il / elle / on | va | eel vah | fait | eel feh |
| nous | allons | noo-zah-lohn | faisons | noo fuh-zohn |
| vous | allez | voo-zah-lay | faites | voo fet |
| ils / elles | vont | eel vohn | font | eel fohn |

### Everyday Uses

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| I am tired | Je suis fatigué / fatiguée | zhuh swee fah-tee-gay | être + adjective |
| It is three o'clock | Il est trois heures | eel eh trwah-zuhr | être for time |
| I am hungry | J'ai faim | zhay fehn | avoir, not être |
| I am thirsty | J'ai soif | zhay swahf | avoir, not être |
| I am cold | J'ai froid | zhay frwah | avoir, not être |
| I am hot | J'ai chaud | zhay shoh | avoir, not être |
| I am 35 years old | J'ai trente-cinq ans | zhay trahnt-sehn-kahn | avoir for age |
| You are right | Vous avez raison | voo-zah-vay reh-zohn | avoir raison |
| There is / there are | Il y a | eel yah | Spoken: often "ya" |
| How are you? | Comment allez-vous? | koh-mahn-tah-lay voo | aller for health |
| I'm fine | Je vais bien | zhuh veh byehn | aller, not être |
| The weather is nice | Il fait beau | eel feh boh | faire for weather |
| I do sport | Je fais du sport | zhuh feh dew spor | faire + activity |
| What are you doing? | Qu'est-ce que vous faites? | kess kuh voo fet | faire |

## Present Tense: The Three Verb Groups

The French present tense covers the English "I speak", "I am speaking" and "I do speak".

### Group 1: Verbs in -er (parler, to speak)

About 90 percent of French verbs follow this pattern. Four of the six forms sound the same.

| Subject | Form | Pronunciation | Ending |
|---------|------|---------------|--------|
| je | parle | zhuh parl | -e |
| tu | parles | tew parl | -es |
| il / elle / on | parle | eel parl | -e |
| nous | parlons | noo par-lohn | -ons |
| vous | parlez | voo par-lay | -ez |
| ils / elles | parlent | eel parl | -ent (silent) |

Other common -er verbs: travailler (to work), habiter (to live), aimer (to like), chercher (to look for), demander (to ask), donner (to give), regarder (to look at), écouter (to listen), acheter (to buy), payer (to pay), manger (to eat), commencer (to start), envoyer (to send), penser (to think), trouver (to find).

### Group 2: Verbs in -ir (finir, to finish)

| Subject | Form | Pronunciation | Ending |
|---------|------|---------------|--------|
| je | finis | zhuh fee-nee | -is |
| tu | finis | tew fee-nee | -is |
| il / elle / on | finit | eel fee-nee | -it |
| nous | finissons | noo fee-nee-sohn | -issons |
| vous | finissez | voo fee-nee-say | -issez |
| ils / elles | finissent | eel fee-neess | -issent |

Other verbs like finir: choisir (to choose), réussir (to succeed), remplir (to fill in), réfléchir (to think it over), réunir (to bring together).

### Group 3: Verbs in -re and Irregular Verbs

Group 3 holds everything else. Start with attendre (to wait) and prendre (to take).

| Subject | Attendre | Pronunciation | Prendre | Pronunciation |
|---------|----------|---------------|---------|---------------|
| je / j' | attends | zhah-tahn | prends | zhuh prahn |
| tu | attends | tew ah-tahn | prends | tew prahn |
| il / elle / on | attend | eel ah-tahn | prend | eel prahn |
| nous | attendons | noo-zah-tahn-dohn | prenons | noo pruh-nohn |
| vous | attendez | voo-zah-tahn-day | prenez | voo pruh-nay |
| ils / elles | attendent | eel-zah-tahnd | prennent | eel pren |

Verbs like attendre: vendre (to sell), répondre (to answer), entendre (to hear), descendre (to go down), perdre (to lose). Verbs like prendre: comprendre (to understand), apprendre (to learn).

### High-Frequency Irregular Verbs

| Infinitive | Meaning | je | tu | il / elle / on | nous | vous | ils / elles |
|------------|---------|----|----|----------------|------|------|-------------|
| pouvoir | can | peux | peux | peut | pouvons | pouvez | peuvent |
| vouloir | to want | veux | veux | veut | voulons | voulez | veulent |
| devoir | must | dois | dois | doit | devons | devez | doivent |
| savoir | to know (a fact) | sais | sais | sait | savons | savez | savent |
| venir | to come | viens | viens | vient | venons | venez | viennent |
| partir | to leave | pars | pars | part | partons | partez | partent |
| dire | to say | dis | dis | dit | disons | dites | disent |
| voir | to see | vois | vois | voit | voyons | voyez | voient |
| mettre | to put | mets | mets | met | mettons | mettez | mettent |
| connaître | to know (a person, a place) | connais | connais | connaît | connaissons | connaissez | connaissent |

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |

## The Past: Passé Composé

The passé composé is the main past tense in conversation. It covers "I ate" and "I have eaten".

**Formula:** present of **avoir** or **être** + past participle.

### Forming the Past Participle

| Verb group | Rule | Example | Pronunciation |
|------------|------|---------|---------------|
| -er | -er becomes -é | parler: parlé | par-lay |
| -ir | -ir becomes -i | finir: fini | fee-nee |
| -re | -re becomes -u | attendre: attendu | ah-tahn-dew |

### Common Irregular Participles

| Infinitive | Participle | Pronunciation | Meaning |
|------------|------------|---------------|---------|
| être | été | ay-tay | been |
| avoir | eu | ew | had |
| faire | fait | feh | done, made |
| prendre | pris | pree | taken |
| comprendre | compris | kohn-pree | understood |
| dire | dit | dee | said |
| voir | vu | vew | seen |
| pouvoir | pu | pew | been able |
| vouloir | voulu | voo-lew | wanted |
| devoir | dû | dew | had to |
| mettre | mis | mee | put |
| écrire | écrit | ay-kree | written |
| lire | lu | lew | read |
| recevoir | reçu | ruh-sew | received |
| ouvrir | ouvert | oo-vehr | opened |
| venir | venu | vuh-new | come |

### Verbs That Take Être

Most verbs take **avoir**. A small group of movement and change verbs takes **être**, and so do all reflexive verbs. With être, the participle agrees with the subject: add **-e** for feminine and **-s** for plural.

| Infinitive | Participle | Meaning | Infinitive | Participle | Meaning |
|------------|------------|---------|------------|------------|---------|
| aller | allé | gone | venir | venu | come |
| arriver | arrivé | arrived | partir | parti | left |
| entrer | entré | entered | sortir | sorti | gone out |
| monter | monté | gone up | descendre | descendu | gone down |
| rester | resté | stayed | tomber | tombé | fallen |
| naître | né | born | mourir | mort | died |
| rentrer | rentré | gone home | retourner | retourné | returned |
| devenir | devenu | become | revenir | revenu | come back |

### Examples

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| I ate | J'ai mangé | zhay mahn-zhay | avoir |
| We finished the report | Nous avons fini le rapport | noo-zah-vohn fee-nee luh rah-por | avoir |
| Did you see the email? | Vous avez vu le courriel? | voo-zah-vay vew luh koo-ryel | avoir |
| We took the train | On a pris le train | ohn-nah pree luh trehn | avoir, with "on" |
| I went to the office | Je suis allé / allée au bureau | zhuh swee ah-lay oh bew-roh | être. A woman writes "allée" |
| She arrived yesterday | Elle est arrivée hier | el eh ah-ree-vay yehr | être, feminine agreement |
| They left at five | Ils sont partis à cinq heures | eel sohn par-tee ah sehn-kuhr | être, plural agreement |
| I got up early | Je me suis levé / levée tôt | zhuh muh swee luh-vay toh | Reflexive verbs take être |
| I didn't understand | Je n'ai pas compris | zhuh nay pah kohn-pree | "ne ... pas" goes around avoir |
| I was born in Toronto | Je suis né / née à Toronto | zhuh swee nay ah toh-rohn-toh | être |

## Negation

Put **ne** before the verb and **pas** after it. "Ne" becomes "n'" before a vowel.

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| I don't speak Spanish | Je ne parle pas espagnol | zhuh nuh parl pah es-pah-nyol | ne ... pas = not |
| I don't have time | Je n'ai pas le temps | zhuh nay pah luh tahn | n' before a vowel |
| I don't have a car | Je n'ai pas de voiture | zhuh nay pah duh vwah-tewr | un, une, des become "de" after a negative |
| I never eat meat | Je ne mange jamais de viande | zhuh nuh mahnzh zhah-meh duh vyahnd | ne ... jamais = never |
| I don't want anything | Je ne veux rien | zhuh nuh vuh ryehn | ne ... rien = nothing |
| I don't work here any more | Je ne travaille plus ici | zhuh nuh trah-vye plew ee-see | ne ... plus = no longer |
| I don't know anyone | Je ne connais personne | zhuh nuh koh-neh pehr-son | ne ... personne = no one |
| I only have ten euros | Je n'ai que dix euros | zhuh nay kuh dee-zuh-roh | ne ... que = only |

### Spoken French Drops "Ne"

In everyday speech, most speakers in France and Quebec drop "ne" and keep only "pas". Keep "ne" in writing and in formal speech.

| English | Written French | Spoken French | Pronunciation |
|---------|----------------|---------------|---------------|
| I don't know | Je ne sais pas | Je sais pas | zhuh seh pah, often "shay pah" |
| It's not serious | Ce n'est pas grave | C'est pas grave | seh pah grav |
| I don't have time | Je n'ai pas le temps | J'ai pas le temps | zhay pah luh tahn |
| There is no problem | Il n'y a pas de problème | Y a pas de problème | yah pah duh proh-blem |
| I can't | Je ne peux pas | Je peux pas | zhuh puh pah |
| Don't worry | Ne t'inquiète pas | T'inquiète pas | tehn-kyet pah |

## Questions

French has three ways to ask a yes or no question. All three mean the same thing.

| Method | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|--------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Rising intonation | Vous parlez anglais? | voo par-lay ahn-gleh | Casual to polite. The most common in speech |
| Est-ce que | Est-ce que vous parlez anglais? | ess kuh voo par-lay ahn-gleh | Polite. Safe in every situation |
| Inversion | Parlez-vous anglais? | par-lay voo ahn-gleh | Very polite. Common in writing |

### Question Words

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Who? | Qui? | kee | People |
| What? | Que? / Qu'est-ce que? | kuh / kess kuh | Before a verb |
| What? | Quoi? | kwah | After a verb or a preposition. Casual when used alone |
| Where? | Où? | oo | Place |
| When? | Quand? | kahn | Time |
| Why? | Pourquoi? | poor-kwah | Reason. Answer with "Parce que" (pars kuh) |
| How? | Comment? | koh-mahn | Manner |
| How much? / How many? | Combien? | kohn-byehn | Use "combien de" before a noun |
| Which? / What? | Quel? / Quelle? | kel | Agrees with the noun. Same sound |
| Which one? | Lequel? / Laquelle? | luh-kel / lah-kel | Choosing between items |

### The Same Question at Three Levels

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| Where do you live? | Tu habites où? | tew ah-beet oo | Casual. Question word at the end |
| Where do you live? | Où est-ce que vous habitez? | oo ess kuh voo-zah-bee-tay | Polite |
| Where do you live? | Où habitez-vous? | oo ah-bee-tay voo | Very polite |
| What are you doing? | Tu fais quoi? | tew feh kwah | Casual |
| What are you doing? | Qu'est-ce que vous faites? | kess kuh voo fet | Polite |
| What are you doing? | Que faites-vous? | kuh fet voo | Very polite |
| When does the train leave? | Le train part quand? | luh trehn par kahn | Casual |
| When does the train leave? | Quand est-ce que le train part? | kahn-tess kuh luh trehn par | Polite |
| When does the train leave? | Quand part le train? | kahn par luh trehn | Very polite |

**Quebec:** Casual speech adds the particle "-tu" after the verb to make a yes or no question. "Tu veux-tu un café?" means "Do you want a coffee?" and "C'est-tu loin?" means "Is it far?". Understand it. Do not use it in formal situations.

## Object Pronouns

Object pronouns replace a noun. In French they go **before** the verb.

### Direct and Indirect Pronouns

| Person | Direct (him, her, it) | Indirect (to him, to her) | Pronunciation |
|--------|-----------------------|---------------------------|---------------|
| me | me / m' | me / m' | muh |
| you (casual) | te / t' | te / t' | tuh |
| him, it (masculine) | le / l' | lui | luh / lwee |
| her, it (feminine) | la / l' | lui | lah / lwee |
| us | nous | nous | noo |
| you (polite or plural) | vous | vous | voo |
| them | les | leur | lay / luhr |

Use the **indirect** form with verbs that take "à" before a person: parler à, dire à, donner à, envoyer à, demander à, répondre à, téléphoner à, écrire à.

### Examples

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| I see him | Je le vois | zhuh luh vwah | Direct |
| I know her | Je la connais | zhuh lah koh-neh | Direct |
| I'll take them | Je les prends | zhuh lay prahn | Direct |
| I'm listening to you | Je vous écoute | zhuh voo-zay-koot | Direct. "écouter" takes no preposition |
| I'm talking to him / to her | Je lui parle | zhuh lwee parl | Indirect. "lui" covers both |
| I'm writing to them | Je leur écris | zhuh luhr ay-kree | Indirect |
| I'm sending you the document | Je vous envoie le document | zhuh voo-zahn-vwah luh doh-kew-mahn | Indirect "vous" |
| Can you help me? | Pouvez-vous m'aider? | poo-vay voo may-day | With two verbs, the pronoun goes before the infinitive |
| I'm going to buy it | Je vais l'acheter | zhuh veh lash-tay | Before the infinitive |
| I don't know him | Je ne le connais pas | zhuh nuh luh koh-neh pah | "ne" goes before the pronoun |
| I saw it | Je l'ai vu | zhuh lay vew | Before "avoir" in the passé composé |
| Give me that | Donnez-moi ça | doh-nay mwah sah | Commands: the pronoun goes after the verb. "me" becomes "moi" |
| Send it to me | Envoyez-le-moi | ahn-vwah-yay luh mwah | Two pronouns after a command |
| Don't do it | Ne le faites pas | nuh luh fet pah | Negative command: pronoun before the verb |

## Y and En

These two small pronouns are everywhere in spoken French.

- **Y** replaces a place, or "à + thing". It means "there" or "about it".
- **En** replaces "de + thing", or a quantity. It means "some", "of it" or "of them".

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| I'm going there | J'y vais | zhee veh | y = to that place |
| Let's go | On y va | ohn-nee vah | Very common |
| Go ahead | Allez-y | ah-lay-zee | Polite. Casual: "Vas-y" (vah-zee) |
| I'm thinking about it | J'y pense | zhee pahnss | penser à + thing |
| We're there | On y est | ohn-nee eh | Arriving |
| I want some | J'en veux | zhahn vuh | en = some |
| I have two | J'en ai deux | zhahn-nay duh | The number stays, "en" replaces the noun |
| I don't have any | Je n'en ai pas | zhuh nahn-nay pah | Negative |
| Is there any left? | Il en reste? | eel ahn rest | Quantity |
| I need it | J'en ai besoin | zhahn-nay buh-zwehn | avoir besoin de + thing |
| I'll take care of it | Je m'en occupe | zhuh mahn-noh-kewp | s'occuper de. Very useful at work |
| What do you think of it? | Qu'est-ce que vous en pensez? | kess kuh voo-zahn pahn-say | penser de + thing |
| I'm leaving | Je m'en vais | zhuh mahn veh | Fixed expression |

### 22 to One Million

France and Quebec use the same numbers. The forms for 70, 80 and 90 are built from 60 and 20.

| Number | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|--------|--------|---------------|-------|
| 22 | vingt-deux | vehnt-duh | The "t" of vingt sounds from 22 to 29 |
| 30 | trente | trahnt | 31 = trente et un |
| 40 | quarante | kah-rahnt | 41 = quarante et un |
| 50 | cinquante | sehn-kahnt | 51 = cinquante et un |
| 60 | soixante | swah-sahnt | 61 = soixante et un |
| 70 | soixante-dix | swah-sahnt-deess | 60 + 10 |
| 71 | soixante et onze | swah-sahnt ay ohnz | 60 + 11 |

## Useful Connectors

| English | French | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|--------|---------------|-------|
| and | et | ay | The "t" is never pronounced |
| but | mais | meh | Contrast |
| or | ou | oo | No accent. "où" with an accent means "where" |
| because | parce que | pars kuh | Reason |
| so | donc | dohnk | Result |
| then | puis / ensuite | pwee / ahn-sweet | Sequence. Quebec, spoken: "pis" (pee) |
| also | aussi | oh-see | Addition |
| with / without | avec / sans | ah-vek / sahn | Prepositions |
| if | si | see | "si + il" becomes "s'il" |

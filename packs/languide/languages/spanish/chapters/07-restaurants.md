## Tipping Guide

| Country | Tipping Custom |
|---------|---------------|
| **Spain** | Not expected. Round up or leave small change. 5-10% for exceptional service. |
| **Mexico** | 10-15% is standard at sit-down restaurants. Check if "propina incluida." |
| **Argentina** | 10% is standard at sit-down restaurants. Cash tips preferred. |
| **Colombia** | 10% often added automatically ("servicio voluntario"). You can decline. |
| **Chile** | 10% is standard. Often added to the bill. |
| **Peru** | 10% at nice restaurants. Not required at casual spots. |

# Core Communication Phrases

This chapter provides essential phrases for everyday interactions in Spanish-speaking countries, organized by communication scenario. Each section includes **pattern phrases** (templates with slots you can fill in) and **fixed phrases** (ready-to-use expressions).

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

These reusable patterns let you express many ideas by swapping words in the slots marked with `[brackets]`.

### Requesting Something

| Pattern | Spanish | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|---------|---------------|---------|
| [thing] please | [cosa] por favor | [KO-sah] por fah-BOR | Agua por favor = Water please |
| I would like [thing] | Quisiera [cosa] | kee-SYEH-rah [KO-sah] | Quisiera un café = I would like a coffee |
| Could you please [action]? | ¿Podría [infinitivo]? | poh-DREE-ah [inf.] | ¿Podría repetir? = Could you please repeat? |
| I need [thing] | Necesito [cosa] | neh-seh-SEE-toh [KO-sah] | Necesito ayuda = I need help |
| I want [thing] | Quiero [cosa] | KYEH-roh [KO-sah] | Quiero la cuenta = I want the bill |
| Please [action] | Por favor, [imperativo] | por fah-BOR [imp.] | Por favor, espere = Please wait |

### Asking Permission

| Pattern | Spanish | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|---------|---------------|---------|
| Can I [action]? | ¿Puedo [infinitivo]? | PWEH-doh [inf.] | ¿Puedo sentarme aquí? = Can I sit here? |
| May I [action]? | ¿Me permite [infinitivo]? | meh pehr-MEE-teh [inf.] | ¿Me permite pasar? = May I pass? |
| Is it okay if I [action]? | ¿Está bien si [presente]? | ehs-TAH byehn see [pres.] | ¿Está bien si fumo? = Is it okay if I smoke? |

### Asking Questions

| Pattern | Spanish | Pronunciation | Example |
|---------|---------|---------------|---------|
| Where is [place]? | ¿Dónde está [lugar]? | DOHN-deh ehs-TAH [loo-GAR] | ¿Dónde está el baño? = Where is the bathroom? |
| How do I get to [place]? | ¿Cómo llego a [lugar]? | KOH-moh YEH-goh ah [loo-GAR] | ¿Cómo llego al centro? = How do I get to downtown? |
| Do you have [thing]? | ¿Tiene [cosa]? | TYEH-neh [KO-sah] | ¿Tiene menú en inglés? = Do you have an English menu? |
| What is [thing]? | ¿Qué es [cosa]? | keh ehs [KO-sah] | ¿Qué es esto? = What is this? |
| How much is [thing]? | ¿Cuánto cuesta [cosa]? | KWAHN-toh KWEHS-tah [KO-sah] | ¿Cuánto cuesta esto? = How much is this? |
| What time does [place] [action]? | ¿A qué hora [verbo] [lugar]? | ah keh OH-rah [BEHR-boh] | ¿A qué hora cierra la tienda? = What time does the store close? |

## Greetings and Basic Courtesies

### Daily Greetings

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | When to Use |
|---------|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Good morning | Buenos días | BWEH-nohs DEE-ahs | Until ~12 PM |
| Good afternoon | Buenas tardes | BWEH-nahs TAHR-dehs | ~12 PM - 8 PM |
| Good evening/night | Buenas noches | BWEH-nahs NOH-chehs | After ~8 PM |
| Hello | Hola | OH-lah | Any time, casual |
| Hi, how are you? | ¡Hola! ¿Qué tal? | OH-lah keh tahl | Casual greeting |
| How are you? (formal) | ¿Cómo está usted? | KOH-moh ehs-TAH oos-TEHD | Formal |
| Goodbye | Adiós | ah-DYOHS | Standard farewell |
| See you later | Hasta luego | AHS-tah LWEH-goh | Common, any formality |
| See you tomorrow | Hasta mañana | AHS-tah mah-NYAH-nah | Same as English |
| See you soon | Hasta pronto | AHS-tah PROHN-toh | Friendly parting |

### Thank You Expressions

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Formality |
|---------|---------|---------------|-----------|
| Thanks | Gracias | GRAH-syahs | Universal |

| I really appreciate it | Se lo agradezco mucho | seh loh ah-grah-DEHS-koh MOO-choh | Formal |

### Apologies and Excusing Yourself

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | When to Use |
|---------|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Excuse me (attention) | Disculpe | dees-KOOL-peh | Getting attention (formal) |
| Excuse me (passing) | Permiso / Con permiso | pehr-MEE-soh / kon pehr-MEE-soh | Passing through a crowd |
| Sorry | Lo siento | loh SYEHN-toh | Apology |
| I'm very sorry | Lo siento mucho | loh SYEHN-toh MOO-choh | Stronger apology |
| Pardon? | ¿Perdón? / ¿Cómo? | pehr-DOHN / KOH-moh | Didn't hear/understand |

### Responding to Thanks

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Formality |
|---------|---------|---------------|-----------|
| You're welcome | De nada | deh NAH-dah | Standard |
| Don't mention it | No hay de qué | noh eye deh keh | Slightly more formal |
| It's nothing | No es nada | noh ehs NAH-dah | Casual |
| My pleasure | Con mucho gusto | kon MOO-choh GOOS-toh | Warm, polite |

## Meeting People and Small Talk

### Introductions

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|---------|---------------|-------|
| Nice to meet you | Mucho gusto | MOO-choh GOOS-toh | Standard, all situations |
| Nice to meet you (formal) | Encantado/Encantada | ehn-kahn-TAH-doh/dah | -o = male speaker, -a = female |
| I'm [name] | Soy [nombre] | soy [NOHM-breh] | Stating your name |
| My name is [name] | Me llamo [nombre] | meh YAH-moh [NOHM-breh] | More formal |
| Where are you from? | ¿De dónde eres/es? | deh DOHN-deh EH-rehs/ehs | eres = tú, es = usted |
| I'm from [country] | Soy de [país] | soy deh [pah-EES] | Stating origin |
| What do you do? | ¿A qué te/se dedica? | ah keh teh/seh deh-DEE-kah | te = tú, se = usted |

### Small Talk Questions

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|---------|---------------|-------|
| Is this your first time here? | ¿Es tu/su primera vez aquí? | ehs too/soo pree-MEH-rah behs ah-KEE | tu = tú, su = usted |
| How long are you staying? | ¿Cuánto tiempo te/se queda? | KWAHN-toh TYEHM-poh teh/seh KEH-dah | Common question |
| Are you traveling alone? | ¿Viaja solo/sola? | BYAH-hah SOH-loh/SOH-lah | -o = male, -a = female |
| Do you like [place]? | ¿Le/Te gusta [lugar]? | leh/teh GOOS-tah [loo-GAR] | Le = usted, Te = tú |

### Small Talk Responses

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Notes |

## Asking for Help

### Getting Attention

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Context |
|---------|---------|---------------|---------|
| Excuse me | Disculpe | dees-KOOL-peh | Standard, formal |
| Excuse me (casual) | Oye / Oiga | OH-yeh / OY-gah | Casual / Semi-formal |

### Requesting Assistance

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Formality |
|---------|---------|---------------|-----------|
| Can you help me? | ¿Puede ayudarme? | PWEH-deh ah-yoo-DAHR-meh | Formal |
| Can you help me? (casual) | ¿Me puedes ayudar? | meh PWEH-dehs ah-yoo-DAHR | Informal |

| Help! | ¡Ayuda! | ah-YOO-dah | Emergency |

| I have a problem | Tengo un problema | TEHN-goh oon proh-BLEH-mah | Explaining situation |

### Communication Difficulties

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|---------|---------------|-------|

| I don't speak Spanish | No hablo español | noh AH-bloh ehs-pah-NYOHL | Explaining |

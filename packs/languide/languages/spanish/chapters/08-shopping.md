## Market and Bargaining

In traditional markets (mercados), bargaining is common in Latin America but less so in Spain.

| English | Spanish | Pronunciation | Notes |
|---------|---------|---------------|-------|
| How much for [item]? | ¿Cuánto por [cosa]? | KWAHN-toh por [KO-sah] | Informal pricing |
| What's the best price? | ¿Cuál es el mejor precio? | kwahl ehs ehl meh-HOR PREH-syoh | Opening to bargain |
| That's too expensive | Es muy caro | ehs mooy KAH-roh | |
| Can you lower the price? | ¿Me puede hacer un descuento? | meh PWEH-deh ah-SEHR oon dehs-KWEHN-toh | Polite way to bargain |
| I'll give you [amount] | Le doy [cantidad] | leh doy [kahn-tee-DAHD] | Making a counter-offer |
| If I buy two? | ¿Si compro dos? | see KOHM-proh dohs | Volume discount |
| Deal | ¡Trato hecho! | TRAH-toh EH-choh | Agreeing on price |
| I'll think about it | Voy a pensarlo | boy ah pehn-SAHR-loh | Polite exit |
| Fresh today? | ¿Es fresco/de hoy? | ehs FREHS-koh / deh oy | At food markets |
| Give me half a kilo | Deme medio kilo | DEH-meh MEH-dyoh KEE-loh | Produce markets |
| A bit more / a bit less | Un poco más / un poco menos | oon POH-koh mahs / oon POH-koh MEH-nohs | Adjusting quantity |

## Shopping Hours by Region

| Region | Typical Hours | Notes |
|--------|--------------|-------|
| Spain | 10:00–14:00, 17:00–20:30 | Many close for siesta. Large stores stay open. |
| Spain (large stores) | 10:00–21:00 or 22:00 | El Corte Inglés, shopping centers |
| Mexico | 10:00–20:00 or later | Shopping centers open late |
| Argentina | 10:00–20:00 (shops), 10:00–22:00 (malls) | Some close for lunch in smaller cities |

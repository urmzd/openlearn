# Hotels and Accommodation

## Accommodation Types

| English | Spanish | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| hotel | hotel | Standard hotel, star-rated (1–5 estrellas) |
| hostel | hostal (Spain) / hostel (LatAm) | Budget accommodation; hostal in Spain is a basic hotel (NOT a backpacker hostel) |
| backpacker hostel | albergue juvenil | Shared dorms, social atmosphere |
| bed and breakfast | pensión / casa de huéspedes | Family-run, basic rooms |
| parador | parador | Spain: luxury hotel in historic building (castles, monasteries) |
| inn | posada | Small, traditional inn (common in LatAm) |
| vacation rental | alquiler vacacional / alojamiento | Airbnb-style |
| apartment | apartamento / departamento | apartamento = Spain, departamento = LatAm |
| cabin | cabaña | Mountain/rural areas |
| boutique hotel | hotel boutique | Trendy, design-focused |

## Floor Numbering

**Important:** In Spain and most of Latin America, the ground floor is called **planta baja** (ground floor), and what Americans call the "second floor" is the **primera planta** (first floor).

| American English | Spain / LatAm | Spanish Term |
|-----------------|---------------|-------------|
| 1st floor (ground) | Planta baja (PB) | planta baja |
| 2nd floor | 1st floor (1°) | primera planta/piso |
| 3rd floor | 2nd floor (2°) | segunda planta/piso |
| 4th floor | 3rd floor (3°) | tercera planta/piso |

**Note:** Some modern buildings in Latin America use American-style numbering, especially in newer developments.

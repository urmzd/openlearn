### Taxi Tips by Region

| City | Tip |
|------|-----|
| Madrid/Barcelona | Metered taxis, supplements for airport/luggage. Tipping optional (round up). |
| Mexico City | Use authorized taxis (pink/white) or apps. Avoid hailing random taxis. |
| Buenos Aires | Metered (reloj). Remis = pre-arranged car service, agreed price. |
| Bogotá | Use apps (Uber, InDriver). If hailing, confirm they use the meter. |

# Work and Business

This chapter gives you the Japanese you need at work: introductions, meetings, email, calls, small talk and formal register. Each section uses **pattern phrases** (templates with slots you can fill in) and **fixed phrases** (ready-to-use expressions). Work Japanese is more formal than casual conversation, so most phrases here use the polite or humble level.

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

Swap the words in the slots marked with `[brackets]`.

| Pattern | Japanese | Romaji | Example |
|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| I am [name] of [company] | [会社]の[名前]と申します | [kaisha] no [namae] to mōshimasu | ABC社の田中と申します (ABC-sha no Tanaka to mōshimasu) = I am Tanaka of ABC |
| I am in charge of [area] | [分野]を担当しております | [bun'ya] o tantō shite orimasu | 開発を担当しております (kaihatsu o tantō shite orimasu) = I am in charge of development |
| Could you [action]? | [動詞-て形]いただけますか | [verb-te] itadakemasu ka | 確認していただけますか (kakunin shite itadakemasu ka) = Could you check it? |
| I would be grateful if you could [action] | [動詞-て形]いただけると幸いです | [verb-te] itadakeru to saiwai desu | 教えていただけると幸いです (oshiete itadakeru to saiwai desu) = I would be grateful if you could tell me |
| May I [action]? | [動詞-て形]もよろしいでしょうか | [verb-te] mo yoroshii deshō ka | 質問してもよろしいでしょうか (shitsumon shite mo yoroshii deshō ka) = May I ask a question? |
| About [topic] | [話題]についてですが | [wadai] ni tsuite desu ga | 来週のリリースについてですが (raishū no rirīsu ni tsuite desu ga) = About next week's release |
| I am contacting you about [matter] | [用件]の件でご連絡いたしました | [yōken] no ken de gorenraku itashimashita | 契約の件でご連絡いたしました (keiyaku no ken de gorenraku itashimashita) = I am contacting you about the contract |
| I will [action] by [deadline] | [期限]までに[動詞]ます | [kigen] made ni [verb]masu | 金曜日までに送ります (kin'yōbi made ni okurimasu) = I will send it by Friday |
| How about [option]? | [案]はいかがでしょうか | [an] wa ikaga deshō ka | 来週の火曜日はいかがでしょうか (raishū no kayōbi wa ikaga deshō ka) = How about next Tuesday? |
| I will [action] (humble) | [名詞]いたします | [noun] itashimasu | 確認いたします (kakunin itashimasu) = I will check |
| Is [person] available? | [名前]様はいらっしゃいますか | [namae]-sama wa irasshaimasu ka | 鈴木様はいらっしゃいますか (Suzuki-sama wa irasshaimasu ka) = Is Suzuki-sama available? |

### Business Card Exchange

| English | Japanese | Romaji | Notes |
|---------|----------|--------|-------|
| Business card | 名刺 | meishi | Carry them to every first meeting |
| May we exchange business cards? | 名刺を交換させていただけますか | meishi o kōkan sasete itadakemasu ka | Very polite |
| Thank you, I accept it | ありがとうございます。頂戴いたします | arigatō gozaimasu. chōdai itashimasu | Say this as you receive a card |
| I am sorry, I am out of cards | あいにく名刺を切らしておりまして、申し訳ございません | ainiku meishi o kirashite orimashite, mōshiwake gozaimasen | Standard apology |
| Could you tell me how to read your name? | お名前の読み方を教えていただけますか | onamae no yomikata o oshiete itadakemasu ka | Kanji names have many readings |

- Hand the card over with both hands, with the text facing the other person.
- Say your company and your name as you hand it over, then bow slightly.
- Read the card you receive before you put it away.

## Email and Chat

### Email Structure

A Japanese business email follows a fixed order. Keep each part on its own line.

| Part | Japanese | Romaji | What goes there |
|------|----------|--------|-----------------|
| Subject | 件名 | kenmei | The topic in one short noun phrase |
| Addressee | 宛名 | atena | Company, then family name with 様 |
| Greeting | 挨拶 | aisatsu | お世話になっております |
| Who you are | 名乗り | nanori | Your company and family name |
| Main text | 本文 | honbun | Purpose first, then details |
| Closing | 結び | musubi | よろしくお願いいたします |
| Signature | 署名 | shomei | Company, name, contact details |

### Openers and Closers

| English | Japanese | Romaji | Notes |
|---------|----------|--------|-------|
| Thank you always for your support | いつもお世話になっております | itsumo osewa ni natte orimasu | Standard opener to clients |
| This is [name] of [company] | [会社]の[名前]です | [kaisha] no [namae] desu | Second line of every email |
| Thank you for contacting me | ご連絡ありがとうございます | gorenraku arigatō gozaimasu | Reply opener |
| I am sorry for my late reply | 返信が遅くなり、申し訳ありません | henshin ga osoku nari, mōshiwake arimasen | Reply opener |
| I am attaching the file | ファイルを添付いたします | fairu o tenpu itashimasu | Humble |
| Please check it | ご確認をお願いいたします | gokakunin o onegai itashimasu | Very polite request |
| Please let me know if anything is unclear | ご不明な点がございましたら、お知らせください | gofumei na ten ga gozaimashitara, oshirase kudasai | Formal |
| I look forward to your reply | お返事をお待ちしております | ohenji o omachi shite orimasu | Humble |
| Thank you in advance | 何卒よろしくお願いいたします | nanitozo yoroshiku onegai itashimasu | Strongest standard closer |

## Formal Register: Keigo

Keigo is the system of formal speech. It has three layers, and work conversations use all three.

### The Three Layers

| Layer | Japanese | Romaji | What it does | Example |
|-------|----------|--------|--------------|---------|
| Polite | 丁寧語 | teineigo | Uses です and ます. Safe with everyone | 行きます (ikimasu) = go |
| Respectful | 尊敬語 | sonkeigo | Raises the actions of the other person | いらっしゃいます (irasshaimasu) = go |
| Humble | 謙譲語 | kenjōgo | Lowers your own actions and those of your side | 参ります (mairimasu) = go |

- Use respectful forms for what a client or manager does.
- Use humble forms for what you or your company does.
- Never use respectful forms for yourself.

### Building Keigo from Any Verb

| Pattern | Japanese | Romaji | Example |
|---------|----------|--------|---------|
| Respectful: [person] does [action] | お[動詞-stem]になります | o[verb-stem] ni narimasu | お帰りになります (okaeri ni narimasu) = he or she goes home |
| Humble: I do [action] for you | お[動詞-stem]します | o[verb-stem] shimasu | お持ちします (omochi shimasu) = I will bring it |
| Respectful request: please [action] | お[動詞-stem]ください | o[verb-stem] kudasai | お待ちください (omachi kudasai) = please wait |
| Respectful request with a noun | ご[名詞]ください | go[noun] kudasai | ご確認ください (gokakunin kudasai) = please check |
| Humble with a noun | ご[名詞]いたします | go[noun] itashimasu | ご連絡いたします (gorenraku itashimasu) = I will contact you |

### How Refusals Are Phrased

A direct "no" is rare at work. Listen for these phrases: they usually mean no.

| English | Japanese | Romaji | Notes |
|---------|----------|--------|-------|
| We will consider it | 検討します | kentō shimasu | Often a soft no. Do not treat it as a yes |
| We will consider it (humble) | 検討させていただきます | kentō sasete itadakimasu | Same meaning, to clients |
| That is difficult | 難しいです | muzukashii desu | Usually means no |
| That is a little... | それはちょっと | sore wa chotto | The unfinished sentence is the refusal |
| It is tight for the schedule | スケジュール的に厳しいです | sukejūru-teki ni kibishii desu | Refuses on timing |
| Please let me take it back first | 一度持ち帰らせてください | ichido mochikaerasete kudasai | Delays the decision |
| I cannot answer right now | 今すぐにはお答えできません | ima sugu ni wa okotae dekimasen | Polite |
| Thank you for the offer, but | せっかくですが | sekkaku desu ga | Opens a refusal of an offer or invitation |
| I am sorry, but we will pass this time | 申し訳ございませんが、今回は見送らせていただきます | mōshiwake gozaimasen ga, konkai wa miokurasete itadakimasu | Clear formal refusal |

# Hotels and Accommodation

This chapter covers hotels from booking to check-out. It also covers renting an apartment, because a learner who stays for work often needs a landlord more than a front desk. In Russian a hotel room is a номер, the same word as "number".

---

## Pattern Phrases for Accommodation

| Pattern | Russian | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| I have a booking in the name of [name] | У меня бронь на имя [name, nominative] | u menyá bron' na ímya [name] | У меня бронь на имя Смит (u menyá bron' na ímya Smit) = I have a booking in the name of Smith |
| I need a room for [period] | Мне нужен номер на [period, accusative] | mne núzhen nómer na [period] | Мне нужен номер на две ночи (mne núzhen nómer na dve nóchi) = I need a room for two nights |
| Is there [thing] in the room? | В номере есть [thing, nominative]? | v nómere yest' [thing] | В номере есть сейф? (v nómere yest' seyf) = Is there a safe in the room? |
| There is no [thing] in the room | В номере нет [thing, genitive] | v nómere net [thing] | В номере нет горячей воды (v nómere net goryáchey vodý) = There is no hot water in the room |
| [thing] does not work | Не работает [thing, nominative] | ne rabótayet [thing] | Не работает кондиционер (ne rabótayet konditsionér) = The air conditioning does not work |
| May I have [thing]? | Можно [thing, accusative]? | mózhno [thing] | Можно ещё одну подушку? (mózhno yeshchyó odnú podúshku) = May I have one more pillow? |
| Could you bring [thing]? | Не могли бы вы принести [thing, accusative]? | ne moglí by vy prinestí [thing] | Не могли бы вы принести утюг? (ne moglí by vy prinestí utyúg) = Could you bring an iron? |
| At what time is [event]? | Во сколько [event, nominative]? | vo skól'ko [event] | Во сколько завтрак? (vo skól'ko závtrak) = At what time is breakfast? |
| Where is [place]? | Где [place, nominative]? | gde [place] | Где спортзал? (gde sportzál) = Where is the gym? |
| Wake me at [time] | Разбудите меня в [time, accusative] | razbudíte menyá v [time] | Разбудите меня в семь утра (razbudíte menyá v sem' utrá) = Wake me at seven in the morning |
| How much is [thing] per [period]? | Сколько стоит [thing, nominative] в [period, accusative]? | skól'ko stóit [thing] v [period] | Сколько стоит номер в сутки? (skól'ko stóit nómer v sútki) = How much is a room per day? |
| The key to room [number] | Ключ от номера [number, nominative] | klyuch ot nómera [number] | Ключ от номера триста пять (klyuch ot nómera trísta pyat') = The key to room 305 |
| I want to extend my stay by [period] | Я хочу продлить проживание на [period, accusative] | ya khochú prodlít' prozhivániye na [period] | Я хочу продлить проживание на одну ночь (ya khochú prodlít' prozhivániye na odnú noch') = I want to extend my stay by one night |

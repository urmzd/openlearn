# Directions and Navigation

This chapter covers asking the way, understanding the answer, reading an address and finding a room inside a building. Russian answers to "where" questions use cases, so each pattern names the case of its slot.

---

## Pattern Phrases for Directions

| Pattern | Russian | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Where is [place]? | Где [place, nominative]? | gde [place] | Где аптека? (gde aptéka) = Where is the pharmacy? |
| Where is the nearest [place]? | Где ближайший [place, nominative]? | gde blizháyshiy [place] | Где ближайший банкомат? (gde blizháyshiy bankomát) = Where is the nearest cash machine? Feminine noun: ближайшая (blizháyshaya). Neuter: ближайшее (blizháysheye) |
| How do I walk to [place]? | Как пройти к [place, dative]? | kak proytí k [place] | Как пройти к метро? (kak proytí k metró) = How do I walk to the metro? |
| How do I ride to [place]? | Как доехать до [place, genitive]? | kak doyékhat' do [place] | Как доехать до вокзала? (kak doyékhat' do vokzála) = How do I get to the station? |
| Is [place] far from here? | [place, nominative] далеко отсюда? | [place] dalekó otsyúda | Метро далеко отсюда? (metró dalekó otsyúda) = Is the metro far from here? |
| I am looking for [place] | Я ищу [place, accusative] | ya ishchú [place] | Я ищу гостиницу (ya ishchú gostínitsu) = I am looking for the hotel |
| Is there [place] nearby? | Здесь рядом есть [place, nominative]? | zdes' ryádom yest' [place] | Здесь рядом есть банк? (zdes' ryádom yest' bank) = Is there a bank nearby? |
| How long is the walk to [place]? | Сколько идти до [place, genitive]? | skól'ko idtí do [place] | Сколько идти до центра? (skól'ko idtí do tséntra) = How long is the walk to the centre? |
| Turn [direction] at [landmark] | Поверните [direction] у [landmark, genitive] | poverníte [direction] u [landmark] | Поверните направо у банка (poverníte naprávo u bánka) = Turn right at the bank |
| Go as far as [landmark] | Идите до [landmark, genitive] | idíte do [landmark] | Идите до светофора (idíte do svetofóra) = Go as far as the traffic light |
| It is next to [landmark] | Это рядом с [landmark, instrumental] | éto ryádom s [landmark] | Это рядом с парком (éto ryádom s párkom) = It is next to the park |
| It is opposite [landmark] | Это напротив [landmark, genitive] | éto naprótiv [landmark] | Это напротив вокзала (éto naprótiv vokzála) = It is opposite the station |
| It is on [street] | Это на [street, prepositional] | éto na [street] | Это на улице Пушкина (éto na úlitse Púshkina) = It is on Pushkin Street |
| I am at [place] | Я у [place, genitive] | ya u [place] | Я у входа (ya u vkhóda) = I am at the entrance |

- **к plus dative** means "towards". Use it for walking to a building or a person.
- **до plus genitive** means "as far as". Use it for distance and for rides.
- **в or на plus accusative** means "into" or "to": в центр (v tsentr), на вокзал (na vokzál).

## Politeness Tiers for Asking the Way

| English | Russian | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| Where is the metro? (casual) | Не знаешь, где метро? | ne znáyesh', gde metró | Casual, ты: to a friend or a young person your age |
| Excuse me, where is the metro? (polite) | Извините, где метро? | izviníte, gde metró | Polite: the everyday default |
| Tell me, please, where is the metro? | Скажите, пожалуйста, где метро? | skazhíte, pozháluysta, gde metró | Polite, a full opener |
| Could you tell me where the metro is? | Вы не подскажете, где находится метро? | vy ne podskázhete, gde nakhóditsya metró | Very polite. The negative form softens the question |

## Addresses and Buildings

A Russian address goes from large to small: city, street, building, apartment or office. The ground floor is the **first floor**: первый этаж.

### Parts of an Address

| English | Russian | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| street | улица | úlitsa | Written ул. |
| building | дом | dom | Written д. The building number |
| block | корпус | kórpus | Written к. One of several blocks with the same number |
| structure | строение | stroyéniye | Written стр. A separate building in the same plot |
| entrance, stairwell | подъезд | podyézd | Apartment blocks have several numbered entrances |
| floor | этаж | etázh | The first floor is at street level |
| apartment | квартира | kvartíra | Written кв. |
| office | офис | ófis | In business addresses |
| intercom | домофон | domofón | At the entrance door |
| door code | код | kod | Ask for it before you visit |

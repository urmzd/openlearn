# Writing System: The Cyrillic Alphabet

Russian uses the **Cyrillic alphabet**. It has **33 letters**: 10 vowels, 21 consonants and 2 signs with no sound. Each letter has one main sound, so reading is regular. Most learners read slowly after three or four sessions.

This chapter sorts the letters into three groups. Learn one group per session.

---

## Group 1: Letters That Look and Sound Like English

Six letters are friends. They look familiar and they sound as you expect.

| Upper | Lower | Name | Sound | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|-------|-------|------|-------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| А | а | а | "a" in "father" | атом | átom | atom |
| Е | е | е | "ye" in "yes" | кафе | kafé | cafe |
| К | к | ка | "k" in "kit" | кот | kot | cat |
| М | м | эм | "m" in "map" | мама | máma | mum |
| О | о | о | "o" in "more" | том | tom | volume |
| Т | т | тэ | "t" in "top" | такт | takt | tact |

With these six letters you can already read: кто (kto) = who, там (tam) = there, тема (téma) = topic.

| Upper | Lower | Name | Sound | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |

### The Two Signs

| Upper | Lower | Name | Job | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|-------|-------|------|-----|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Ъ | ъ | твёрдый знак | Hard sign. Separates a prefix from the next vowel | подъезд | podyézd | building entrance |
| Ь | ь | мягкий знак | Soft sign. Makes the consonant before it soft | день | den' | day |

The names of the signs are твёрдый знак (tvyórdyy znak) and мягкий знак (myágkiy znak). The name of й is и краткое (i krátkoye), "short i". No word starts with ъ, ы or ь.

| Position | Letters |

| 12 to 22 | К Л М Н О П Р С Т У Ф |
| 23 to 33 | Х Ц Ч Ш Щ Ъ Ы Ь Э Ю Я |

### The Letter ё in Real Life

Many printed texts replace ё with е. Readers know the word and say "yo". This guide always writes ё. When you see всё written as все in a text, the context shows which word it is.

| With ё | Transliteration | Meaning | Often printed as |
|--------|-----------------|---------|------------------|
| ещё | yeshchyó | still, more | еще |
| счёт | schyot | bill, invoice | счет |
| всё | vsyo | everything | все |
| идёт | idyót | goes | идет |
| зелёный | zelyónyy | green | зеленый |

## Italic and Handwritten Forms

Italic print and handwriting use shapes that differ from the upright print letters. Five lowercase letters cause most of the trouble. Menus, shop signs, notes from colleagues and greeting cards all use these forms.

| Print | Italic | The italic form looks like | Russian | In italics | Transliteration |
|-------|--------|----------------------------|---------|------------|-----------------|
| т | *т* | Latin "m" | тут | *тут* | tut |
| д | *д* | Latin "g", or a rounded "d" with a tail up | да | *да* | da |
| г | *г* | A mirrored "s", or a small hook | год | *год* | god |
| и | *и* | Latin "u" | или | *или* | íli |
| п | *п* | Latin "n" | пять | *пять* | pyat' |

### More Shapes That Change

| Print | Italic | The italic form looks like | Notes |
|-------|--------|----------------------------|-------|
| в | *в* | A tall loop, close to Latin "b" | In handwriting it has two loops |
| й | *й* | Latin "u" with a small curve above | The curve is the only difference from и |
| ш | *ш* | Latin "w" | Handwriting often adds a line under it |
| м | *м* | Print м with a small hook at the start | The hook separates it from the letters before |
| л | *л* | A narrow arch with a small hook at the start | Easy to mix with м |
| б | *б* | A "6" with a flat tail to the right | Same idea as print |

### Reading Handwriting

- In handwriting, ш, и, л and м join into rows of similar strokes. Count the strokes.
- Writers often put a line **under** ш and a line **over** т to tell them apart.
- A handwritten т has three strokes, like "m". A handwritten ш has three strokes, like "w".
- You do not need to write cursive. Printing each letter by hand is acceptable for an adult learner. Reading italic is necessary.

## Typing in Russian

### Two Keyboard Layouts

| Layout | How it works | Good for |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| Standard Russian (ЙЦУКЕН) | Every key has its own Cyrillic letter. The layout is not related to QWERTY | Keyboards with printed Cyrillic letters, shared office computers |
| Phonetic | Each Cyrillic letter sits on the Latin key with a similar sound | A learner with a Latin keyboard who wants to type on day one |

### Setting Up a Phonetic Layout

| System | Where to find it | Layout name |
|--------|------------------|-------------|
| macOS | System Settings, Keyboard, Input Sources | Russian Phonetic |
| Windows 10 and 11 | Settings, Time and Language, Language, Russian, Keyboards | Russian Mnemonic |
| Linux | Keyboard settings, add an input source | Russian (phonetic) |
| iPhone and Android | Keyboard settings, add Russian | Standard Russian. Phones show the letters, so the standard layout is easy |

### Phonetic Key Map

This table shows the usual phonetic map. Layouts differ a little, so check your own with the on-screen keyboard viewer.

| Latin key | Cyrillic | Latin key | Cyrillic | Latin key | Cyrillic |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|
| A | а | J | й | S | с |
| B | б | K | к | T | т |
| V | в | L | л | U | у |
| G | г | M | м | F | ф |
| D | д | N | н | H | х or ч |
| E | е | O | о | C | ц |
| Z | з | P | п | W | ш |
| I | и | R | р | Y | ы |

The letters ё, ж, щ, ъ, ь, э, ю and я do not match a Latin letter. One of х and ч takes the H key, and the other moves to another key. A phonetic layout puts all these letters on Q, X and the punctuation keys. Their places differ between systems. Find them once in the keyboard viewer and note them.

### Typing Tips

- On a phone, hold е to type ё. Hold ь to type ъ.
- Switch layouts with one shortcut. Set it up on the first day.
- Type the phrases from each Practice table. Typing is the fastest way to fix spelling.
- Search engines accept Russian typed in Cyrillic only. Transliteration gives poor results.

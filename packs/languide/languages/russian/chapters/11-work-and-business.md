# Work and Business

This chapter covers Russian at work: introductions, meetings, email, chat, calls and office vocabulary. Work Russian is more formal than street Russian. Start with the rules of the formal register, then learn the phrases.

---

## The Formal Register at Work

### Seven Rules

1. **Use вы with everyone at first.** Move to ты only when a colleague offers it or when the whole team uses it.
2. **Use name and patronymic for senior people.** Managers, clients, officials and older colleagues expect it: Иван Петрович, Анна Сергеевна.
3. **Use the full first name with вы as the neutral form.** Many modern companies drop the patronymic. Follow what others do.
4. **Do not use a short name until the person offers it.** Say Александр, not Саша, to a new contact.
5. **Write Вы and Ваш with a capital letter in a formal letter to one person.** In chat, lowercase is normal.
6. **Use the conditional for requests upward.** Не могли бы вы and я хотел бы replace plain imperatives.
7. **Keep job titles masculine in formal speech.** A woman is also директор, менеджер and инженер.

## Pattern Phrases for Work

| Pattern | Russian | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| I work as [job] | Я работаю [job, instrumental] | ya rabótayu [job] | Я работаю аналитиком (ya rabótayu analítikom) = I work as an analyst |
| I work at [company] | Я работаю в [company, prepositional] | ya rabótayu v [company] | Я работаю в банке (ya rabótayu v bánke) = I work at a bank |
| I am responsible for [area] | Я отвечаю за [area, accusative] | ya otvecháyu za [area] | Я отвечаю за тестирование (ya otvecháyu za testírovaniye) = I am responsible for testing |
| I work on [project] | Я работаю над [project, instrumental] | ya rabótayu nad [project] | Я работаю над новым проектом (ya rabótayu nad nóvym proyéktom) = I work on a new project |
| Let's discuss [topic] | Давайте обсудим [topic, accusative] | daváyte obsúdim [topic] | Давайте обсудим сроки (daváyte obsúdim sróki) = Let's discuss the deadlines |
| May we move [event] to [day]? | Можно перенести [event, accusative] на [day, accusative]? | mózhno perenestí [event] na [day] | Можно перенести встречу на пятницу? (mózhno perenestí vstréchu na pyátnitsu) = May we move the meeting to Friday? |
| Please send [thing] | Пришлите, пожалуйста, [thing, accusative] | prishlíte, pozháluysta, [thing] | Пришлите, пожалуйста, договор (prishlíte, pozháluysta, dogovór) = Please send the contract |
| When will [thing] be ready? | Когда будет готов [thing, nominative]? | kogdá búdet gotóv [thing] | Когда будет готов отчёт? (kogdá búdet gotóv otchyót) = When will the report be ready? Feminine noun: готова (gotóva). Neuter: готово (gotóvo). Plural: будут готовы (búdut gotóvy) |
| I will do [thing] by [deadline] | Я сделаю [thing, accusative] до [deadline, genitive] | ya sdélayu [thing] do [deadline] | Я сделаю это до пятницы (ya sdélayu éto do pyátnitsy) = I will do it by Friday |
| I have a question about [topic] | У меня вопрос по [topic, dative] | u menyá voprós po [topic] | У меня вопрос по бюджету (u menyá voprós po byudzhétu) = I have a question about the budget |
| I agree with [person] | Я согласен с [person, instrumental] | ya soglásen s [person] | Я согласен с Анной (ya soglásen s Ánnoy) = I agree with Anna. Feminine speaker: согласна (soglásna) |
| I am writing about [topic] | Пишу по поводу [topic, genitive] | pishú po póvodu [topic] | Пишу по поводу договора (pishú po póvodu dogovóra) = I am writing about the contract |
| I need access to [system] | Мне нужен доступ к [system, dative] | mne núzhen dóstup k [system] | Мне нужен доступ к серверу (mne núzhen dóstup k sérveru) = I need access to the server |
| Who is responsible for [area]? | Кто отвечает за [area, accusative]? | kto otvecháyet za [area] | Кто отвечает за релиз? (kto otvecháyet za relíz) = Who is responsible for the release? |
| A meeting at [time] on [day] | Встреча в [time, accusative] в [day, accusative] | vstrécha v [time] v [day] | Встреча в десять утра в четверг (vstrécha v désyat' utrá v chetvérg) = A meeting at ten in the morning on Thursday |
| I am waiting for [thing] from [person] | Я жду [thing, accusative] от [person, genitive] | ya zhdu [thing] ot [person] | Я жду договор от клиента (ya zhdu dogovór ot kliyénta) = I am waiting for the contract from the client |

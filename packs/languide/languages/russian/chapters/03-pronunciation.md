# Pronunciation Guide

Russian spelling is regular, but two things are not written: the **stress** and the **weakening of unstressed vowels**. This chapter explains both. It also covers the sounds that English does not have. Read every example aloud.

---

## How the Transliteration Works

The transliteration column in this guide follows the Russian spelling, letter by letter. It adds one thing that Russian print leaves out: an acute accent on the stressed vowel.

### Letter Map

| Cyrillic | Latin | Cyrillic | Latin | Cyrillic | Latin |
|----------|-------|----------|-------|----------|-------|
| а | a | л | l | ц | ts |
| б | b | м | m | ч | ch |
| в | v | н | n | ш | sh |
| г | g | о | o | щ | shch |
| д | d | п | p | ъ | not written |
| е | e or ye | р | r | ы | y |
| ё | yo | с | s | ь | ' |
| ж | zh | т | t | э | e |
| з | z | у | u | ю | yu |
| и | i | ф | f | я | ya |
| й | y | х | kh | | |
| к | k | | | | |

### Reading Rules for the Transliteration

- **Stress:** The accented vowel is the stressed one: á é í ó ú ý. A word of one syllable has no accent.
- **The letter е:** It is "ye" at the start of a word, after a vowel, and after ь or ъ. It is "e" after a consonant. The consonant before it is soft.
- **The letter y:** It stands for two letters. After a vowel it is й, as in "boy": музей (muzéy). In other places it is the vowel ы: сыр (syr). The ending "yy" is ый: новый (nóvyy).
- **The apostrophe:** It is the soft sign ь. It makes the consonant before it soft: день (den').
- **The hard sign ъ:** It is not written. The "y" of the next vowel shows the break: подъезд (podyézd).
- **The letter ё:** It is always "yo" and almost always stressed.

## Stress

Every Russian word of two or more syllables has **one** stressed syllable. The stressed vowel is longer and clearer. The other vowels are short and weak.

- Stress can fall on any syllable. No rule predicts it.
- Stress is not marked in normal print. Dictionaries and textbooks mark it.
- Stress can move between forms of one word.
- Wrong stress is the most common reason that people do not understand a learner.

### Stress Changes the Meaning

| Word 1 | Transliteration | Meaning | Word 2 | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-----------------|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| замок | zámok | castle | замок | zamók | lock |
| мука | múka | torment | мука | muká | flour |
| плачу | pláchu | I am crying | плачу | plachú | I am paying |
| уже | úzhe | narrower | уже | uzhé | already |
| стоит | stóit | it costs | стоит | stoít | it stands |
| дома | dóma | at home | дома | domá | houses |
| выходите | vykhódite | you are getting off | выходите | vykhodíte | get out (a command) |

### Stress Moves Inside One Word

| English | Russian | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| water (subject) | вода | vodá | Stress on the ending |
| water (object) | воду | vódu | Stress moves to the stem |
| city | город | górod | Singular |
| cities | города | gorodá | Plural |
| letter | письмо | pis'mó | Singular |
| letters | письма | pís'ma | Plural |
| I understood (man) | понял | pónyal | Masculine |
| I understood (woman) | поняла | ponyalá | Feminine |

### How to Learn Stress

1. Learn every new word together with its stress. Say it aloud three times.
2. Trust the letter ё. It is always stressed.
3. Exaggerate the stressed vowel at first. Make it long.
4. Copy whole phrases from this guide, not single words. Phrases keep the rhythm.

## Vowels

Russian has ten vowel letters but only six vowel sounds. The letters come in pairs. The second letter of each pair makes the consonant before it soft.

### Stressed Vowels

| Letter | Latin | Sound | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-------|-------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| а | a | "a" in "father" | там | tam | there |
| я | ya | "ya" in "yard" | пять | pyat' | five |
| э | e | "e" in "met" | это | éto | this |
| е | e, ye | "ye" in "yes" | нет | net | no |
| ы | y | No English match. See below | сыр | syr | cheese |
| и | i | "ee" in "meet" | три | tri | three |
| о | o | "o" in "more", with no glide | дом | dom | house |
| ё | yo | "yo" in "yonder" | всё | vsyo | everything |
| у | u | "oo" in "boot" | тут | tut | here |
| ю | yu | "you" | юг | yug | south |

### Vowel Reduction

Unstressed vowels lose their full sound. Two rules cover most cases.

- **Unstressed о sounds like "a".** In the syllable just before the stress it is a clear "a". In other syllables it is a weak "uh".
- **Unstressed е and я sound like a short "i".**

| Russian | Transliteration | Sounds like | Meaning |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|---------|
| Москва | Moskvá | maskvá | Moscow |
| окно | oknó | aknó | window |
| спасибо | spasíbo | spasíba | thank you |
| хорошо | khoroshó | kharashó | good, OK |
| молоко | molokó | malakó | milk |
| понедельник | ponedél'nik | panidélnik | Monday |
| метро | metró | mitró | metro |
| телефон | telefón | tilifón | telephone |
| сестра | sestrá | sistrá | sister |
| язык | yazýk | yizýk | language |
| десять | désyat' | désit | ten |
| часы | chasý | chisý | clock, watch. Unstressed а after ч also sounds like "i" |

A full "o" in an unstressed syllable sounds foreign. Say "spasíba", not "spasíbo".

## Hard and Soft Consonants

Most Russian consonants come in two versions: **hard** and **soft**. For a soft consonant, raise the middle of the tongue to the roof of the mouth. It sounds like a consonant and a very short "y" at the same moment.

### How to See Hard and Soft in the Spelling

| The consonant is | When the next letter is | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------------------|-------------------------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Hard | а, э, ы, о, у | мы | my | we |
| Hard | Another consonant or nothing | брат | brat | brother |
| Soft | я, е, и, ё, ю | мир | mir | world, peace |
| Soft | ь | брать | brat' | to take |

### Letters That Never Change

| Type | Letters | Russian | Transliteration | Notes |
|------|---------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| Always hard | ж, ш, ц | машина | mashína | After ж and ш the letter и sounds like ы: "mashýna" |
| Always hard | ж, ш, ц | жить | zhit' | Sounds like "zhyt" |
| Always soft | ч, щ, й | чай | chay | The tongue stays high and forward |

### Hard and Soft Pairs

| Hard | Transliteration | Meaning | Soft | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|-----------------|---------|------|-----------------|---------|
| мат | mat | checkmate | мать | mat' | mother |
| брат | brat | brother | брать | brat' | to take |
| угол | úgol | corner | уголь | úgol' | coal |
| ест | yest | he eats | есть | yest' | to eat, there is |
| стал | stal | became | сталь | stal' | steel |
| лук | luk | onion | люк | lyuk | hatch |
| нос | nos | nose | нёс | nyos | was carrying |
| рад | rad | glad | ряд | ryad | row |
| мал | mal | too small | мял | myal | was crumpling |

## The Soft Sign and the Hard Sign

These two letters have no sound of their own.

| Letter | Name | Job | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|------|-----|---------|-----------------|---------|
| ь | soft sign | Makes the consonant before it soft | день | den' | day |
| ь | soft sign | Makes the consonant before it soft | соль | sol' | salt |
| ь | soft sign | Separates a consonant from "ya", "ye", "yo", "yu" | семья | sem'yá | family |
| ь | soft sign | Separates a consonant from "ya", "ye", "yo", "yu" | пью | p'yu | I drink |
| ъ | hard sign | Keeps a prefix hard and separates it from the next vowel | подъезд | podyézd | entrance to an apartment block |
| ъ | hard sign | Keeps a prefix hard and separates it from the next vowel | объявление | obyavléniye | announcement |
| ъ | hard sign | Changes the word | съел | syel | ate up. Compare сел (sel) = sat down |

The hard sign is rare. The soft sign is everywhere, and it changes meaning. Always pronounce the difference.

## Difficult Sounds

### The Vowel ы

English has no ы. Say "ee", keep the lips relaxed, and pull the whole tongue back. Do not round the lips. The sound is close to the "i" in "bit", but further back in the mouth.

| English | Russian | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| you (casual) | ты | ty | Compare ти in тихо (tíkho) = quietly |
| you (polite) | вы | vy | Very frequent |
| we | мы | my | Very frequent |
| cheese | сыр | syr | Stressed ы |
| market | рынок | rýnok | Stressed ы |
| exit | выход | výkhod | Stressed ы |
| was | был | byl | Compare бил (bil) = was hitting |
| to be | быть | byt' | Compare бить (bit') = to hit |
| mouse | мышка | mýshka | Compare мишка (míshka) = teddy bear |
| to forget | забыть | zabýt' | Compare забить (zabít') = to score a goal |

### The Consonants ш and щ

| Letter | Latin | How to say it | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| ш | sh | Hard. Pull the tongue tip back. A dark "sh" | шесть | shest' | six |
| ш | sh | Hard | наш | nash | our |
| ш | sh | Hard | хорошо | khoroshó | good |
| щ | shch | Soft and long. Tongue flat and forward, lips spread, as in "sheep" held longer | щи | shchi | cabbage soup |
| щ | shch | Soft and long | борщ | borshch | beetroot soup |
| щ | shch | Soft and long | ещё | yeshchyó | still, more |
| щ | shch | Soft and long | площадь | plóshchad' | square |
| щ | shch | Soft and long | помощь | pómoshch' | help |

Modern speakers say щ as one long soft "sh". They do not say "sh" plus "ch". The letters сч together make the same sound: счёт, счастье (schyot, schást'ye).

| ш word | Transliteration | Meaning | щ word | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-----------------|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| прошу | proshú | I ask | прощу | proshchú | I will forgive |
| шутка | shútka | joke | щука | shchúka | pike (fish) |
| чаша | chásha | bowl | чаща | cháshcha | thicket |

### The Consonants ж, ц, ч, х

| Letter | Latin | How to say it | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------------|---------|-----------------|---------|

| ж | zh | Always hard | уже | uzhé | already |

### The Consonant р

The Russian р is a tap or a short roll. The tongue tip strikes the ridge behind the upper teeth. American English has this tap in the middle of "butter" and "ladder". Do not curl the tongue back as in English "r".

| English | Russian | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| work | работа | rabóta | Hard р at the start |
| three | три | tri | Soft р after т |
| rouble | рубль | rubl' | Hard р |
| good, kind | добрый | dóbryy | р inside a cluster |
| to speak | говорить | govorít' | Soft р |
| door | дверь | dver' | Soft р at the end |
| row | ряд | ryad | Soft р. Compare рад (rad) = glad |

### The Consonants л and й

| Letter | Latin | How to say it | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-------|---------------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| л | l | Hard: the dark "l" in "full" | стол | stol | table |
| л | l | Hard | ладно | ládno | all right |
| ль | l' | Soft: the "l" in "million" | соль | sol' | salt |
| ль | l' | Soft | сколько | skól'ko | how much |
| й | y | The "y" in "boy" | мой | moy | my |

## Voicing Rules

### Voiced Consonants Lose Their Voice at the End of a Word

| Spelling | Sounds like | Russian | Transliteration | Sounds like (word) | Meaning |
|----------|-------------|---------|-----------------|--------------------|---------|
| б | p | хлеб | khleb | khlep | bread |
| в | f | любовь | lyubóv' | lyubóf | love |
| г | k | друг | drug | druk | friend |
| д | t | город | górod | górat | city |
| ж | sh | этаж | etázh | etásh | floor, storey |
| з | s | раз | raz | ras | time, once |

### Neighbours Change Each Other

In a group of consonants, the last one decides if the whole group is voiced or voiceless.

| Russian | Transliteration | Sounds like | Meaning |
|---------|-----------------|-------------|---------|
| водка | vódka | vótka | vodka |
| автобус | avtóbus | aftóbus | bus |
| завтра | závtra | záftra | tomorrow |
| вход | vkhod | fkhot | entrance |
| второй | vtoróy | ftaróy | second |
| вокзал | vokzál | vagzál | railway station |
| сделать | sdélat' | zdélat | to do (perfective) |
| в кино | v kinó | fkinó | to the cinema |

The short prepositions в, к and с join the next word. Say в офисе as one word: "vófise". Say с вами as "svámi".

## Consonant Clusters

Russian puts three or four consonants together. Do not add a vowel between them. Build the cluster from the end, then add one consonant at a time.

### Building a Cluster

| Russian | Transliteration | Build it like this | Meaning |
|---------|-----------------|--------------------|---------|
| встреча | vstrécha | re, tre, stre, fstre, fstrécha | meeting |

| взгляд | vzglyad | lyat, glyat, zglyat, vzglyat | glance, view |
| вторник | vtórnik | tor, ftor, ftórnik | Tuesday |
| где | gde | de, gde | where |
| кто | kto | to, kto | who |
| сколько | skól'ko | kol, skol, skólka | how much |

## Intonation

Russian questions often have the same words as statements. Only the voice shows the difference.

| Type | What the voice does | Russian | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|---------------------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Statement | Falls on the last stressed syllable | Это ваш офис. | éto vash ófis | This is your office. |
| Yes or no question | Jumps high on the stressed syllable of the key word, then drops | Это ваш офис? | éto vash ófis | Is this your office? |
| Question with a question word | Starts high on the question word, then falls | Где ваш офис? | gde vash ófis | Where is your office? |
| Unfinished list or clause | Rises a little, then continues | Чай, кофе, вода | chay, kófe, vodá | Tea, coffee, water |

The jump can move. Вы завтра работаете? with the jump on завтра asks "Is it tomorrow that you work?" With the jump on работаете it asks "Do you work tomorrow or not?"

## Practice

Cover the right column. Say the answer aloud, then check.

| Prompt | Answer |
|--------|--------|

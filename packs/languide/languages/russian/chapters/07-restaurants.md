# Restaurants and Dining

This chapter follows a meal from the door to the bill. It also covers coffee shops, canteens and lunch with colleagues. Waiters use вы with guests, and guests use вы with waiters.

---

## Pattern Phrases for Dining

| Pattern | Russian | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| I will have [dish] | Я буду [dish, accusative] | ya búdu [dish] | Я буду борщ (ya búdu borshch) = I will have borscht |
| For me [dish], please | Мне [dish, accusative], пожалуйста | mne [dish], pozháluysta | Мне салат, пожалуйста (mne salát, pozháluysta) = A salad for me, please |
| A table for [number] | Столик на [number, accusative] | stólik na [number] | Столик на двоих (stólik na dvoíkh) = A table for two. For three: на троих (na troíkh). For four: на четверых (na chetverýkh) |
| May I have [thing]? | Можно [thing, accusative]? | mózhno [thing] | Можно меню? (mózhno menyú) = May I have the menu? |
| Bring [thing], please | Принесите, пожалуйста, [thing, accusative] | prinesíte, pozháluysta, [thing] | Принесите, пожалуйста, воду (prinesíte, pozháluysta, vódu) = Bring some water, please |
| Is there [ingredient] in this dish? | В этом блюде есть [ingredient, nominative]? | v étom blyúde yest' [ingredient] | В этом блюде есть орехи? (v étom blyúde yest' orékhi) = Are there nuts in this dish? |
| What is in [dish]? | Что входит в [dish, accusative]? | chto vkhódit v [dish] | Что входит в этот салат? (chto vkhódit v étot salát) = What is in this salad? |
| Without [ingredient], please | Без [ingredient, genitive], пожалуйста | bez [ingredient], pozháluysta | Без лука, пожалуйста (bez lúka, pozháluysta) = Without onion, please |
| [dish] with [thing] | [dish, nominative] с [thing, instrumental] | [dish] s [thing] | Чай с лимоном (chay s limónom) = Tea with lemon |
| One more [thing], please | Ещё один [thing, accusative], пожалуйста | yeshchyó odín [thing], pozháluysta | Ещё один кофе, пожалуйста (yeshchyó odín kófe, pozháluysta) = One more coffee, please. Feminine noun: одну (odnú). Neuter: одно (odnó) |
| [thing] to take away | [thing, accusative] с собой | [thing] s sobóy | Кофе с собой (kófe s sobóy) = A coffee to take away |
| I do not eat [food] | Я не ем [food, accusative] | ya ne yem [food] | Я не ем свинину (ya ne yem svinínu) = I do not eat pork |
| I cannot have [food] | Мне нельзя [food, accusative] | mne nel'zyá [food] | Мне нельзя сахар (mne nel'zyá sákhar) = I cannot have sugar |
| A bottle of [drink] | Бутылку [drink, genitive] | butýlku [drink] | Бутылку воды (butýlku vodý) = A bottle of water |

## Politeness Tiers for Ordering

| English | Russian | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|---------|-----------------|-------|
| Pass the salt (casual) | Передай соль | peredáy sol' | Casual, ты: friends at the table |
| Pass the salt, please (polite) | Передайте, пожалуйста, соль | peredáyte, pozháluysta, sol' | Polite, вы: colleagues, a host |
| A coffee, please (polite) | Кофе, пожалуйста | kófe, pozháluysta | Polite: short orders are normal |
| May I have a coffee? (polite) | Можно кофе? | mózhno kófe | Polite and very common |
| Could you bring the bill? | Не могли бы вы принести счёт? | ne moglí by vy prinestí schyot | Very polite: formal restaurants |
| Would you be so kind, the menu | Будьте добры, меню | búd'te dobrý, menyú | Very polite, slightly old-fashioned |

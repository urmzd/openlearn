# Writing Systems: Tajik Cyrillic and Persian Script

This chapter is the centre of the guide. It teaches both alphabets and the way to move between them.

- **Part A** teaches Tajik Cyrillic: 35 letters, left to right, one sound for each letter.
- **Part B** teaches the Persian script: 32 letters, right to left, short vowels not written.
- **Part C** shows how one word looks in both scripts and how to convert it.

A heritage speaker starts here. You already know the words. You only need to link each letter to a sound that you already say. Plan one to two weeks for Part A and three to four weeks for Part B, at 30 minutes each day.

A learner reads Part A after chapter 1, and Part B after the scenario chapters.

---

## Part A: Tajik Cyrillic

Tajikistan has used the Cyrillic alphabet since 1940. The Tajik alphabet is the Russian alphabet without four letters (ц, щ, ы, ь) and with six added letters (ғ, ӣ, қ, ӯ, ҳ, ҷ). It is written left to right. It has capital and small letters, like English.

Russian loan words and names can also show the letters ц (ts) and ы (i) in older texts. Current Tajik spelling replaces them: ц becomes с or тс, and ы becomes и. The soft sign ь is no longer used.

### The Six Tajik Letters

These six letters do not exist in Russian. Each one is a Russian letter with a small mark.

| Letter | Built from | Mark | Sound | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-----------|------|-------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| ғ | г | A bar through the stem | gh | боғ | باغ | bogh | garden |
| ӣ | и | A straight line above | ī | тоҷикӣ | تاجیکی | tojikī | Tajik (language) |
| қ | к | A small tail at the lower right | q | қанд | قند | qand | sugar cube |
| ӯ | у | A straight line above | ū | дӯст | دوست | dūst | friend |
| ҳ | х | A small tail at the lower right | h | ҳаво | هوا | havo | air, weather |
| ҷ | ч | A small tail at the lower right | j | ҷавоб | جواب | javob | answer |

Points to watch:

- The mark changes the sound. к and қ are two letters. х and ҳ are two letters.
- **ӣ** has a straight line. **й** has a curved mark. They are different letters with different jobs.
- Text typed on a Russian keyboard often drops the marks: "точики" for тоҷикӣ. Expect it in chat messages. Do not copy it.

### The Letters е, ё, ю, я and э

Four letters start with a "y" sound: **ё** (yo), **ю** (yu), **я** (ya), and **е** in some positions.

| Letter | Position | Sound | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|----------|-------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| е | After a consonant | e | дер | دیر | der | late |
| е | After a consonant | e | меҳмон | مهمان | mehmon | guest |
| е | After a vowel | ye | оед | آیید | oyed | come (polite) |
| е | After a vowel | ye | мегӯед | می گویید | megūyed | you say |
| э | At the start of a word | e | Эрон | ایران | Eron | Iran |
| э | At the start of a word | e | эълон | اعلان | e'lon | announcement |
| ё | At the start | yo | ёд | یاد | yod | memory |
| ё | After a consonant | yo | дарё | دریا | daryo | river |
| ё | After a vowel | yo | зиёд | زیاد | ziyod | much, many |
| ю | At the start | yu | юнонӣ | یونانی | yunonī | Greek |
| ю | After a vowel, as "and" | yu | сию як | سی و یک | siyu yak | thirty-one |
| я | At the start | ya | ях | یخ | yakh | ice |
| я | After a vowel | ya | ҷоям | جایم | joyam | my place |
| я | After a consonant | ya | дунявӣ | دنیوی | dunyavī | secular, worldly |

Rules:

- **е** is a plain "e" after a consonant. This is its normal job.
- **е** is "ye" at the start of a word and after a vowel.
- **э** is a plain "e" at the start of a word. Tajik needs it because е at the start would be "ye".
- **ё, ю, я** always contain the "y" sound.

### The Letters и, ӣ and й

These three letters are the hardest part of Cyrillic spelling for a heritage speaker.

| Letter | Job | Where | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-----|-------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| и | The vowel "i" | Anywhere | имрӯз | امروز | imrūz | today |
| и | The vowel "i" | Anywhere | шир | شیر | shir | milk |
| и | The izofat link | End of a word, no stress | китоби ман | کتاب من | kitobi man | my book |
| ӣ | Suffix that makes a noun or adjective | End of a word, with stress | тоҷикӣ | تاجیکی | tojikī | Tajik |
| ӣ | Suffix that makes a noun or adjective | End of a word, with stress | дӯстӣ | دوستی | dūstī | friendship |
| ӣ | Verb ending for ту | End of a verb | меравӣ | می روی | meravī | you go |
| ӣ | Part of the word | End of a word | моҳӣ | ماهی | mohī | fish |
| ӣ | Part of the word | End of a word | чӣ | چه | chī | what |
| й | The consonant "y" | After a vowel | чой | چای | choy | tea |
| й | The consonant "y" | After a vowel | рӯй | روی | rūy | face |
| й | The consonant "y" | After a vowel | май | می | may | May, wine |

### Spelling the Final -ӣ

| Rule | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| ӣ stands only at the end of a word | зиндагӣ | زندگی | zindagī | life |
| Before -ро, ӣ becomes и | тоҷикиро | تاجیکی را | tojikiro | Tajik (as object) |
| Before -ҳо, ӣ becomes и | моҳиҳо | ماهی ها | mohiho | fish (plural) |
| Before the izofat, ӣ becomes и: the result is ии | дӯстии мо | دوستی ما | dūstii mo | our friendship |
| Before -ю ("and"), ӣ becomes и | сию панҷ | سی و پنج | siyu panj | thirty-five |
| After о the suffix stays ӣ | зебоӣ | زیبایی | zeboī | beauty |
| After о the suffix stays ӣ | шиносоӣ | شناسایی | shinosoī | acquaintance |
| After а the verb ending stays ӣ | рафтаӣ | رفته ای | raftaī | you have gone |

A test for the end of a word: say the word. If the final "i" carries the stress, or if it is the ending for ту, write **ӣ**. If it links the word to the next word, write **и**.

### The Sign ъ

| Rule | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| After a vowel, before a consonant | баъд | بعد | ba'd | after |
| After a vowel, before a consonant | маъно | معنا | ma'no | meaning |
| After a vowel, before a consonant | таъмир | تعمیر | ta'mir | repair |
| After a consonant, before a vowel | ҷумъа | جمعه | jum'a | Friday |
| After a consonant, before a vowel | санъат | صنعت | san'at | art, craft |
| At the end of a word | ҷамъ | جمع | jam' | sum, gathering |
| Not written at the start of a word | илм | علم | ilm | science |
| Not written between two vowels | соат | ساعت | soat | hour, clock |

ъ appears only in words of Arabic origin. In Persian script it is the letter ع or the sign hamza.

### Spelling Rules for the Izofat

| Rule | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| After a consonant: add -и | номи шумо | نام شما | nomi shumo | your name |
| After а: add -и | хонаи мо | خانه ی ما | khonai mo | our house |
| After о: add -и | ҳавои гарм | هوای گرم | havoi garm | hot weather |
| After у and ӯ: add -и | орзуи ман | آرزوی من | orzui man | my wish |
| After й: add -и | ҷойи нишаст | جای نشست | joyi nishast | a place to sit |
| After ӣ: ӣ becomes и, then add -и | моҳии калон | ماهی کلان | mohii kalon | a big fish |
| In a chain, every word except the last takes -и | хонаи калони мо | خانه ی کلان ما | khonai kaloni mo | our big house |
| The izofat is always joined to its word | китоби нав | کتاب نو | kitobi nav | a new book |

### Spelling Rules for -ро

| Rule | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| -ро is joined to its word | китобро | کتاب را | kitobro | the book (object) |
| It follows the plural ending | китобҳоро | کتاب ها را | kitobhoro | the books (object) |
| It follows the attached pronoun | китобамро | کتابم را | kitobamro | my book (object) |
| It goes at the end of an izofat chain | китоби навро | کتاب نو را | kitobi navro | the new book (object) |
| ман plus -ро is маро | маро | مرا | maro | me |
| Final ӣ becomes и before -ро | киро | کی را | kiro | whom |

### Spelling Rules for Verbs

| Rule | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| ме- is joined to the verb | мекунам | می کنم | mekunam | I do |
| на- is joined to the verb | намекунам | نمی کنم | namekunam | I do not do |
| A compound verb is two words | кор кардан | کار کردن | kor kardan | to work |
| ме- and на- go on the verb part | кор намекунам | کار نمی کنم | kor namekunam | I do not work |
| A verb with a prefix is one word | баромадан | برآمدن | baromadan | to go out |
| ме- goes in front of the prefix | мебароям | می برآیم | mebaroyam | I go out |
| The perfect is one word with two а | рафтаам | رفته ام | raftaam | I have gone |
| The progressive is two words | хонда истодаам | خوانده ایستاده ام | khonda istodaam | I am reading |
| "Can" is two words | хонда метавонам | خوانده می توانم | khonda metavonam | I can read |

### Other Spelling Rules

| Rule | Tajik | Persian script | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|-------|----------------|-----------------|---------|
| "And" as -у is joined to the first word | падару модар | پدر و مادر | padaru modar | father and mother |
| After a vowel "and" is -ю | бобою бибӣ | بابا و بی بی | boboyu bibī | grandfather and grandmother |
| "And" as ва is a separate word | ман ва ту | من و تو | man va tu | I and you |
| "Here" and "there" are two words | ин ҷо, он ҷо | اینجا، آنجا | in jo, on jo | here, there |
| -тар and -тарин are joined | калонтар, калонтарин | کلانتر، کلانترین | kalontar, kalontarin | bigger, biggest |
| бе- is joined when it makes an adjective | бекор | بیکار | bekor | without work |
| бе is separate as "without" | бе шакар | بی شکر | be shakar | without sugar |
| ҳам- is joined when it makes a noun | ҳамкор | همکار | hamkor | colleague |
| Repeated words take a hyphen | кам-кам | کم کم | kam-kam | little by little |
| A double consonant is written twice | муаллим | معلم | muallim | teacher |

#### Step 5: A Short Paragraph

Read the paragraph aloud. Then check it against the transliteration and the translation.

> Номи ман Нигина аст. Ман дар Лондон зиндагӣ мекунам. Падару модарам аз Тоҷикистон ҳастанд. Мо дар хона бо забони тоҷикӣ гап мезанем. Ман тоҷикиро хуб мефаҳмам, аммо то имрӯз хонда наметавонистам. Ҳоло ҳар рӯз сӣ дақиқа машқ мекунам. Аввал ҳарфҳоро омӯхтам, баъд калимаҳоро хондам. Имрӯз ин матнро бе ёрӣ хондам. Модарам хеле хурсанд шуд.

Transliteration:

> Nomi man Nigina ast. Man dar London zindagī mekunam. Padaru modaram az Tojikiston hastand. Mo dar khona bo zaboni tojikī gap mezanem. Man tojikiro khub mefahmam, ammo to imrūz khonda nametavonistam. Holo har rūz sī daqiqa mashq mekunam. Avval harfhoro omūkhtam, ba'd kalimahoro khondam. Imrūz in matnro be yorī khondam. Modaram khele khursand shud.

Translation:

> My name is Nigina. I live in London. My father and mother are from Tajikistan. At home we speak the Tajik language. I understand Tajik well, but until today I could not read. Now I practise thirty minutes every day. First I learned the letters, then I read the words. Today I read this text without help. My mother was very happy.

## Part B: Persian Script

The Persian script is the Arabic script with four added letters. Iran and Afghanistan use it. Tajik was written in it until the late 1920s, so all older Tajik literature is in this script.

### Five Facts Before You Start

| Fact | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| Right to left | A line starts at the right. A book opens from what English calls the back. |
| No capital letters | Names and sentences start with a normal letter. |
| Letters join | Most letters join to the next letter, in print and in handwriting. |
| Letters change shape | A letter can have up to four shapes: alone, at the start, in the middle, at the end. The core of the letter and its dots stay the same. |
| Short vowels are not written | The reader supplies а, и and у from knowledge of the word. |

Numbers are the exception to the first fact. Digits run left to right inside the right-to-left line.

Seven letters join to the letter before them, but never to the letter after them. After one of these letters there is a small gap inside the word, and the next letter starts again in its "start" shape.

Arabic has no sounds p, ch, zh or g. Persian made four letters by adding dots or a stroke to an Arabic letter.

### How to Guess the Short Vowels

1. **Use your ear.** A heritage speaker reads the consonants and recognises the word. This works for most everyday words.
2. **Try а first.** It is the most frequent short vowel.
3. **Learn the fixed parts.** The prefix می is always ме-. The prefix ن on a verb is на-. The ending م is -ам. The word را is -ро.
4. **Use the word family.** Words from one Arabic root share their consonants: علم, معلم, معلوم, تعلیم are илм, муаллим, маълум, таълим.
5. **Use the sentence.** After a number you expect a noun. At the end of the sentence you expect a verb.
6. **Check in Cyrillic.** A Tajik dictionary shows every vowel.

## Part C: Moving Between the Scripts

### What Is Easy and What Is Hard

| Direction | Easy part | Hard part |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Persian script to Cyrillic | Every consonant has one clear Tajik letter. | You must supply the short vowels, and choose between и and е, and between у and ӯ. A Tajik speaker knows them by ear. |
| Cyrillic to Persian script | Every vowel is given. | You must choose between several letters for з, с, т and ҳ, and remember the silent letters. |

A heritage speaker is strong in the first direction. Reading Persian script aloud is mostly a matter of practice. Writing it needs memory for spelling.

### Rules of Thumb

| Number | Rule | Example |
|--------|------|---------|
| 1 | Tajik о is ا. At the start of a word it is آ. | бозор is بازار. об is آب |
| 2 | Tajik а, и and у inside a word are not written. | падар is پدر. дил is دل. гул is گل |
| 3 | A final а is ه. | хона is خانه |
| 4 | ӯ is و. A long у is و. | рӯз is روز. дур is دور |
| 5 | е and ӣ are ی. A long и is ی. | себ is سیب. моҳӣ is ماهی. шир is شیر |
| 6 | қ is ق and ғ is غ. Always. | қалам is قلم. боғ is باغ |
| 7 | х is خ. Always. ҳ is ه, or ح in Arabic loans. | хона is خانه. ҳафта is هفته. ҳол is حال |
| 8 | The izofat -и is not written. After ا and و write ی. | номи ман is نام من. ҳавои гарм is هوای گرم |
| 9 | -ро is the separate word را. ме- is the separate piece می. | китобро is کتاب را. меравам is می روم |
| 10 | A double consonant is written once. | муаллим is معلم. аввал is اول |
| 11 | ъ is ع in most words. | баъд is بعد. маълум is معلوم |
| 12 | "And" as -у or -ю is the separate letter و. | падару модар is پدر و مادر |
| 13 | Before ҳ and ъ, the vowels е and ӯ are not written. | меҳмон is مهمان. мӯҳлат is مهلت |

### Typing Both Scripts

#### On a Phone

| System | Tajik Cyrillic | Persian script |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| iPhone, iPad | Settings, General, Keyboard, Keyboards, Add New Keyboard, Tajik | Same path, then Persian |
| Android (Gboard) | Gboard settings, Languages, Add keyboard, Tajik | Same path, then Persian |
| Switching | Press the globe key | Press the globe key |

Tips for a phone:

- A Russian keyboard does not have ғ, ӣ, қ, ӯ, ҳ, ҷ on its main keys. On many phones a long press on г, и, к, у, х, ч shows them. A Tajik keyboard has all six as normal keys.
- On the Persian keyboard the digits are Persian digits.

#### On a Computer

| System | Tajik Cyrillic | Persian script |
|--------|----------------|----------------|
| macOS | System Settings, Keyboard, Input Sources, add Tajik (Cyrillic) | Same path, add Persian |
| Windows | Settings, Time and language, Language and region, Add a language, Tajik (Cyrillic) | Same path, add Persian |
| Linux | Keyboard settings, Input Sources, Tajik | Same path, Persian |

Tips for a computer:

- Choose the **Persian** layout, not the Arabic layout. The Arabic layout types different forms of ک and ی, and it lacks پ, چ, ژ and گ on the main keys. Text typed with the wrong forms looks nearly the same, but search does not find it.
- Persian text uses a narrow joining space inside some words, for example between می and the verb. On the standard Persian layout it is Shift plus Space. This guide uses a normal space in its place. Both are readable.
- The cursor moves right to left in Persian text. Digits and Latin words inside the line run left to right.
- To check a spelling, type the word in Cyrillic into a Tajik dictionary, and type the Persian spelling into a Persian dictionary. Automatic converters between the scripts make errors in the letters for з, с, т and ҳ.

# Directions and Navigation

This chapter covers how to ask the way, understand the answer, describe where something is, read an address and arrange to meet someone. Mainland speakers in the north often give directions by compass point. Speakers in the south more often say left and right.

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

| Pattern | Chinese | Pinyin | Example |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| Where is [place]? | [地方]在哪儿？ | [dìfang] zài nǎr? | 地铁站在哪儿？ (Dìtiě zhàn zài nǎr?) = Where is the subway station? |
| How do I get to [place]? | 去[地方]怎么走？ | Qù [dìfang] zěnme zǒu? | 去火车站怎么走？ (Qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu?) = How do I get to the train station? |
| Is there a [place] nearby? | 附近有[地方]吗？ | Fùjìn yǒu [dìfang] ma? | 附近有药店吗？ (Fùjìn yǒu yàodiàn ma?) = Is there a pharmacy nearby? |
| Is [place] far from here? | [地方]离这儿远吗？ | [dìfang] lí zhèr yuǎn ma? | 机场离这儿远吗？ (Jīchǎng lí zhèr yuǎn ma?) = Is the airport far from here? |
| How long does it take to walk to [place]? | 走路到[地方]要多久？ | Zǒulù dào [dìfang] yào duōjiǔ? | 走路到公司要多久？ (Zǒulù dào gōngsī yào duōjiǔ?) = How long is the walk to the office? |
| [place] is [position] of [landmark] | [地方]在[地标]的[方位] | [dìfang] zài [dìbiāo] de [fāngwèi] | 银行在超市的对面 (Yínháng zài chāoshì de duìmiàn) = The bank is opposite the supermarket |
| Go toward [direction] | 往[方向]走 | Wǎng [fāngxiàng] zǒu | 往北走 (Wǎng běi zǒu) = Go north |
| Turn [left or right] at [landmark] | 在[地标][左/右]转 | Zài [dìbiāo] [zuǒ/yòu] zhuǎn | 在红绿灯右转 (Zài hónglǜdēng yòu zhuǎn) = Turn right at the traffic light |
| How do I get from [A] to [B]? | 从[A]到[B]怎么走？ | Cóng [A] dào [B] zěnme zǒu? | 从这儿到机场怎么走？ (Cóng zhèr dào jīchǎng zěnme zǒu?) = How do I get from here to the airport? |
| I am waiting for you at [place] | 我在[地方]等你 | Wǒ zài [dìfang] děng nǐ | 我在门口等你 (Wǒ zài ménkǒu děng nǐ) = I am waiting for you at the door |

- 哪儿 (nǎr) and 这儿 (zhèr) are northern mainland forms. 哪里 (nǎlǐ) and 这里 (zhèlǐ) mean the same. They are the usual forms in southern China and Taiwan.

## Asking for Directions

### Opening Politely

Always open with a polite word before you ask a stranger.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| Excuse me, may I ask | 请问 | Qǐngwèn | Polite. The standard opener |
| Hello, may I ask | 你好，请问一下 | Nǐ hǎo, qǐngwèn yíxià | Polite. Slightly softer |
| Sorry to disturb you | 不好意思，打扰一下 | Bù hǎoyìsi, dǎrǎo yíxià | Very polite |
| I would like to ask the way | 我想问一下路 | Wǒ xiǎng wèn yíxià lù | Polite |
| Sir (to a driver or worker) | 师傅 | Shīfu | Polite. Mainland. Drivers, guards, tradespeople |

## Addresses

A Chinese address runs from the **largest unit to the smallest**: province, city, district, road, number, building, floor, room.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| address | 地址 | dìzhǐ | Standard |
| province | 省 | shěng | 广东省 (Guǎngdōng Shěng) = Guangdong Province |
| city | 市 | shì | 上海市 (Shànghǎi Shì) = Shanghai City |
| district | 区 | qū | 朝阳区 (Cháoyáng Qū) = Chaoyang District |
| road | 路 | lù | 南京西路 (Nánjīng Xī Lù) = Nanjing West Road |
| street | 街 | jiē | Standard |
| number | 号 | hào | 100号 (yìbǎi hào) = number 100 |
| building number | 号楼 | hào lóu | 3号楼 (sān hào lóu) = Building 3. Also 栋 (dòng) |
| floor | 层 | céng | 5层 (wǔ céng) = fifth floor. Also 楼 (lóu) |
| room | 室 | shì | 502室 (wǔ líng èr shì) = Room 502 |
| What is the address? | 地址是什么？ | Dìzhǐ shì shénme? | Standard |
| Please write down the address | 请把地址写下来 | Qǐng bǎ dìzhǐ xiě xiàlai | Polite |
| Can you send me the address? | 能把地址发给我吗？ | Néng bǎ dìzhǐ fā gěi wǒ ma? | Polite. By message |
| Can you send me a location pin? | 可以发个定位给我吗？ | Kěyǐ fā ge dìngwèi gěi wǒ ma? | Mainland. The normal way to share a place on WeChat |

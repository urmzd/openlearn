# Core Communication Phrases

This chapter gives the essential phrases for everyday interactions in Mandarin, organized by communication scenario. Each section includes **pattern phrases** (templates with slots you fill in) and **fixed phrases** (ready-to-use expressions).

Mandarin verbs do not change form. Once you know a pattern, you can swap words into the `[bracket]` slots without changing anything else.

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

These reusable patterns let you express many ideas by swapping words in the slots marked with `[brackets]`.

### Requesting Something

| Pattern | Chinese | Pinyin | Example |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| Please give me [thing] | 请给我[东西] | Qǐng gěi wǒ [dōngxi] | 请给我一杯水 (Qǐng gěi wǒ yì bēi shuǐ) = Please give me a glass of water |
| I want [thing] | 我要[东西] | Wǒ yào [dōngxi] | 我要这个 (Wǒ yào zhège) = I want this one |
| I would like to [action] | 我想[动作] | Wǒ xiǎng [dòngzuò] | 我想喝茶 (Wǒ xiǎng hē chá) = I would like to drink tea |
| Please [action] | 请[动作] | Qǐng [dòngzuò] | 请等一下 (Qǐng děng yíxià) = Please wait a moment |
| Could you help me [action]? | 能帮我[动作]吗？ | Néng bāng wǒ [dòngzuò] ma? | 能帮我拍张照吗？ (Néng bāng wǒ pāi zhāng zhào ma?) = Could you take a photo for me? |
| Could I trouble you to [action] | 麻烦你[动作] | Máfan nǐ [dòngzuò] | 麻烦你再说一遍 (Máfan nǐ zài shuō yí biàn) = Could I trouble you to say it again |
| I need [thing] | 我需要[东西] | Wǒ xūyào [dōngxi] | 我需要帮助 (Wǒ xūyào bāngzhù) = I need help |

### Asking Permission

| Pattern | Chinese | Pinyin | Example |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| Can I [action]? | 我可以[动作]吗？ | Wǒ kěyǐ [dòngzuò] ma? | 我可以坐这儿吗？ (Wǒ kěyǐ zuò zhèr ma?) = Can I sit here? |
| Is it OK to [action] here? | 这里可以[动作]吗？ | Zhèlǐ kěyǐ [dòngzuò] ma? | 这里可以拍照吗？ (Zhèlǐ kěyǐ pāizhào ma?) = Is it OK to take photos here? |
| May I ask, [question]? | 请问，[问题]？ | Qǐngwèn, [wèntí]? | 请问，现在几点？ (Qǐngwèn, xiànzài jǐ diǎn?) = Excuse me, what time is it? |

### Asking Questions

| Pattern | Chinese | Pinyin | Example |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| Where is [place]? | [地方]在哪儿？ | [dìfang] zài nǎr? | 洗手间在哪儿？ (Xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr?) = Where is the restroom? |
| Do you have [thing]? | 有[东西]吗？ | Yǒu [dōngxi] ma? | 有菜单吗？ (Yǒu càidān ma?) = Do you have a menu? |
| How much is [thing]? | [东西]多少钱？ | [dōngxi] duōshao qián? | 这个多少钱？ (Zhège duōshao qián?) = How much is this? |
| What is [thing]? | [东西]是什么？ | [dōngxi] shì shénme? | 这是什么？ (Zhè shì shénme?) = What is this? |
| How do I get to [place]? | 去[地方]怎么走？ | Qù [dìfang] zěnme zǒu? | 去地铁站怎么走？ (Qù dìtiě zhàn zěnme zǒu?) = How do I get to the subway station? |
| What time does [place] [action]? | [地方]几点[动作]？ | [dìfang] jǐ diǎn [dòngzuò]? | 银行几点开门？ (Yínháng jǐ diǎn kāimén?) = What time does the bank open? |
| How do you say [word] in Chinese? | [词]用中文怎么说？ | [cí] yòng Zhōngwén zěnme shuō? | 这个用中文怎么说？ (Zhège yòng Zhōngwén zěnme shuō?) = How do you say this in Chinese? |
| Is [thing] [adjective]? | [东西][形容词]吗？ | [dōngxi] [xíngróngcí] ma? | 这个辣吗？ (Zhège là ma?) = Is this spicy? |

### Stating Preferences and Needs

| Pattern | Chinese | Pinyin | Example |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| I don't eat [food] | 我不吃[食物] | Wǒ bù chī [shíwù] | 我不吃肉 (Wǒ bù chī ròu) = I don't eat meat |
| I'm allergic to [thing] | 我对[东西]过敏 | Wǒ duì [dōngxi] guòmǐn | 我对花生过敏 (Wǒ duì huāshēng guòmǐn) = I'm allergic to peanuts |
| Don't add [thing] | 不要放[东西] | Bú yào fàng [dōngxi] | 不要放辣椒 (Bú yào fàng làjiāo) = Don't add chili |
| I'm looking for [thing] | 我在找[东西] | Wǒ zài zhǎo [dōngxi] | 我在找我的手机 (Wǒ zài zhǎo wǒ de shǒujī) = I'm looking for my phone |
| I like [thing] | 我喜欢[东西] | Wǒ xǐhuan [dōngxi] | 我喜欢中国菜 (Wǒ xǐhuan Zhōngguó cài) = I like Chinese food |
| I want to go to [place] | 我要去[地方] | Wǒ yào qù [dìfang] | 我要去机场 (Wǒ yào qù jīchǎng) = I want to go to the airport |
| I am [identity] | 我是[身份] | Wǒ shì [shēnfèn] | 我是工程师 (Wǒ shì gōngchéngshī) = I am an engineer |
| Too [adjective] | 太[形容词]了 | Tài [xíngróngcí] le | 太贵了 (Tài guì le) = Too expensive |

**Notes on the patterns**

- 哪儿 (nǎr) is the northern mainland form of "where". 哪里 (nǎlǐ) means the same and is the usual form in southern China and Taiwan.
- 要 (yào) is direct. It is normal when you order or buy. 想 (xiǎng) is softer.
- Add 请 (qǐng) at the front or 谢谢 (xièxie) at the end to make any request polite.

## Survival Phrases

### Yes, No and Basic Responses

Mandarin has no single word for "yes" or "no". You repeat the verb of the question, or add 不 (bù) or 没 (méi) before it.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| Yes (it is) | 是 | Shì | Answers a 是 question. Also 是的 (shì de) |
| No (it is not) | 不是 | Bú shì | 不 changes to bú before a fourth tone |
| Right | 对 | Duì | The most common "yes" |
| Not right | 不对 | Bú duì | Correcting |
| OK, good | 好 | Hǎo | Agreement. 好的 (hǎo de) is softer |
| OK, that works | 可以 | Kěyǐ | Giving permission |
| Fine | 行 | Xíng | Casual. Northern speech |
| Not OK | 不行 | Bù xíng | Firm refusal |
| I have, there is | 有 | Yǒu | Answers a 有 question |
| I don't have, there isn't | 没有 | Méiyǒu | The only negative of 有 |
| I want it | 要 | Yào | Answers a 要 question |
| I don't want it | 不要 | Bú yào | Direct |
| No need, thank you | 不用了，谢谢 | Búyòng le, xièxie | Polite way to decline |
| I know | 我知道 | Wǒ zhīdào | Standard |
| I don't know | 我不知道 | Wǒ bù zhīdào | Standard |
| I understand now | 我明白了 | Wǒ míngbai le | Standard |
| I don't understand (what I hear) | 我听不懂 | Wǒ tīng bu dǒng | Very useful |
| I can't read it | 我看不懂 | Wǒ kàn bu dǒng | For written text |
| Maybe | 可能 | Kěnéng | Standard |
| Of course | 当然 | Dāngrán | Standard |
| No problem | 没问题 | Méi wèntí | Standard |
| Wait a moment | 等一下 | Děng yíxià | Casual |
| Please wait a moment | 请稍等 | Qǐng shāo děng | Polite. You will hear this from staff |
| Either is fine | 都可以 | Dōu kěyǐ | When offered a choice |
| I think so | 我觉得是 | Wǒ juéde shì | Tentative yes |
| This one | 这个 | Zhège | Point and say it |
| That one | 那个 | Nàge | Point and say it |
| Here | 这儿 | Zhèr | Also 这里 (zhèlǐ) |
| There | 那儿 | Nàr | Also 那里 (nàlǐ) |

## Politeness Tiers

The same request changes with the listener. The words carry the politeness, because the verb does not change form.

| Tier | Markers | Use with | Example |
|------|---------|----------|---------|
| Casual | 你, short sentences, 吧 | Friends, peers, younger people | 帮我一下 (Bāng wǒ yíxià) = Help me out |
| Polite | 请, 麻烦你, 不好意思, 一下 | Strangers, service staff, colleagues | 请帮我一下 (Qǐng bāng wǒ yíxià) = Please help me |
| Very polite | 您, 麻烦您, 请问, 好吗 | Seniors, clients, elders, formal settings | 麻烦您帮我一下，好吗？ (Máfan nín bāng wǒ yíxià, hǎo ma?) = Could I trouble you to help me? |

- 一下 (yíxià) after a verb makes the request sound small and light.
- 吧 (ba) at the end turns a command into a suggestion.
- A bare verb with no softener, such as 等 (děng, "wait"), sounds like an order.

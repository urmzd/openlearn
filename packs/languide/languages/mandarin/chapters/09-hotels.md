# Hotels and Accommodation

This chapter covers booking, check-in, requests during a stay, problems, check-out and the basic vocabulary for renting a flat. Hotel staff use the respectful 您 (nín) with guests. Polite forms with 请 (qǐng) and 麻烦 (máfan) are the right level for your replies.

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

| Pattern | Chinese | Pinyin | Example |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| I would like a [room type] | 我想要一间[房型] | Wǒ xiǎng yào yì jiān [fángxíng] | 我想要一间大床房 (Wǒ xiǎng yào yì jiān dàchuángfáng) = I would like a room with one large bed |
| I am staying [number] nights | 我住[数字]晚 | Wǒ zhù [shùzì] wǎn | 我住三晚 (Wǒ zhù sān wǎn) = I am staying three nights |
| From the [date] to the [date] | 从[日期]到[日期] | Cóng [rìqī] dào [rìqī] | 从十号到十三号 (Cóng shí hào dào shísān hào) = From the 10th to the 13th |
| Does it include [thing]? | 包括[东西]吗？ | Bāokuò [dōngxi] ma? | 包括早餐吗？ (Bāokuò zǎocān ma?) = Does it include breakfast? |
| The [thing] is broken | [东西]坏了 | [dōngxi] huài le | 空调坏了 (Kōngtiáo huài le) = The air conditioning is broken |
| There is no [thing] in the room | 房间里没有[东西] | Fángjiān li méiyǒu [dōngxi] | 房间里没有毛巾 (Fángjiān li méiyǒu máojīn) = There are no towels in the room |
| Could you give me another [thing]? | 能再给我[数量][东西]吗？ | Néng zài gěi wǒ [shùliàng] [dōngxi] ma? | 能再给我一条毛巾吗？ (Néng zài gěi wǒ yì tiáo máojīn ma?) = Could you give me another towel? |
| What time is [service]? | [服务]是几点？ | [fúwù] shì jǐ diǎn? | 早餐是几点？ (Zǎocān shì jǐ diǎn?) = What time is breakfast? |
| Is there a [facility]? | 有[设施]吗？ | Yǒu [shèshī] ma? | 有健身房吗？ (Yǒu jiànshēnfáng ma?) = Is there a gym? |
| Can I [action] a little [earlier or later]? | 可以[早/晚]一点儿[动作]吗？ | Kěyǐ [zǎo/wǎn] yìdiǎnr [dòngzuò] ma? | 可以晚一点儿退房吗？ (Kěyǐ wǎn yìdiǎnr tuìfáng ma?) = Can I check out a little later? |
| Could you help me [action]? | 可以帮我[动作]吗？ | Kěyǐ bāng wǒ [dòngzuò] ma? | 可以帮我叫一辆出租车吗？ (Kěyǐ bāng wǒ jiào yí liàng chūzūchē ma?) = Could you call a taxi for me? |

## Politeness at the Front Desk

| Tier | Chinese | Pinyin | Use with |
|------|---------|--------|----------|
| Casual | 给我一条毛巾 | Gěi wǒ yì tiáo máojīn | Too blunt for a hotel. Avoid it |
| Polite | 请给我一条毛巾 | Qǐng gěi wǒ yì tiáo máojīn | The normal level for a guest |
| Very polite | 麻烦您再给我一条毛巾，谢谢 | Máfan nín zài gěi wǒ yì tiáo máojīn, xièxie | When you ask for a favor or complain |

- Start a complaint with 不好意思 (bù hǎoyìsi). The problem is then fixed faster and nobody loses face.
- Room numbers are read digit by digit. Room 1208 is yāo èr líng bā.

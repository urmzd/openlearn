# Language Foundations: Quick Reference

Mandarin grammar is simple in form. Verbs never change. Nouns have no gender and no plural ending. Meaning comes from **word order**, **particles** and **time words**. This chapter gives the rules a beginner needs to build correct sentences.

---

## Sentence Structure

### Basic Word Order

Mandarin follows **Subject, Verb, Object** order, the same as English. The large difference is that **time and place come before the verb**.

**Subject + Time + Place + Verb + Object**

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I drink tea | 我喝茶 | Wǒ hē chá | Subject, verb, object |
| I am going to Beijing tomorrow | 我明天去北京 | Wǒ míngtiān qù Běijīng | Time before the verb |
| I eat lunch at the office | 我在公司吃午饭 | Wǒ zài gōngsī chī wǔfàn | Place before the verb |
| I work at home tomorrow | 我明天在家工作 | Wǒ míngtiān zài jiā gōngzuò | Time, then place, then verb |
| He starts work at eight every morning | 他每天早上八点上班 | Tā měitiān zǎoshang bā diǎn shàngbān | Time goes from large unit to small unit |
| Tomorrow I am going to Beijing | 明天我去北京 | Míngtiān wǒ qù Běijīng | Time can also come first |
| This one I don't want | 这个我不要 | Zhège wǒ bú yào | Topic first. Very common in speech |

- Never put the time at the end of the sentence. "I go to Beijing tomorrow" in English order is wrong in Mandarin.
- Large units come before small units: year, month, day, hour. Country, city, street, number.

### No Conjugation

The verb is the same for every person, number and time.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I go | 我去 | Wǒ qù | Same verb form |
| You go | 你去 | Nǐ qù | Same verb form |
| She goes | 她去 | Tā qù | Same verb form |
| We go | 我们去 | Wǒmen qù | Same verb form |
| I went yesterday | 我昨天去了 | Wǒ zuótiān qù le | Time word plus 了 |
| I will go tomorrow | 我明天去 | Wǒ míngtiān qù | Time word only |

### Pronouns

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I, me | 我 | wǒ | No difference between subject and object |
| you | 你 | nǐ | Casual and standard |
| you (respectful) | 您 | nín | Very polite. Seniors, clients, elders |
| he, him | 他 | tā | Same sound as 她 and 它 |
| she, her | 她 | tā | Different character only |
| it | 它 | tā | Often left out |
| we, us | 我们 | wǒmen | 们 (men) makes pronouns plural |
| we (including you) | 咱们 | zánmen | Casual. Northern speech |
| you (plural) | 你们 | nǐmen | 您们 is not standard. Use 各位 (gèwèi) to be formal |
| they, them | 他们 | tāmen | 她们 for a group of women |
| everyone | 大家 | dàjiā | Standard |
| oneself | 自己 | zìjǐ | 我自己 (wǒ zìjǐ) = myself |

Speakers drop pronouns when the meaning is clear. 去吗？ (Qù ma?) can mean "Are you going?".

## The Three Core Verbs: 是, 有, 在

### 是 (shì): To Be, for Nouns

是 links two nouns. It does **not** link a noun to an adjective.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I am a teacher | 我是老师 | Wǒ shì lǎoshī | Noun 是 noun |
| This is my colleague | 这是我同事 | Zhè shì wǒ tóngshì | Noun 是 noun |
| He is not the manager | 他不是经理 | Tā bú shì jīnglǐ | Negative: 不是 (bú shì) |
| Are you Mr. Wang? | 你是王先生吗？ | Nǐ shì Wáng xiānsheng ma? | Question with 吗 |
| I am busy | 我很忙 | Wǒ hěn máng | No 是 before an adjective. Use 很 |
| This is expensive | 这个很贵 | Zhège hěn guì | 很 (hěn) here is a link, not a strong "very" |
| This is not expensive | 这个不贵 | Zhège bú guì | 不 replaces 很 |

### 有 (yǒu): To Have, There Is

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I have a question | 我有一个问题 | Wǒ yǒu yí ge wèntí | Possession |
| I don't have time | 我没有时间 | Wǒ méiyǒu shíjiān | Negative is always 没有, never 不有 |
| Is there a restroom here? | 这里有洗手间吗？ | Zhèlǐ yǒu xǐshǒujiān ma? | Place + 有 = "there is" |
| There is a bank nearby | 附近有一家银行 | Fùjìn yǒu yì jiā yínháng | Place + 有 + thing |
| Do you have it or not? | 有没有？ | Yǒu méiyǒu? | Positive plus negative makes a question |

### 在 (zài): To Be At, In, On

在 has three jobs: a verb of location, a preposition, and a marker for an action in progress.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I am at home | 我在家 | Wǒ zài jiā | Verb of location |
| He is not here | 他不在 | Tā bú zài | Negative: 不在 (bú zài) |
| Where is the station? | 车站在哪儿？ | Chēzhàn zài nǎr? | Thing + 在 + place |
| I work in Shanghai | 我在上海工作 | Wǒ zài Shànghǎi gōngzuò | Preposition. Place before the verb |
| I am eating | 我在吃饭 | Wǒ zài chīfàn | 在 + verb = action in progress |
| He is in a meeting right now | 他正在开会 | Tā zhèngzài kāihuì | 正在 is a stronger "right now" |

### Choosing Between Them

| You want to say | Use | Example |
|-----------------|-----|---------|
| A is B (noun) | 是 | 他是医生 (Tā shì yīshēng) = He is a doctor |
| A is (adjective) | 很 | 他很高 (Tā hěn gāo) = He is tall |
| A is at a place | 在 | 他在医院 (Tā zài yīyuàn) = He is at the hospital |
| A has B | 有 | 他有车 (Tā yǒu chē) = He has a car |
| There is a B at a place | 有 | 医院里有药店 (Yīyuàn li yǒu yàodiàn) = There is a pharmacy in the hospital |

## The Particle 的 (de)

的 joins a describing word or phrase to the noun after it. The describing part always comes **first**.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| my phone | 我的手机 | wǒ de shǒujī | Possession: owner + 的 + thing |
| the company's address | 公司的地址 | gōngsī de dìzhǐ | Possession |
| a very good teacher | 很好的老师 | hěn hǎo de lǎoshī | Adjective phrase + 的 + noun |
| the book that I bought | 我买的书 | wǒ mǎi de shū | Clause + 的 + noun. No word for "that" |
| the person who speaks English | 会说英语的人 | huì shuō Yīngyǔ de rén | Clause + 的 + noun |
| my mother | 我妈妈 | wǒ māma | 的 is dropped for close family and one's own company |
| the red one | 红的 | hóng de | Noun dropped: "the red one" |
| It is mine | 是我的 | Shì wǒ de | Noun dropped |
| I came yesterday | 我是昨天来的 | Wǒ shì zuótiān lái de | 是...的 stresses when, where or how a past action happened |

Two other particles sound the same but have different characters and jobs:

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| You speak very well | 你说得很好 | Nǐ shuō de hěn hǎo | 得 after a verb describes how the action is done |
| Walk slowly | 慢慢地走 | Mànmàn de zǒu | 地 after an adverb, before a verb |

## The Particle 了 (le)

了 does **not** mean past tense. It marks a **completed action** or a **change of situation**.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I have eaten | 我吃了 | Wǒ chī le | Completed action |
| I bought two books | 我买了两本书 | Wǒ mǎi le liǎng běn shū | 了 after the verb, before a counted object |
| It is raining now | 下雨了 | Xià yǔ le | Change: it was not raining before |
| I am hungry now | 我饿了 | Wǒ è le | Change of state |
| I understand now | 我明白了 | Wǒ míngbai le | Change of state |
| I am leaving | 我走了 | Wǒ zǒu le | A change that is about to happen |
| I have studied for two years so far | 我学了两年了 | Wǒ xué le liǎng nián le | Two 了: the action continues to now |
| Too expensive | 太贵了 | Tài guì le | 太...了 is a fixed pair |
| I did not eat | 我没吃 | Wǒ méi chī | Negative past: 没, and no 了 |
| I used to live in Beijing | 我以前住在北京 | Wǒ yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng | A past habit or state takes no 了 |

## Questions

### Question Particles: 吗, 呢, 吧

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| Are you a teacher? | 你是老师吗？ | Nǐ shì lǎoshī ma? | 吗 turns a statement into a yes or no question |
| Are you busy? | 你忙吗？ | Nǐ máng ma? | No change in word order |
| I am fine. And you? | 我很好，你呢？ | Wǒ hěn hǎo, nǐ ne? | 呢 returns the same question |
| Where is my phone? | 我的手机呢？ | Wǒ de shǒujī ne? | Noun + 呢 = "where is it?" |
| You are Mr. Wang, right? | 你是王先生吧？ | Nǐ shì Wáng xiānsheng ba? | 吧 asks for confirmation |
| Let's go | 我们走吧 | Wǒmen zǒu ba | 吧 makes a suggestion |
| All right then | 好吧 | Hǎo ba | 吧 shows reluctant agreement |

### Verb-Not-Verb Questions

Put the positive and negative forms together. Do not add 吗.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| Are you going or not? | 你去不去？ | Nǐ qù bu qù? | 不 is neutral in the middle |
| Is it or isn't it? | 是不是？ | Shì bu shì? | Also used as a tag: "right?" |
| Is that OK? | 好不好？ | Hǎo bu hǎo? | Tag after a suggestion |
| Can I or not? | 可不可以？ | Kě bu kěyǐ? | Only the first syllable repeats |
| Do you have any? | 有没有？ | Yǒu méiyǒu? | 有 uses 没 |

### Answering Yes and No

Repeat the verb for "yes". Put 不 or 没 before it for "no".

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| Are you going? Yes. | 你去吗？去。 | Nǐ qù ma? Qù. | Repeat the verb |
| Are you going? No. | 你去吗？不去。 | Nǐ qù ma? Bú qù. | 不 + verb |
| Do you have it? No. | 有吗？没有。 | Yǒu ma? Méiyǒu. | 没有 for 有 |
| Is it expensive? No. | 贵吗？不贵。 | Guì ma? Bú guì. | 不 + adjective |

### Question Words

The question word stays in the same position as the answer. The word order does not change.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| what | 什么 | shénme | 你叫什么？ (Nǐ jiào shénme?) = What are you called? |
| who | 谁 | shéi | 他是谁？ (Tā shì shéi?) = Who is he? |
| where | 哪儿 | nǎr | 你去哪儿？ (Nǐ qù nǎr?) = Where are you going? Also 哪里 (nǎlǐ) |
| when | 什么时候 | shénme shíhou | 你什么时候来？ (Nǐ shénme shíhou lái?) = When are you coming? |
| what time | 几点 | jǐ diǎn | 几点开会？ (Jǐ diǎn kāihuì?) = What time is the meeting? |
| why | 为什么 | wèi shénme | 为什么不行？ (Wèi shénme bù xíng?) = Why not? |
| how (method) | 怎么 | zěnme | 怎么去？ (Zěnme qù?) = How do I get there? |
| how is it | 怎么样 | zěnmeyàng | 你觉得怎么样？ (Nǐ juéde zěnmeyàng?) = What do you think? |
| how much, how many | 多少 | duōshao | 多少钱？ (Duōshao qián?) = How much money? |
| how many (under ten) | 几 | jǐ | 几个人？ (Jǐ ge rén?) = How many people? Needs a measure word |
| which | 哪 | nǎ | 哪个？ (Nǎge?) = Which one? Needs a measure word |
| how long (time) | 多久 | duōjiǔ | 要多久？ (Yào duōjiǔ?) = How long does it take? |
| how far | 多远 | duō yuǎn | 多 + adjective asks for degree |
| whose | 谁的 | shéi de | 这是谁的？ (Zhè shì shéi de?) = Whose is this? |

## Measure Words

A number cannot stand directly before a noun. A **measure word** goes between them, as in English "two cups of tea" or "a sheet of paper". In Mandarin every noun needs one.

**Number + Measure Word + Noun**

The same rule applies after 这 (zhè, this), 那 (nà, that), 哪 (nǎ, which) and 几 (jǐ, how many).

### The 20 Most Useful Measure Words

| Measure word | Pinyin | Used for | Example |
|--------------|--------|----------|---------|
| 个 | gè | People and most things. The general measure word | 一个人 (yí ge rén) = one person |
| 位 | wèi | People, respectfully | 两位客人 (liǎng wèi kèrén) = two guests |
| 本 | běn | Books, notebooks | 三本书 (sān běn shū) = three books |
| 张 | zhāng | Flat things: tickets, paper, tables, cards | 一张票 (yì zhāng piào) = one ticket |
| 杯 | bēi | Cups and glasses of drink | 一杯咖啡 (yì bēi kāfēi) = a cup of coffee |
| 瓶 | píng | Bottles | 两瓶水 (liǎng píng shuǐ) = two bottles of water |
| 碗 | wǎn | Bowls of food | 一碗米饭 (yì wǎn mǐfàn) = a bowl of rice |
| 件 | jiàn | Clothes (upper body), matters, luggage | 一件衣服 (yí jiàn yīfu) = one piece of clothing |
| 条 | tiáo | Long narrow things: trousers, roads, fish, messages | 一条裤子 (yì tiáo kùzi) = a pair of trousers |
| 只 | zhī | Small animals, one of a pair | 一只猫 (yì zhī māo) = one cat |
| 辆 | liàng | Vehicles with wheels | 一辆车 (yí liàng chē) = one car |
| 双 | shuāng | Pairs: shoes, chopsticks, socks | 一双鞋 (yì shuāng xié) = a pair of shoes |
| 份 | fèn | Portions, copies of documents, jobs | 一份文件 (yí fèn wénjiàn) = one document |
| 块 | kuài | Pieces and lumps. Also the spoken unit of money | 十块钱 (shí kuài qián) = ten yuan |
| 次 | cì | Number of times | 去过一次 (qù guo yí cì) = have been once |
| 家 | jiā | Companies, shops, restaurants | 一家公司 (yì jiā gōngsī) = one company |
| 台 | tái | Machines: computers, televisions | 一台电脑 (yì tái diànnǎo) = one computer |
| 间 | jiān | Rooms | 一间会议室 (yì jiān huìyìshì) = one meeting room |
| 种 | zhǒng | Kinds, types | 两种方法 (liǎng zhǒng fāngfǎ) = two methods |
| 把 | bǎ | Things with a handle: umbrellas, chairs, keys | 一把伞 (yì bǎ sǎn) = one umbrella |

### Rules for Measure Words

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| this book | 这本书 | zhè běn shū | 这 + measure word + noun |
| that person | 那个人 | nàge rén | 那 + measure word + noun |
| which one? | 哪个？ | Nǎge? | 哪 + measure word |
| how many people? | 几个人？ | Jǐ ge rén? | 几 + measure word |
| two people | 两个人 | liǎng ge rén | Use 两 (liǎng), not 二 (èr), before a measure word |
| the second one | 第二个 | dì èr ge | Use 二 for order and for counting |
| some things | 一些东西 | yìxiē dōngxi | 些 (xiē) means "some" and replaces the measure word |

If you forget the right measure word, use 个 (ge). It is sometimes wrong, but people understand it.

## Negation: 不 and 没

| Word | Negates | Do not use with |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| 不 (bù) | Present, future, habits, wishes, adjectives, 是 | 有 |
| 没 (méi) | Completed or past actions, experience, 有 | 是, adjectives |

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I don't drink alcohol | 我不喝酒 | Wǒ bù hē jiǔ | Habit: 不 |
| I am not going tomorrow | 我明天不去 | Wǒ míngtiān bú qù | Future: 不 |
| It is not expensive | 不贵 | Bú guì | Adjective: 不 |
| I can't speak Japanese | 我不会说日语 | Wǒ bú huì shuō Rìyǔ | Ability: 不 |
| I did not go | 我没去 | Wǒ méi qù | Past action: 没 |
| I have not eaten yet | 我还没吃 | Wǒ hái méi chī | 还没 = not yet |
| I have never been there | 我没去过 | Wǒ méi qù guo | Experience: 没 + verb + 过 |
| I don't have time | 我没有时间 | Wǒ méiyǒu shíjiān | 有: always 没 |
| Don't worry | 别担心 | Bié dānxīn | 别 (bié) = "don't" in commands |
| Don't go | 不要去 | Bú yào qù | 不要 also means "don't" |
| Not very good | 不太好 | Bú tài hǎo | 不太 softens a negative |

## Time and Aspect

Mandarin has no tenses. A **time word** places the action in time. An **aspect particle** shows whether the action is complete, in progress or experienced.

### Time Words

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| today | 今天 | jīntiān | Before the verb |
| yesterday | 昨天 | zuótiān | Before the verb |
| tomorrow | 明天 | míngtiān | Before the verb |
| now | 现在 | xiànzài | Standard |
| just now | 刚才 | gāngcái | A moment ago |
| before, in the past | 以前 | yǐqián | Also after a noun: 开会以前 (kāihuì yǐqián) = before the meeting |
| after, in the future | 以后 | yǐhòu | Also after a noun: 下班以后 (xiàbān yǐhòu) = after work |
| already | 已经 | yǐjīng | Usually with 了 |
| right away | 马上 | mǎshàng | Standard |
| last week | 上个星期 | shàng ge xīngqī | 上 = previous |
| next month | 下个月 | xià ge yuè | 下 = next |
| last year | 去年 | qùnián | Not 上年 |
| this year | 今年 | jīnnián | Standard |
| next year | 明年 | míngnián | Not 下年 |
| often | 经常 | jīngcháng | Before the verb |
| sometimes | 有时候 | yǒu shíhou | Before the verb |

### Aspect Markers

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I ate | 我吃了 | Wǒ chī le | 了: the action is complete |
| I have been to Shanghai | 我去过上海 | Wǒ qù guo Shànghǎi | 过: experience at some time in the past |
| I am writing an email | 我在写邮件 | Wǒ zài xiě yóujiàn | 在: action in progress |
| The door is open | 门开着 | Mén kāi zhe | 着: a state that continues |
| I am about to leave | 我要走了 | Wǒ yào zǒu le | 要...了: about to happen |
| We are almost there | 快到了 | Kuài dào le | 快...了: soon |
| It will rain tomorrow | 明天会下雨 | Míngtiān huì xià yǔ | 会: a prediction |
| I am going to buy a phone | 我要买手机 | Wǒ yào mǎi shǒujī | 要: a plan or intention |

## Modal Verbs

Modal verbs go before the main verb. Their negative uses 不.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| I would like to go | 我想去 | Wǒ xiǎng qù | 想: wish, soft |
| I want to go, I am going to go | 我要去 | Wǒ yào qù | 要: firm wish or plan |
| I can speak Chinese | 我会说中文 | Wǒ huì shuō Zhōngwén | 会: a learned skill |
| I can come tomorrow | 我明天能来 | Wǒ míngtiān néng lái | 能: ability or possibility in the situation |
| You may sit here | 你可以坐这儿 | Nǐ kěyǐ zuò zhèr | 可以: permission |
| I have to go | 我得走了 | Wǒ děi zǒu le | 得 is read děi here: must |
| You should rest | 你应该休息 | Nǐ yīnggāi xiūxi | 应该: should |
| I need to think about it | 我需要考虑一下 | Wǒ xūyào kǎolǜ yíxià | 需要: need |
| No need to queue | 不用排队 | Búyòng páiduì | 不用: the negative of 得 and 需要 |

## Comparisons with 比 (bǐ)

**A + 比 + B + Adjective** means "A is more (adjective) than B". Do not add 很 before the adjective.

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| Today is colder than yesterday | 今天比昨天冷 | Jīntiān bǐ zuótiān lěng | A 比 B + adjective |
| This one is more expensive than that one | 这个比那个贵 | Zhège bǐ nàge guì | No 很 |
| This one is a little more expensive | 这个比那个贵一点儿 | Zhège bǐ nàge guì yìdiǎnr | Add 一点儿 after the adjective |
| This one is much more expensive | 这个比那个贵得多 | Zhège bǐ nàge guì de duō | Add 得多 after the adjective |
| This one is 50 yuan more expensive | 这个比那个贵五十块 | Zhège bǐ nàge guì wǔshí kuài | Add the amount after the adjective |
| This one is even better | 这个更好 | Zhège gèng hǎo | 更 = even more |
| This one is not as good as that one | 这个没有那个好 | Zhège méiyǒu nàge hǎo | Negative comparison uses 没有 |
| This one is the same as that one | 这个跟那个一样 | Zhège gēn nàge yíyàng | A 跟 B 一样 |
| This one is as big as that one | 这个跟那个一样大 | Zhège gēn nàge yíyàng dà | Add the adjective after 一样 |
| This one is the best | 这个最好 | Zhège zuì hǎo | 最 = most |
| A bit cheaper, please | 便宜一点儿 | Piányi yìdiǎnr | Adjective + 一点儿 = "a bit more" |

## Linking Words

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| and (joins nouns) | 和 | hé | 我和你 (wǒ hé nǐ) = you and I. Does not join sentences |
| also | 也 | yě | Before the verb: 我也去 (Wǒ yě qù) = I am going too |
| all, both | 都 | dōu | Before the verb: 我们都去 (Wǒmen dōu qù) = We are all going |
| but | 但是 | dànshì | Also 可是 (kěshì), 不过 (búguò) |
| because, so | 因为，所以 | yīnwèi, suǒyǐ | Often used as a pair |
| if, then | 如果，就 | rúguǒ, jiù | 就 goes before the verb in the second half |
| or (in questions) | 还是 | háishi | 茶还是咖啡？ (Chá háishi kāfēi?) = Tea or coffee? |
| or (in statements) | 或者 | huòzhě | 今天或者明天 (jīntiān huòzhě míngtiān) = today or tomorrow |
| then, after that | 然后 | ránhòu | Sequence |
| first, then | 先，再 | xiān, zài | 先吃饭，再开会 (Xiān chīfàn, zài kāihuì) = Eat first, then meet |
| although | 虽然 | suīrán | Pair with 但是 in the second half |
| with, and | 跟 | gēn | 跟我来 (Gēn wǒ lái) = Come with me |

## Sentence Pattern Templates

| Pattern | Chinese | Pinyin | Example |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| [A] is [noun] | [A]是[名词] | [A] shì [míngcí] | 我是学生 (Wǒ shì xuésheng) = I am a student |
| [A] is [adjective] | [A]很[形容词] | [A] hěn [xíngróngcí] | 今天很热 (Jīntiān hěn rè) = Today is hot |
| [A] is at [place] | [A]在[地方] | [A] zài [dìfang] | 我在公司 (Wǒ zài gōngsī) = I am at the office |
| [A] does [action] at [place] | [A]在[地方][动作] | [A] zài [dìfang] [dòngzuò] | 我在家吃饭 (Wǒ zài jiā chīfàn) = I eat at home |
| [A] has done [action] before | [A][动作]过 | [A] [dòngzuò] guo | 我吃过火锅 (Wǒ chī guo huǒguō) = I have eaten hotpot |
| [A] is more [adjective] than [B] | [A]比[B][形容词] | [A] bǐ [B] [xíngróngcí] | 地铁比公交车快 (Dìtiě bǐ gōngjiāochē kuài) = The subway is faster than the bus |
| Because [reason], so [result] | 因为[原因]，所以[结果] | Yīnwèi [yuányīn], suǒyǐ [jiéguǒ] | 因为下雨，所以我不去 (Yīnwèi xià yǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ bú qù) = Because it is raining, I am not going |
| If [condition], then [result] | 如果[条件]，就[结果] | Rúguǒ [tiáojiàn], jiù [jiéguǒ] | 如果有时间，我就去 (Rúguǒ yǒu shíjiān, wǒ jiù qù) = If I have time, I will go |
| First [action], then [action] | 先[动作]，再[动作] | Xiān [dòngzuò], zài [dòngzuò] | 先付钱，再拿票 (Xiān fù qián, zài ná piào) = Pay first, then take the ticket |
| [A] or [B]? | [A]还是[B]？ | [A] háishi [B]? | 今天还是明天？ (Jīntiān háishi míngtiān?) = Today or tomorrow? |

# Shopping

This chapter covers browsing, sizes and colors, prices, mobile payment, returns, delivery and everyday items. Prices are fixed in malls, supermarkets and chain stores. Bargaining is normal only at markets and small independent stalls.

## Pattern Phrase Templates

## Prices and Bargaining

| English | Chinese | Pinyin | Notes |
|---------|---------|--------|-------|
| It is too expensive | 太贵了 | Tài guì le | The standard first move |
| Can it be a little cheaper? | 可以便宜一点儿吗？ | Kěyǐ piányi yìdiǎnr ma? | Polite |
| What is your lowest price? | 最低多少钱？ | Zuì dī duōshao qián? | At markets |
| Is there a discount? | 能打折吗？ | Néng dǎzhé ma? | Standard |
| What is the discount? | 打几折？ | Dǎ jǐ zhé? | The answer is a number. See the note below |
| Is there any special offer? | 有优惠吗？ | Yǒu yōuhuì ma? | Standard |
| Is it cheaper if I buy two? | 买两个能便宜吗？ | Mǎi liǎng ge néng piányi ma? | Standard |
| Is 100 yuan OK? | 一百块行吗？ | Yìbǎi kuài xíng ma? | Make an offer |
| Then I don't want it | 那我不要了 | Nà wǒ bú yào le | Walking away is part of bargaining |
| I will look around some more | 我再看看 | Wǒ zài kànkan | Polite way to leave |
| I will think about it | 我再考虑一下 | Wǒ zài kǎolǜ yíxià | Polite way to leave |
| OK, I will take it | 好，我要了 | Hǎo, wǒ yào le | Agreeing to the price |
| Do you have a membership card? | 有会员卡吗？ | Yǒu huìyuánkǎ ma? | You will hear this at the till |
| No, I don't | 没有 | Méiyǒu | Reply |

**How discounts are stated.** A discount names the part you **pay**, in tenths. 八折 (bā zhé) means you pay 80 percent, so the discount is 20 percent. 五折 (wǔ zhé) is half price. 九五折 (jiǔ wǔ zhé) means you pay 95 percent.

## Politeness in Shops

| Tier | Chinese | Pinyin | Use with |
|------|---------|--------|----------|
| Casual | 这个多少钱？ | Zhège duōshao qián? | Markets, small shops |
| Polite | 你好，请问这个多少钱？ | Nǐ hǎo, qǐngwèn zhège duōshao qián? | Any shop |
| Very polite | 您好，麻烦您帮我看一下这个的价格 | Nín hǎo, máfan nín bāng wǒ kàn yíxià zhège de jiàgé | Formal stores, older staff |

- Call a shop owner 老板 (lǎobǎn, "boss"). It is friendly and normal at stalls and small shops.

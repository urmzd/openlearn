# Transportation

This chapter covers the subway, buses, taxis and ride-hailing, trains, flights and the daily commute. In mainland cities, most tickets and fares are paid by phone. Drivers are addressed as 师傅 (shīfu).

---

## Pattern Phrase Templates

| Pattern | Chinese | Pinyin | Example |
|---------|---------|--------|---------|
| I want to go to [place] | 我要去[地方] | Wǒ yào qù [dìfang] | 我要去机场 (Wǒ yào qù jīchǎng) = I want to go to the airport |
| Driver, to [place] please | 师傅，去[地方] | Shīfu, qù [dìfang] | 师傅，去火车站 (Shīfu, qù huǒchēzhàn) = Driver, to the train station |
| One ticket to [place] | 一张去[地方]的票 | Yì zhāng qù [dìfang] de piào | 一张去上海的票 (Yì zhāng qù Shànghǎi de piào) = One ticket to Shanghai |
| Does this go to [place]? | 这趟车到[地方]吗？ | Zhè tàng chē dào [dìfang] ma? | 这趟车到西站吗？ (Zhè tàng chē dào xī zhàn ma?) = Does this one go to the west station? |
| Which line goes to [place]? | 去[地方]坐几号线？ | Qù [dìfang] zuò jǐ hào xiàn? | 去机场坐几号线？ (Qù jīchǎng zuò jǐ hào xiàn?) = Which line goes to the airport? |
| Where do I transfer for [place]? | 去[地方]在哪儿换乘？ | Qù [dìfang] zài nǎr huànchéng? | 去南站在哪儿换乘？ (Qù nán zhàn zài nǎr huànchéng?) = Where do I transfer for the south station? |
| How long does it take to [place]? | 到[地方]要多久？ | Dào [dìfang] yào duōjiǔ? | 到市中心要多久？ (Dào shì zhōngxīn yào duōjiǔ?) = How long to the city center? |
| How much is it to [place]? | 到[地方]多少钱？ | Dào [dìfang] duōshao qián? | 到酒店多少钱？ (Dào jiǔdiàn duōshao qián?) = How much to the hotel? |
| Please stop at [place] | 请在[地方]停一下 | Qǐng zài [dìfang] tíng yíxià | 请在门口停一下 (Qǐng zài ménkǒu tíng yíxià) = Please stop at the entrance |
| What time is the next one to [place]? | 下一班去[地方]的车几点？ | Xià yì bān qù [dìfang] de chē jǐ diǎn? | 下一班去北京的车几点？ (Xià yì bān qù Běijīng de chē jǐ diǎn?) = What time is the next one to Beijing? |
| I go to work by [transport] | 我坐[交通工具]上班 | Wǒ zuò [jiāotōng gōngjù] shàngbān | 我坐地铁上班 (Wǒ zuò dìtiě shàngbān) = I go to work by subway |

- 坐 (zuò, "to sit") is the verb for riding a vehicle with a seat. Use 骑 (qí) for a bicycle and 开 (kāi) when you drive.

# Core Communication Phrases

This chapter gives the essential phrases for everyday interactions. Each section has **pattern phrases** (templates with slots that you fill) or **fixed phrases** (ready-to-use expressions). The main columns are Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). The Notes column gives the Levantine spoken form when it is different.

Two words change in almost every polite phrase when you speak to a woman. The tables do not repeat this note in every row.

| English | Arabic | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| please (to a man) | من فضلك | min faḍlika | Levantine: من فضلك (min faḍlak) |
| please (to a woman) | من فضلك | min faḍliki | Same spelling. Levantine: من فضلك (min faḍlik) |
| excuse me, if you permit (to a man) | لو سمحت | law samaḥta | Levantine: لو سمحت (law samaḥt) |
| excuse me, if you permit (to a woman) | لو سمحت | law samaḥti | Same spelling. Levantine: لو سمحتي (law samaḥtī) |

## Pattern Phrase Templates

These patterns let you say many things. Change the word in the `[brackets]`.

### Requesting Something

| Pattern | Arabic | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| [thing] please | [شيء] من فضلك | [shayʾ] min faḍlika | قهوة من فضلك (qahwa min faḍlika) = Coffee please |
| I want [thing] | أريد [شيء] | urīd [shayʾ] | أريد تذكرة (urīd tadhkira) = I want a ticket |
| I would like to [action] | أود أن [فعل] | awadd an [fiʿl] | أود أن أحجز طاولة (awadd an aḥjiz ṭāwila) = I would like to book a table |
| I need [thing] | أحتاج إلى [شيء] | aḥtāj ilā [shayʾ] | أحتاج إلى مساعدة (aḥtāj ilā musāʿada) = I need help |
| Could you [action]? | هل يمكنك أن [فعل]؟ | hal yumkinuka an [fiʿl]? | هل يمكنك أن تساعدني؟ (hal yumkinuka an tusāʿidanī?) = Could you help me? |
| Possible, [thing]? | ممكن [شيء]؟ | mumkin [shayʾ]? | ممكن الحساب؟ (mumkin al-ḥisāb?) = Could I have the bill? |
| Give me [thing] | أعطني [شيء] | aʿṭinī [shayʾ] | أعطني الملف (aʿṭinī al-milaff) = Give me the file |

### Asking Permission

| Pattern | Arabic | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| May I [action]? | هل يمكنني أن [فعل]؟ | hal yumkinunī an [fiʿl]? | هل يمكنني أن أجلس هنا؟ (hal yumkinunī an ajlis hunā?) = May I sit here? |
| Is [activity] allowed? | هل [نشاط] مسموح؟ | hal [nashāṭ] masmūḥ? | هل التدخين مسموح؟ (hal at-tadkhīn masmūḥ?) = Is smoking allowed? |
| With your permission, [action] | بعد إذنك، [فعل] | baʿda idhnika, [fiʿl] | بعد إذنك، سأفتح النافذة (baʿda idhnika, sa-aftaḥ an-nāfidha) = With your permission, I will open the window |

### Asking Questions

| Pattern | Arabic | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Where is [place]? | أين [مكان]؟ | ayna [makān]? | أين المحطة؟ (ayna al-maḥaṭṭa?) = Where is the station? |
| How do I get to [place]? | كيف أصل إلى [مكان]؟ | kayfa aṣil ilā [makān]? | كيف أصل إلى المطار؟ (kayfa aṣil ilā al-maṭār?) = How do I get to the airport? |
| Do you have [thing]? | هل عندك [شيء]؟ | hal ʿindaka [shayʾ]? | هل عندك قلم؟ (hal ʿindaka qalam?) = Do you have a pen? |
| Is there [thing]? | هل يوجد [شيء]؟ | hal yūjad [shayʾ]? | هل يوجد مصعد؟ (hal yūjad miṣʿad?) = Is there an elevator? |
| What is [thing]? | ما [شيء]؟ | mā [shayʾ]? | ما هذا؟ (mā hādhā?) = What is this? |
| How much is [thing]? | بكم [شيء]؟ | bi-kam [shayʾ]? | بكم هذا الكتاب؟ (bi-kam hādhā al-kitāb?) = How much is this book? |
| When does [thing] [action]? | متى [فعل] [شيء]؟ | matā [fiʿl] [shayʾ]? | متى يبدأ الاجتماع؟ (matā yabdaʾ al-ijtimāʿ?) = When does the meeting start? |
| What does [word] mean? | ما معنى [كلمة]؟ | mā maʿnā [kalima]? | ما معنى "عاجل"؟ (mā maʿnā "ʿājil"?) = What does "urgent" mean? |

## Politeness Tiers

The same request has three levels. Use the **polite** level with every person that you do not know well.

| English | Arabic | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| Give me the menu | أعطني القائمة | aʿṭinī al-qāʾima | Casual. To a woman: أعطيني (aʿṭīnī) |
| The menu, please | القائمة من فضلك | al-qāʾima min faḍlika | Polite. The default |
| Could I have the menu? | ممكن القائمة لو سمحت؟ | mumkin al-qāʾima law samaḥta? | Polite, softer |
| If you would be so kind, I would like the menu | لو تكرمت، أريد القائمة | law takarramta, urīd al-qāʾima | Very polite. To a woman: law takarramti |
| you (casual) | أنت | anta | To a woman: anti. Levantine: إنت (inta), إنتي (inti) |
| you (polite) | حضرتك | ḥaḍratuka | To a woman: ḥaḍratuki. Levantine: حضرتك (ḥaḍirtak) |
| sir | سيدي | sayyidī | Very polite. Service and formal speech |
| madam | سيدتي | sayyidatī | Very polite |

# Shopping

This chapter gives the language for shops, markets, groceries, phone shops, and returns. The main columns are Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). The Notes column gives the Levantine spoken form, which is the normal language of a shop.

When you address a woman, من فضلك is min faḍliki and لو سمحت is law samaḥti. The Notes column gives the other changes.

---

## Pattern Templates

| Pattern | Arabic | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| How much is [thing]? | بكم [شيء]؟ | bi-kam [shayʾ]? | بكم هذا القميص؟ (bi-kam hādhā al-qamīṣ?) = How much is this shirt? |
| Do you have [thing]? | هل عندكم [شيء]؟ | hal ʿindakum [shayʾ]? | هل عندكم شاحن؟ (hal ʿindakum shāḥin?) = Do you have a charger? |
| I am looking for [thing] | أبحث عن [شيء] | abḥath ʿan [shayʾ] | أبحث عن هدية (abḥath ʿan hadiyya) = I am looking for a gift |
| I want [quantity] of [thing] | أريد [كمية] من [شيء] | urīd [kammiyya] min [shayʾ] | أريد كيلو من التفاح (urīd kīlū min at-tuffāḥ) = I want a kilo of apples |
| Do you have this in [colour]? | هل عندكم هذا باللون [لون]؟ | hal ʿindakum hādhā bil-lawn [lawn]? | هل عندكم هذا باللون الأسود؟ (hal ʿindakum hādhā bil-lawn al-aswad?) = Do you have this in black? |
| Do you have a [size] size? | هل عندكم مقاس [مقاس]؟ | hal ʿindakum maqās [maqās]? | هل عندكم مقاس أكبر؟ (hal ʿindakum maqās akbar?) = Do you have a bigger size? |
| Can I [action] this? | هل يمكنني أن [فعل] هذا؟ | hal yumkinunī an [fiʿl] hādhā? | هل يمكنني أن أجرب هذا؟ (hal yumkinunī an ujarrib hādhā?) = Can I try this? |
| This is too [adjective] | هذا [صفة] جدا | hādhā [ṣifa] jiddan | هذا كبير جدا (hādhā kabīr jiddan) = This is too big |

## Prices and Bargaining

In a traditional market, people expect you to bargain. In a shopping centre and a supermarket, the price is fixed.

| English | Arabic | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| How much is this? | بكم هذا؟ | bi-kam hādhā? | Levantine: قديش هاد؟ (ʾaddēsh hād?) |
| What is the price? | ما السعر؟ | mā as-siʿr? | Levantine: قديش السعر؟ (ʾaddēsh is-siʿr?) |
| It is expensive | السعر مرتفع | as-siʿr murtafiʿ | Literally "the price is high". Levantine: غالي (ghālī) |
| It is very expensive | السعر مرتفع جدا | as-siʿr murtafiʿ jiddan | Levantine: كتير غالي (ktīr ghālī) |

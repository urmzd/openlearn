# Pronunciation Guide

Arabic spelling is regular. Each letter has one sound, and each sound has one letter. This chapter explains every sound and the transliteration that this guide uses. The Arabic words in this chapter have vowel marks, so that you can see every sound. Chapter 4 explains the marks.

---

## How This Guide Writes Sounds

### The Transliteration Key

| Symbol | Arabic letter | Sound |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| a, i, u | Short vowel marks | Short vowels, as in "cat", "sit", "put" |
| ā, ī, ū | ا, ي, و | Long vowels. Hold them twice as long |
| ʾ | ء | Hamza: a catch in the throat, as in "uh-oh" |
| ʿ | ع | ʿAyn: a voiced squeeze deep in the throat |
| ḥ | ح | A strong, breathy h from the throat |
| kh | خ | As in Scottish "loch" |
| gh | غ | As the French r in "Paris" |
| q | ق | A k from the far back of the mouth |
| ṣ, ḍ, ṭ, ẓ | ص, ض, ط, ظ | The four emphatic ("heavy") consonants |
| th | ث | As in "think" |
| dh | ذ | As in "this" |
| sh | ش | As in "ship" |
| ē, ō | No letter | Long e and long o. Levantine forms only |

### Rules of the Transliteration

1. **Careful speech, no case endings.** Formal grammar adds short vowels to the end of nouns. People do not pronounce them in normal careful speech. This guide writes كِتَاب as kitāb, not kitābun.
2. **The vowel before an attached pronoun stays.** The pronoun needs it: اِسْمُكَ is ismuka "your name".
3. **The ending ة is -a.** It is -at when another noun or a pronoun follows: غُرْفَة is ghurfa, but غُرْفَة الاجتماعات is ghurfat al-ijtimāʿāt.
4. **A hamza at the start of a word is not written.** أَنَا is anā. A hamza in the middle or at the end is written: سُؤَال is suʾāl.
5. **The article is al- with a hyphen.** Before a sun letter, the transliteration shows the real sound: الشَّمْس is ash-shams.
6. **One-letter words are joined with a hyphen.** بِ is bi-, لِ is li-, سَ is sa-. With the article they become bil- and lil-.
7. **A doubled consonant is written twice.** سُكَّر is sukkar.
8. **Two letters, two sounds.** If s and h are separate sounds, a hyphen divides them: أَسْهَل is as-hal "easier".
9. **Fixed formulas keep their old endings.** السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُم is as-salāmu ʿalaykum, and شُكْرًا is shukran.

The letter **ا** (alif) is the first letter of the alphabet. It is not a consonant of its own. It writes the long vowel ā, or it carries a hamza.

### Sounds That Are the Same as English

These 15 consonants need no special practice: b, t, th, j, d, dh, z, s, sh, f, k, l, m, n, w, y. Three details are important.

- **t** and **d**: the tongue touches the back of the top teeth, not the ridge behind them.
- **l**: it is always light, as in "leaf". It is never the dark l of "full". The only exception is the word الله (allāh).
- **h**: pronounce it at the end of a syllable too: مَهْم is not a word, but قَهْوَة (qahwa) "coffee" has a clear h before the w.

### The Rolled r

Arabic **r** is a short tap of the tongue, as in Spanish "pero". A doubled rr is a full roll: مُدَرِّس (mudarris) "teacher". Do not use the English r.

## The Ten Difficult Sounds

Practise these sounds every day for the first two weeks. Record your voice and compare it with a native recording.

| Letter | Symbol | How to make the sound | Practice word | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|--------|-----------------------|---------------|-----------------|---------|
| ء | ʾ | Close the throat for a moment, then release. It is the stop in the middle of "uh-oh" | سُؤَال | suʾāl | question |
| ع | ʿ | Say "aah" and tighten the throat until the sound is squeezed. The voice does not stop | عَرَبِيّ | ʿarabī | Arabic |
| ح | ḥ | Breathe out as if you clean your glasses, but with the throat tight. There is no voice | مَرْحَبًا | marḥaban | hello |
| خ | kh | Lift the back of the tongue to the soft palate and let the air scrape. No voice | خَيْر | khayr | good |
| غ | gh | The same place as kh, with voice. It is a soft gargle | غَدًا | ghadan | tomorrow |
| ق | q | Make a k, but with the tongue as far back as possible. The sound is deep and sharp | قَهْوَة | qahwa | coffee |
| ص | ṣ | Say s with the tongue flat and low and the back of the tongue raised. The sound is dark | صَبَاح | ṣabāḥ | morning |
| ض | ḍ | Say d with the same tongue shape as ṣ | ضَرِيبَة | ḍarība | tax |
| ط | ṭ | Say t with the same tongue shape. Do not release a puff of air | طَالِب | ṭālib | student |
| ظ | ẓ | Say dh, as in "this", with the same tongue shape | ظُهْر | ẓuhr | noon |

### The Emphatic Consonants

The four emphatic consonants each have a light partner. The emphatic consonant also changes the vowel next to it. Near an emphatic, **a** sounds like the a in "father". Near a light consonant, **a** sounds like the a in "cat".

| Light | Emphatic | Light example | Emphatic example |
|-------|----------|---------------|------------------|
| س (s) | ص (ṣ) | سَيْف (sayf) sword | صَيْف (ṣayf) summer |
| د (d) | ض (ḍ) | دَرْب (darb) path | ضَرْب (ḍarb) hitting |
| ت (t) | ط (ṭ) | تِين (tīn) figs | طِين (ṭīn) clay |
| ذ (dh) | ظ (ẓ) | ذَلَّ (dhalla) he was humbled | ظَلَّ (ẓalla) he remained |

If you cannot make the emphatic sound yet, change the vowel. An Arabic speaker hears the dark vowel and understands the word.

### ʿAyn and Hamza

These two sounds are different letters, and they change the meaning. English speakers often drop both.

| Sound | Word | Transliteration | Meaning |
|-------|------|-----------------|---------|
| ʾ | أَمَل | amal | hope |
| ʿ | عَمَل | ʿamal | work |
| ʾ | سَأَلَ | saʾala | he asked |
| ʿ | سَعَلَ | saʿala | he coughed |

## Vowels

Arabic has three vowels. Each one is short or long. The length changes the meaning.

### Short and Long Vowels

| Type | Mark or letter | Symbol | Sound | Example | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|----------------|--------|-------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| Short a | fatḥa: بَ | a | "cat", or "father" near an emphatic | كَتَبَ | kataba | he wrote |
| Short i | kasra: بِ | i | "sit" | مِن | min | from |
| Short u | ḍamma: بُ | u | "put" | كُتُب | kutub | books |
| Long a | alif: بَا | ā | "man", held long | بَاب | bāb | door |
| Long i | yāʾ: بِي | ī | "see" | كَبِير | kabīr | big |
| Long u | wāw: بُو | ū | "moon" | نُور | nūr | light |

### Diphthongs

| Spelling | Symbol | Sound | Example | Transliteration | Meaning |
|----------|--------|-------|---------|-----------------|---------|
| بَيْ | ay | "eye", or "day" in relaxed speech | بَيْت | bayt | house |
| بَوْ | aw | "now", or "go" in relaxed speech | يَوْم | yawm | day |

Levantine changes these two diphthongs to the long vowels ē and ō: بيت is bēt and يوم is yōm.

### Vowel Length Changes the Meaning

| Short | Long |
|-------|------|
| مَطَر (maṭar) rain | مَطَار (maṭār) airport |
| عَلَم (ʿalam) flag | عَالَم (ʿālam) world |
| جَمَل (jamal) camel | جَمَال (jamāl) beauty |
| كَتَبَ (kataba) he wrote | كَاتَبَ (kātaba) he wrote to somebody |

## Doubling (Shadda)

The mark **shadda** (ّ) shows a doubled consonant. Hold the consonant for two beats, as the "n" in English "unknown". Doubling changes the meaning.

| Single | Double |
|--------|--------|
| دَرَسَ (darasa) he studied | دَرَّسَ (darrasa) he taught |
| حَمَام (ḥamām) pigeons | حَمَّام (ḥammām) bathroom |

### Common Words With a Doubled Consonant

| Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| سُكَّر | sukkar | sugar |
| سَيَّارَة | sayyāra | car |
| مَحَطَّة | maḥaṭṭa | station |
| مُدَرِّس | mudarris | teacher |
| كُلّ | kull | all, every |
| مُمْتَاز | mumtāz | excellent. Two different m letters, no shadda |

## The Article and Sun Letters

The article ال is written the same way before every noun. Before a **sun letter** you do not pronounce the l. You double the next consonant.

| Type | Arabic | Transliteration | Meaning |
|------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| Moon letter | الْقَمَر | al-qamar | the moon |
| Moon letter | الْبَيْت | al-bayt | the house |
| Moon letter | الْمَكْتَب | al-maktab | the office |
| Sun letter | الشَّمْس | ash-shams | the sun |
| Sun letter | السَّلَام | as-salām | the peace |
| Sun letter | الطَّرِيق | aṭ-ṭarīq | the road |
| Sun letter | النُّور | an-nūr | the light |
| Sun letter | الرَّجُل | ar-rajul | the man |

The 14 sun letters are ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن. All other letters are moon letters.

### Linking

In fluent speech, the a of al- disappears after a vowel. The two words join.

| Written in this guide | Fluent speech | Meaning |
|-----------------------|---------------|---------|
| fī al-bayt | fil-bayt | in the house |
| ilā al-maṭār | ilal-maṭār | to the airport |
| wa al-ān | wal-ān | and now |
| mā ismuka? | masmuka? | What is your name? |

This guide writes the full form, so that you can identify each word. Say the full form first. The linked form comes with speed.

## Stress

Arabic stress is regular. Count the syllables from the end of the word.

- A **heavy** syllable has a long vowel, or it ends in a consonant.
- A **super-heavy** syllable has a long vowel plus a consonant, or it ends in two consonants.

| Rule | Example | Stress | Meaning |
|------|---------|--------|---------|
| 1. The last syllable is super-heavy: stress it | كِتَاب (kitāb) | ki-TĀB | book |
| 1. The last syllable is super-heavy: stress it | جَدِيد (jadīd) | ja-DĪD | new |
| 1. The last syllable is super-heavy: stress it | اِجْتِمَاع (ijtimāʿ) | ij-ti-MĀʿ | meeting |
| 2. If not, the syllable before the last is heavy: stress it | مُدَرِّس (mudarris) | mu-DAR-ris | teacher |
| 2. If not, the syllable before the last is heavy: stress it | سَيَّارَة (sayyāra) | say-YĀ-ra | car |
| 2. If not, the syllable before the last is heavy: stress it | مُسْتَشْفَى (mustashfā) | mus-TASH-fā | hospital |
| 3. If not, stress the third syllable from the end | مَدْرَسَة (madrasa) | MAD-ra-sa | school |
| 3. If not, stress the third syllable from the end | شَرِكَة (sharika) | SHA-ri-ka | company |
| 3. If not, stress the third syllable from the end | مُشْكِلَة (mushkila) | MUSH-ki-la | problem |
| A word with two syllables: stress the first, unless rule 1 applies | مَكْتَب (maktab) | MAK-tab | office |

The article al- never takes the stress. Stress rules are different in some dialects, but these rules are understood everywhere.

## Levantine Sounds

Levantine uses the same letters, but some sounds change. The Levantine transliterations in this guide show the spoken sound, not the spelling.

| Letter | MSA sound | Levantine sound | Example |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|---------|
| ق | q | ʾ in the cities. A g in some rural areas | قهوة: qahwa becomes ʾahwe |
| ث | th | t in everyday words, s in formal words | ثلاثة: thalātha becomes tlāte |
| ذ | dh | d in everyday words, z in formal words | هذا: hādhā becomes hād |
| ظ | ẓ | A heavy z, or ḍ | ظهر: ẓuhr becomes ḍuhr |
| ج | j | j in Jordan and Palestine. The s of "measure" in Syria and Lebanon | جديد: jadīd becomes jdīd |
| ة | -a | -e after most consonants. It stays -a after throat and emphatic consonants | مدرسة: madrasa becomes madrase |
| The diphthong ay | ay | ē | بيت: bayt becomes bēt |
| The diphthong aw | aw | ō | يوم: yawm becomes yōm |
| Short vowels in the first syllable | Pronounced | Often dropped | كبير: kabīr becomes kbīr |
| The article | al- | il- | البيت: al-bayt becomes il-bēt |

Some formal words keep the q in Levantine too, for example القرآن (al-qurʾān) and ثقافة (thaqāfa) "culture".

## Common Mistakes of English Speakers

| Mistake | Correction |
|---------|------------|
| Short and long vowels sound the same | Count two beats for ā, ī, ū |
| ḥ sounds like h, or like kh | ḥ has no scrape and no voice. It is a strong breath from the throat |
| ʿ is dropped | Squeeze the throat. عَمَل (ʿamal) is not أَمَل (amal) |
| q sounds like k | Move the tongue back. قَلْب (qalb) is not كَلْب (kalb) |
| The English r | Tap the tongue once |
| A doubled consonant is too short | Hold it. حَمَّام (ḥammām) is not حَمَام (ḥamām) |
| The h is silent at the end of a syllable | Pronounce it: قَهْوَة (qahwa), مَهْم is wrong, مُهِمّ (muhimm) "important" |
| The l is pronounced before a sun letter | Say ash-shams, not al-shams |
| An unstressed vowel becomes the weak English vowel of "about" | Give each a, i, u its full sound |

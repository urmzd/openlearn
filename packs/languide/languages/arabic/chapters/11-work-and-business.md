# Work and Business

This chapter gives the language of the workplace: introductions, meetings, email, calls, office talk, and the vocabulary of office and software work. Written work uses Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). Spoken work uses a mix of MSA and dialect. The Notes column gives the Levantine spoken form.

When you address a woman, من فضلك is min faḍliki and لو سمحت is law samaḥti. The Notes column gives the other changes.

---

## The Formal Register: Seven Rules

1. **Write in MSA.** Email, reports, slides and contracts are in MSA. Dialect in a document looks unprofessional.
2. **Greet before business.** Start every email, call and meeting with a greeting and a good wish. A message that starts with the request is rude.
3. **Use a title and the first name.** Say "Ustādh Samir" or "Duktūra Layla", not the family name alone.
4. **Use the respectful "you".** In speech, use حضرتك (ḥaḍratuka). In writing, use the plural ending كم (-kum) for one person.
5. **Make a request with a noun, not with a command.** Write أرجو إرسال التقرير (arjū irsāl at-taqrīr) "I request the sending of the report".
6. **Say "we" for the company.** Use نحن (naḥnu) and نود (nawadd) "we would like".
7. **Close with thanks and respect.** End with a formula of thanks, then a formula of greeting, then your name.

### One Request in Three Registers

| English | Arabic | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| Send me the report | أرسل لي التقرير | arsil lī at-taqrīr | Casual. A direct command to a close colleague. To a woman: أرسلي (arsilī) |
| Could you send me the report, please? | هل يمكنك إرسال التقرير من فضلك؟ | hal yumkinuka irsāl at-taqrīr min faḍlika? | Polite. The default in speech |
| I request that you send the report | أرجو إرسال التقرير | arjū irsāl at-taqrīr | Very polite. The default in email |
| Kindly send the report | يرجى إرسال التقرير | yurjā irsāl at-taqrīr | Formal and impersonal. Notices and official letters |

## Pattern Templates

| Pattern | Arabic | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| I work as [job] at [company] | أعمل [وظيفة] في [شركة] | aʿmal [waẓīfa] fī [sharika] | أعمل مهندسا في شركة برمجيات (aʿmal muhandisan fī sharikat barmajiyyāt) = I work as an engineer at a software company |

| Is [day] suitable for you? | هل يناسبك يوم [يوم]؟ | hal yunāsibuka yawm [yawm]? | هل يناسبك يوم الثلاثاء؟ (hal yunāsibuka yawm ath-thulāthāʾ?) = Is Tuesday suitable for you? |
| I will send [thing] [time] | سأرسل [شيء] [وقت] | sa-ursil [shayʾ] [waqt] | سأرسل التقرير غدا (sa-ursil at-taqrīr ghadan) = I will send the report tomorrow |
| Could you send [thing]? | هل يمكنك إرسال [شيء]؟ | hal yumkinuka irsāl [shayʾ]? | هل يمكنك إرسال الفاتورة؟ (hal yumkinuka irsāl al-fātūra?) = Could you send the invoice? |

| The deadline is [date] | الموعد النهائي هو [تاريخ] | al-mawʿid an-nihāʾī huwa [tārīkh] | الموعد النهائي هو يوم الخميس (al-mawʿid an-nihāʾī huwa yawm al-khamīs) = The deadline is Thursday |
| I think that [opinion] | أعتقد أن [رأي] | aʿtaqid anna [raʾy] | أعتقد أن الخطة جيدة (aʿtaqid anna al-khuṭṭa jayyida) = I think that the plan is good |
| I suggest that we [action] | أقترح أن [فعل] | aqtariḥ an [fiʿl] | أقترح أن نبدأ غدا (aqtariḥ an nabdaʾ ghadan) = I suggest that we start tomorrow |
| Regarding [topic] | بخصوص [موضوع] | bi-khuṣūṣ [mawḍūʿ] | بخصوص العقد (bi-khuṣūṣ al-ʿaqd) = Regarding the contract |
| Who is responsible for [thing]? | من المسؤول عن [شيء]؟ | man al-masʾūl ʿan [shayʾ]? | من المسؤول عن الاختبار؟ (man al-masʾūl ʿan al-ikhtibār?) = Who is responsible for the testing? |
| We need to [action] | نحتاج أن [فعل] | naḥtāj an [fiʿl] | نحتاج أن نراجع الميزانية (naḥtāj an nurājiʿ al-mīzāniyya) = We need to review the budget |

### Disagreeing Politely

Do not say "no" or "you are wrong" in a meeting. Use one of these forms.

| English | Arabic | Transliteration | Notes |
|---------|--------|-----------------|-------|
| I have another opinion | عندي رأي آخر | ʿindī raʾy ākhar | Levantine: عندي رأي تاني (ʿindī raʾy tānī) |
| I have a remark | عندي ملاحظة | ʿindī mulāḥaẓa | A soft way to start an objection |
| I do not fully agree | لا أتفق تماما | lā attafiq tamāman | Polite and clear |
| With my respect, I see the matter differently | مع احترامي، أرى الأمر بشكل مختلف | maʿa iḥtirāmī, arā al-amr bi-shakl mukhtalif | Very polite |

## Email and Chat

### Right-to-Left Email Conventions

- **Alignment.** The whole message is aligned to the right. Set the paragraph direction to right to left before you type.
- **Subject line.** Start with the topic. The label is الموضوع (al-mawḍūʿ) "subject".
- **Salutation.** Write the title, the name, then المحترم (al-muḥtaram) "the respected". End the line with the Arabic comma ،.
- **Opening formula.** The line تحية طيبة وبعد (taḥiyya ṭayyiba wa baʿd) follows the salutation. It means "a good greeting, and now to the subject".
- **A good wish.** Add one sentence that hopes the reader is well. Then give the purpose.
- **Respectful plural.** Address one reader with كم (-kum): رسالتكم (risālatukum) "your message".
- **Numbers and dates.** Write the date as day, month, year. Both sets of numerals are acceptable. Use one set in the whole message.
- **English terms.** A product name or a file name in Latin letters can move to an unexpected place in the line. Read the line again before you send it.
- **Closing.** Give thanks, then a formula of respect, then your name, your title, and your telephone number.

## Titles and the Ritual Before Business

Chapter 10 explains titles and the greeting ritual. At work, follow these steps.

1. Greet the group: السلام عليكم (as-salāmu ʿalaykum).
2. Greet each person with the title and the first name.
3. Ask about health and family. Answer with الحمد لله (al-ḥamdu lillāh).
4. Accept the coffee or the tea.
5. Wait for the senior person to start the subject.

| Doctor Layla | الدكتورة ليلى | ad-duktūra laylā | For a doctorate or a physician |

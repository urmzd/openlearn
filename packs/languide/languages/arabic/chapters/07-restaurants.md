# Restaurants and Dining

This chapter gives the language for restaurants, cafés, a work lunch, and a meal at a colleague's home. The main columns are Modern Standard Arabic (MSA). The Notes column gives the Levantine spoken form, which is what most waiters speak.

When you address a woman, من فضلك is min faḍliki and لو سمحت is law samaḥti. The Notes column gives the other changes.

---

## Pattern Templates

| Pattern | Arabic | Transliteration | Example |
|---------|--------|-----------------|---------|
| A table for [number], please | طاولة ل[عدد] من فضلك | ṭāwila li-[ʿadad] min faḍlika | طاولة لشخصين من فضلك (ṭāwila li-shakhṣayn min faḍlika) = A table for two people, please |
| I would like [dish] | أريد [طبق] | urīd [ṭabaq] | أريد دجاجا مشويا (urīd dajājan mashwiyyan) = I would like grilled chicken |
| I will have [dish] | سآخذ [طبق] | sa-ākhudh [ṭabaq] | سآخذ السمك (sa-ākhudh as-samak) = I will have the fish |
| Without [ingredient], please | بدون [مكون] من فضلك | bi-dūn [mukawwin] min faḍlika | بدون بصل من فضلك (bi-dūn baṣal min faḍlika) = Without onion, please |
| Does this contain [ingredient]? | هل يحتوي هذا على [مكون]؟ | hal yaḥtawī hādhā ʿalā [mukawwin]? | هل يحتوي هذا على مكسرات؟ (hal yaḥtawī hādhā ʿalā mukassarāt?) = Does this contain nuts? |
| I don't eat [food] | لا آكل [طعام] | lā ākul [ṭaʿām] | لا آكل لحم الخنزير (lā ākul laḥm al-khinzīr) = I don't eat pork |
| More [thing], please | المزيد من [شيء] من فضلك | al-mazīd min [shayʾ] min faḍlika | المزيد من الخبز من فضلك (al-mazīd min al-khubz min faḍlika) = More bread, please |
| What is [dish]? | ما هو [طبق]؟ | mā huwa [ṭabaq]? | ما هو المنسف؟ (mā huwa al-mansaf?) = What is mansaf? |

# What the System Does After Pass 11

After Pass 11 the system is released as v1.0.0 and operated. Its storage and API formats migrate without downtime, dependencies upgrade safely, a performance regression is bisected, the core incidents are rehearsed with runbooks and postmortems, its security is reviewed, and the whole design is defended in a review and in a mock customer engagement. Everything from earlier passes keeps working.

## Core incident drills are detected, resolved, and documented

Each drill injects one failure into the running system: [a decode pod killed under load](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/01-kill-decode.md), [the durable server killed with `kill -9`](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/02-durable-kill9.md), [a poison task](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/03-poison-task.md), the [KV v1 to v2](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/04-kv-v2-migration.md) and [API v1 to v2](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/05-api-v2-migration.md) migrations, [a dependency upgrade](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/06-dependency-upgrade.md), [a performance regression](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/07-performance-regression.md), [a data incident](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/08-data-incident.md), [a noisy neighbour](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/09-noisy-neighbor.md), [a runaway agent](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/10-runaway-agent.md), [a full event-log disk](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/11-eventlog-disk-full.md), and [a Raft partition](../../../systems/05-incident-response-and-chaos/12-raft-partition.md). For each, the evidence is a runbook for the alert that fires and, where an incident happened, a blameless postmortem. The solution's runbooks are in [`docs/runbooks`](../../../../../course/solutions/docs/runbooks/RaftPartition.md); the Raft partition runbook explains the linearizability guarantee the operator must preserve. Its postmortems are in [`docs/postmortems`](../../../../../course/solutions/docs/postmortems/2026-10-09-kill-decode.md).

Why this matters: a system is only operable if the people on call have rehearsed its failures. A runbook written before the incident, and a postmortem written after it, are what turn one person's debugging into the team's knowledge.

## v1.0.0 is released, migrated, reviewed, and handed off

The release follows the procedure in [`releases/v1.0.0.md`](../../../../../course/solutions/docs/releases/v1.0.0.md): a complete changelog entry, a clean tree with green CI, an annotated `v1.0.0` tag, and publication only after the tag workflow passes, with a written rollback.

Around the release sits the work that makes it trustworthy. Documentation comes first, with [C4 diagrams in D2](../../../diagramming/01-diagramming-c4/01-c4-diagrams-in-d2.md) and [runbooks and Diataxis docs](../../../diagramming/02-documentation-writing/02-runbooks-and-diataxis.md). Versioning follows [commits, semver, and releases](../../../software-craftsmanship/06-releases-and-deprecation/01-commits-semver-and-releases.md) and a [deprecation policy](../../../software-craftsmanship/06-releases-and-deprecation/02-deprecation-policy.md). The [KV format](../../../software-craftsmanship/07-maintenance/01-kv-format-v1-to-v2.md) and [API](../../../software-craftsmanship/07-maintenance/02-api-v1-to-v2.md) migrations are built as modules, alongside [dependency upgrades](../../../software-craftsmanship/07-maintenance/03-dependency-upgrades.md) and a [bisected performance regression](../../../software-craftsmanship/07-maintenance/04-performance-regression-bisect.md). Security is covered by a [STRIDE threat model](../../../software-craftsmanship/08-security/01-threat-model-stride.md), an [SBOM and supply-chain review](../../../software-craftsmanship/08-security/02-sbom-and-supply-chain.md), and a [secrets and authorization review](../../../software-craftsmanship/08-security/03-secrets-and-authorization-review.md), recorded in [`THREAT_MODEL.md`](../../../../../course/solutions/docs/THREAT_MODEL.md). The design is reviewed twice, in [the control-plane review](../../../systems/01-system-design/02-design-review-control-plane.md) and [the whole-system review](../../../systems/01-system-design/03-design-review-whole-system.md). [Professional responsibility and disclosure](../../../responsible-ai/06-professional-responsibility/01-professional-responsibility.md) covers the engineer's obligations. A mock customer engagement runs from [discovery](../../../field-engineering/08-mock-engagement/01-discovery.md) to [handoff](../../../field-engineering/08-mock-engagement/07-escalation-and-handoff.md). And the pass ends with [Defend your system](../../../interview-preparation/defend-your-system/01-defend-your-system.md).

Why this matters: v1.0.0 is a promise to users that the API and formats will change only through announced migrations. The migrations, the deprecation policy, and the security and design reviews are what make that promise keepable, and the engagement and the defense show that the system can be explained to the people who will rely on it. [Pass 12](../course-p12-multimodal/README.md) extends the system beyond text.

For a release, write the model card first to expose missing evidence. For an API, decide usage policy and logging rules before onboarding callers. Use the corpus pipeline in Data Engineering, gateway in AI Engineering, and incident reviews in Systems Engineering as concrete cases. State limitations and justify decisions with recorded evidence.

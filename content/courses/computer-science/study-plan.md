# Suggested Study Plan

This plan suggests a route through mathematics, programming, AI, systems, and applied technology over roughly a year of steady study. Readers from any background can take only the subjects relevant to their interests. The week ranges are pacing suggestions at about ten hours a week, not deadlines or promises of expertise; scale them up or down as your time allows. Use the [learning guide](README.md) and the [prerequisite map](prerequisites.md) to find your starting point, and if you already have a mathematics background, skip ahead to algorithms or machine learning.

## The mathematics, in parallel

The first sixteen weeks build the mathematics, two subjects at a time, because the topics are designed to be studied side by side. Spend the first four weeks on [Calculus 1](../mathematics/07-calculus-1/README.md) and [Linear Algebra](../mathematics/09-linear-algebra/README.md), the next four on [Calculus 2](../mathematics/08-calculus-2/README.md) and [Discrete Math 1](../mathematics/11-discrete-math-1/README.md), and weeks nine to twelve on [Calculus 3](../mathematics/10-calculus-3/README.md) and [Discrete Math 2](../mathematics/12-discrete-math-2/README.md). Keep week thirteen free for review: revisit the hardest topics and catch up on anything that slipped. Weeks fourteen to sixteen complete the sequence with [Probability and Statistics](../mathematics/13-probability-statistics/README.md).

## Algorithms and contests

Weeks seventeen to nineteen work through [Programming and Algorithms](../programming-and-algorithms/README.md), following its seventeen-day route at roughly one week per calendar week. Weeks twenty and twenty-one take the advanced techniques and contest practice of [Programming and Algorithms](../programming-and-algorithms/README.md).

## Machine learning

Weeks twenty-two to twenty-six move from [Statistical learning](../ai-engineering/01-statistical-learning/README.md), read with ISLR, into [Deep learning](../ai-engineering/02-deep-learning/README.md), read with Goodfellow. Weeks twenty-seven to thirty continue deep learning to research-level depth, alongside [Information Theory](../mathematics/17-information-theory/README.md). Weeks thirty-one to thirty-four cover [Reinforcement learning](../ai-engineering/04-reinforcement-learning/README.md) with Sutton and Barto and Spinning Up.

## Systems and the platform

Weeks thirty-five to thirty-nine read [Systems Engineering](../systems/README.md), from [System design](../systems/01-system-design/README.md) through [Observability](../systems/11-observability/README.md). Weeks forty to forty-six take [AI Engineering](../ai-engineering/README.md) patterns first: [training and frameworks](../ai-engineering/16-training-frameworks/README.md), [RPC and protocols](../systems/03-rpc-and-protocols/README.md), [streaming](../systems/04-streaming/README.md), [distributed data and caching](../systems/06-distributed-data-and-caching/README.md), [durable orchestration](../systems/07-durable-orchestration/README.md), [design patterns](../software-engineering/04-design-patterns/README.md), [retrieval and RAG](../ai-engineering/27-retrieval-and-rag/README.md), [authorization](../software-engineering/10-authorization/README.md), and [evaluation](../ai-engineering/29-evaluation/README.md).

## Applying it

Weeks forty-seven and forty-eight are the applied pass over the theory: the worked builds of [Case Studies](../../references/worked-projects.md). Leave [Interview Preparation](../interview-preparation/README.md) for last, in weeks forty-nine to fifty-one, with company-targeted study and mock interviews; the guides are most effective after you have built deep understanding.

Whatever pace you choose, do not skip the cross-references between books. The connections, such as information theory showing up in tokenizer evaluation or durable workflows showing up in training runs, are where real understanding lives. Readers who want to go deeper into research after the machine-learning chapters should continue with the [research reading plan](../research/study-plan.md).

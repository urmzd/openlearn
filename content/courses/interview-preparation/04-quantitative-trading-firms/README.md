# Quantitative Trading Firms

Trading firms test speed and rigor more than breadth: probability and expected value under time pressure, hard algorithm rounds, and, for engineering roles, latency measured in microseconds or nanoseconds. Each section is one firm.

## Before you begin

Read [Shared concepts](../01-shared-concepts/README.md) first. Every system design round in these loops returns to its material on distributed systems, full-stack AI, and ML infrastructure.

## In this chapter

[Jane Street](01-jane-street/README.md) covers OCaml, probability, low latency, and market making. [Two Sigma](02-two-sigma/README.md) covers DP-heavy coding, data infrastructure, and quantitative reasoning. [Citadel and Citadel Securities](03-citadel/README.md) covers some of the hardest algorithm rounds anywhere, C++, and low-latency matching engines. [Hudson River Trading](04-hrt/README.md) covers nanosecond latency, C++, FPGAs, and kernel bypass. And [Renaissance Technologies](05-renaissance-technologies/README.md) covers the most exclusive firm of all: invitation-only, with a PhD-level bar.

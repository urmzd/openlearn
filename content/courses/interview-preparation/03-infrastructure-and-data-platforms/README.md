# Infrastructure and Data Platforms

These companies sell or run infrastructure that other businesses depend on: streaming, cloud services, data platforms, payments, and data integration. Their loops lean on practical coding, correctness under failure, and the design of services that stay up. Each section is one company.

## Before you begin

Read [Shared concepts](../01-shared-concepts/README.md) first. Every system design round in these loops returns to its material on distributed systems, full-stack AI, and ML infrastructure.

## In this chapter

[Netflix](01-netflix/README.md) covers distributed systems, streaming, recommendations, and its distinctive culture. [Amazon](02-amazon/README.md) covers the Leadership Principles that are tested in every round, services architecture, and AWS ML. [Databricks](03-databricks/README.md) covers the most selective loop outside the largest platforms, with runnable-code rounds and Lakehouse, Spark, and Delta Lake design. [Stripe](04-stripe/README.md) covers its no-LeetCode loop: practical coding, the bug bash, payment infrastructure, and financial correctness. And [Palantir](05-palantir/README.md) covers problem decomposition, data platforms, entity modeling, and AIP.

# Big Tech and AI Labs

The large platforms and the frontier AI labs run the loops most readers aim for first. They share a bar for system design at scale and, more and more, expect fluency in how models are trained and served. Each section is one company: how its process runs, what each round tests, and how to prepare for it.

## Before you begin

Read [Shared concepts](../01-shared-concepts/README.md) first. Every system design round in these loops returns to its material on distributed systems, full-stack AI, and ML infrastructure.

## In this chapter

[Anthropic](01-anthropic/README.md) is the most detailed guide in the book, with one page per round of the infrastructure loop and three concept pages on concurrency, GPU inference serving, and streaming over Server-Sent Events. [Google](02-google/README.md) covers data structures and algorithms at scale, system design, Googleyness, and TPU and ML topics. [Google DeepMind](03-deepmind/README.md) covers its research-grade engineering bar, JAX, and the systems behind AlphaFold and Gemini. [OpenAI](04-openai/README.md) covers API infrastructure, ML systems, and mission alignment. [Meta](05-meta/README.md) covers planetary-scale systems, its AI-enabled coding round, and LLaMA. [Apple](06-apple/README.md) covers privacy-first ML, on-device inference, and product excellence. [NVIDIA](07-nvidia/README.md) covers GPU and CUDA programming, parallel computing, and AI infrastructure. And [Moonshot AI](08-moonshot/README.md) covers long-context LLMs and NLP research at the Chinese AI frontier.
